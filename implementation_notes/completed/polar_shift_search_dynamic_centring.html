    Dynamic centring: the disk per particle, across the stages¶

    Proposal, 2026-11-02 (Claude, for Hans's ruling before implementation). Companion to polar_shift_search.md, whose rulings 15-18 and Phase 4 this builds on. Completed with the plan on 2026-11-03. Ruled and built the same day (ruling 19; the as-built record is the note's progress entry of 2026-11-02): the two metrics of section 2.6 with the rule of section 2.1, the box's mask per disk particle (B), the subspace pool in the state mode and the geometric neighbourhood in the geom mode, trs_window and the drain exits retired, the 2D particle disk cache removed on the way.

    1. The oversight¶

    The centring is a stage: solve2D's stage 2 and solve3D's stage 3 search the disk of 3 sigma for every sampled particle, drain the particles into the next stage's window, and end when few still need the disk (rulings 16, S1, S3). Every stage after searches the window from the coarse window map (Phase 4). The design assumed that the particles the centring stage sees are the particles the workflow has, and the beta-gal bench, which samples all 4463 particles every iteration (NSAMPLE_DEFAULT_2D = 200000, nsample above the active count), never contradicted it.

    The workflows sample. solve3D draws update_frac of the particles per iteration from the lowest updatecnt tiers (sample4update_cnt, class or class-average balanced under balance), fills in every fifth iteration (sample_ptcls4fillin), and its default nsample is 10000 against UPDATE_FRAC_MAX = 0.9 and the full-sampling switch at 0.9 of the active count. solve2D holds a sticky random subset through stage 1 and resamples by updatecnt from stage 2 (sample_ptcls4update2D). The stream's pool receives new particles every iteration and clears their updatecnt and sampled on injection (simple_stream_pool2D, decision 5). So:

    • a particle first sampled after the centring stage ended never sees the disk. With a centring stage of k iterations at update fraction f, the share of the particles it never sampled is about (1 - f)^k: at f = 0.3 and k = 4, 24 %; at f = 0.1, 66 %. Those particles carry their picking error into the window stages, which cannot recover a 20-30 A offset (the finding that motivated the work);
    • a particle whose later move exceeds its window is never centred again: the drain's second rule (shincr above the window) is read only in the centring stage;
    • the stream has no centring stage at all: the pool's refine2D runs the window from the first iteration, under the default key, with the window map.

    The centring belongs to the particle, not to the stage.

    2. The proposal¶

    Replace the centring stage by a per-particle state that every table fill reads and writes, in 2D and 3D, in the dense, sparse and neighbourhood tables alike.

    2.1 The state (ruled 2026-11-02: both metrics, section 2.6)¶

    Each searched particle carries two numbers, written by the consumer (the 2D and 3D probabilistic strategies) after the polish:

    • shincr, the last move: the magnitude of the polished increment, in pixels;
    • shsig, the particle's prior width for its next search, in pixels: the posterior width of section 2.6, inflated to the radius searched when the move reached that radius's bound. Below zero is the sentinel, no history in this workflow.

    A sampled particle's next box is the smallest rung of the ladder of radii (section 2.6) that covers three of its prior widths, floored at the stage's trs and capped at the disk's 3 sigma; the sentinel takes the disk. With the two rungs the fills have today, the window and the disk, the rule reads: the disk when shsig is the sentinel or exceeds a third of the stage's trs, the window otherwise. The fill writes nothing.

    The sentinel is set once per workflow, at the start of solve2D and solve3D (the commanders, in place of the refine2D/refine3D masters' per-run reset: ruling 1, per workflow; the 2D shifts stay the 3D's start, the 3D disk is searched about them under the pick prior), and on every injection: import_particles for a new project, the stream's pool on the chunk's arrival (beside its clean_entry('updatecnt', 'sampled')); the fill-in and missing-update passes need nothing since their particles carry the state from the workflow's start. A standalone refine2D or refine3D sets it at startit = 1 only.

    The alternative sentinel, updatecnt == 0 at the fill, needs no new write but fails in the stream (the pool clears updatecnt on every injection, for the new and the old alike) and in a continued refine3D (the counts persist while the centring state should not). The particle's own record is the cleaner carrier.

    2.2 The disk in every fill¶

    The dense fills have both paths since Phase 4 (process_particle_with_map with l_disk). Two changes make the disk independent of the stage:

    • the disk's radius is 3 sigma in the stage's pixels whatever the stage's trs, bounded by the box (today it is min(3 sigma, trs), which is why the controllers raise the centring stage's trs). The polish of a disk particle runs in a second search object per thread with limits of 3 sigma (the pair the consolidation had before Phase 4 and for the same reason: the box polish clamps to the object's limits); the window object keeps trs;
    • the drained particles' window is the stage's trs, so the window map's radius is trs in every stage and the second map geometry of the centring stage (trs_window) goes.

    The sparse 2D fill (prob_neigh, solve2D's stages 3-6 and the stream's pool) scores a bounded, extremal-decayed set of classes at the seed and refines the best of them. A disk particle there is a particle without a trustworthy class: it scores every active class at the origin (one dense row, 90 posterior values at 5.6 us each), screens the pool holding 99 % of that posterior with the disk map, and polishes the best smpl_ncls of the screen; the window particles keep the sparse path. The fill's candidate buffer takes the extra rows unchanged.

    The neighbourhood table (solve3D's stages 6-8, prob_neigh in its modes) scores the neighbourhood of the particle's stored direction. A disk particle there has a direction as stale as its shift: an injected particle's direction is the random start's, a mover's may be a neighbour's. Its disk is the subspace's: the coarse scoring over the nspace_sub representatives at the origin that the state and geom modes already do (find_peak_subspaces, build_pooled_neighborhood), the disk map over the pooled neighbourhood of the best representatives, and the polish of the best of the screen; in the shc and snhc modes (solve3D's stages 1-2, no shifts) nothing changes. This is the one place the proposal widens a search beyond the shift: a particle that needs the disk also gets the subspace's direction search, which is the right thing for an injected particle and a small cost for a mover.

    2.3 What goes¶

    • the centring stage as a stage: CENTRING_STAGE and solve2D_centring_stage in 2D, the stage-3 raise of trs and cfg%trs_window in 3D, the trs_window parameter and its UI entries;
    • the drain as a stage exit: the shift_stable_its blocks of check_conv2D and check_conv3D, DISK_NEED_FRAC, the tester cases test_shift_map_exit and test_shift_map_exit3D; the stages end on their own rules again. The master keeps printing the disk set it is about to search, split into the never centred and the movers, so a run shows its injection rate;
    • the masters' per-run reset of shincr (the commanders set the sentinel once).

    2.4 The mask of a disk particle (ruled 2026-11-02: B)¶

    The centring stage runs under the box's mask because a particle 20-30 A off its pick is cut by the molecular mask at the pick. Ruled: a particle that needs the disk is prepared under the box's mask whatever the stage. The matcher's particle preparation (prepimg4align in simple_matcher_2Dprep and the 3D batch) takes a per-particle mask radius: the box's for a particle whose state says the disk (the sentinel or a width above the window's third), the stage's for the others. The read-side branch is the only place the state is read outside the fills; the fill and the preparation apply the same rule to the same field, so they agree. stage 2 (2D) keeps the box's mask as a stage policy for the mask estimate at its exit (mskdiam=0), which is a mask question, not a centring one.

    2.5 Costs¶

    From the beta-gal runs of 2026-11-02 (4 parts of 1116 particles, 6 threads each, the fills' wall time per part and iteration):

    path 2D (90 classes) 3D (solve3D stage 3)
    the disk (screen + polish) 5.1 ms per particle 14 ms per particle
    the window map 3.6 ms (dense), 0.5 ms (sparse) 11 ms
    the window search without the key 1.2 ms (dense), 0.4 ms (sparse) 8 ms

    The disk is 1.4 times the window map in 2D and 1.3 times in 3D, so moving a particle's disk from the centring stage to the iteration it is first sampled changes the total work little: every particle is centred once, the movers again, and the work follows the sampling instead of the stage. Under fractional sampling the never-centred pool drains geometrically with the sampling, so the first stage with shifts costs a disk iteration spread over its iterations rather than one expensive first iteration. The sparse 2D stages are the one place the disk is far above the stage's own cost (5 ms against 0.5 ms a particle): a stream pool with 10 % new particles per iteration would spend half its fill on them, which is the price of centring them at all.

    2.6 The two metrics and one rule (ruled 2026-11-02)¶

    Ruled: record the last move and the posterior width, and combine them. The combination, and why it is the sensible one:

    The width. The coarse map's draw already evaluates the posterior over every cell of the box searched (the shift cells of the map at every rotation). The posterior width s is the standard deviation of the shift under that posterior at the physical temperature (prob_beta = 1: a measurement of uncertainty, not the exploration-tempered draw), in pixels: the square root of the trace of the covariance of the cell shifts in the particle's axes, from the first and second moments the draw's loop accumulates (the mean needs each cell's shift rotated into the particle's axes; the second moment is rotation-invariant). About the mean, not the mode, so a two-peaked posterior is as wide as it is uncertain. Under the disk the weights carry the pick prior with the data term, as the draw does; under the window the data term alone. Measured on the coarse band, s is an upper bound of the full-band width: conservative, more disks rather than fewer.

    The move. The width is the posterior's within the box that was searched. A polish whose move d reached that box's bound (within half a map step of the radius R searched) says the posterior was truncated there: the mass beyond the edge was never seen, and s understates the uncertainty by an unknown amount. The one honest statement is that the uncertainty is at least the radius searched. So the particle's prior width for its next search is

    sigma_next = s                 if d < R - step/2
sigma_next = max(s, R)         if d >= R - step/2

    and shsig stores sigma_next. The consumer computes it, since it alone knows s, d and R together; the fill reads one number.

    The box. The next search covers three prior widths, the confidence the disk itself uses (3 sigma of the pick prior): the smallest rung of the ladder of radii at or above 3 sigma_next, floored at the stage's trs (no box smaller than the window: a settled particle searches the window as today, and no particle can lock itself into a box its posterior cannot leave) and capped at the disk. With two rungs, the window and the disk, a particle takes the disk when it was never searched, or when its last move reached its box's bound (the rule in force today, which this reproduces exactly), or when its posterior is wider than a third of the window although it did not move; and the window otherwise. A third rung (the geometric mean of the two, say) is one more map geometry when the widths on data show particles between.

    The pick prior at the first centring only (as built, 2026-11-02). The first fixture run re-centred correctly placed particles under the pick prior and lost 56 points: the draw tempers the data term by prob_beta (0.3) and not the prior, so at a re-centring the prior weighed more than three times its physical weight and pulled the particle back towards its pick. A re-centring is now the data's alone; the pick prior regularises a particle's first centring, where the references are poorest and the pick is the only information. The bound test is a tenth of the radius, or half a map step where the map is finer, since a window map of two rings has a step equal to its radius.

    The width as measured, and the Laplace width (2026-11-02, after Hans's beta-gal run). On data the coarse map's width reads 0.00 for nearly every particle from the second iteration on, with 90th percentiles of 0.27 px early and 0.00 later. That is a resolution limit of the measurement: the coarse band ends at shell kscreen, so the map's radial step is box / (4 kscreen), 3.6 px at beta-gal's stage 4 against a window of 4.4 px (the window map has two rings, the origin and the rim), and at the physical temperature the loss differences between cells are tens of nats, so all the mass sits on one cell. A read of 0.00 means "surer than one cell", which is the truth for a well aligned particle, and the rule "three widths beyond the window" needs widths resolved below 1.5 px, which the map cannot give. As it stands the width is a two-state flag, multi-modal at the coarse band or not, and the re-centring is carried by the move at the bound, which drains to a few movers an iteration on data. Two changes within the map do not help: the draw's temperature (0.3 times the physical) inflates the widths with the exploration's flatness, not the particle's uncertainty; a floor at the cell's size makes every particle wide against a window of one cell.

    The informative version, the Laplace width at the polished optimum on the full band (the loss's curvature at the committed pose), with the review's qualifications, is its own planned note since 2026-11-03: ../planned/polar_shift_laplace_width.md. Not built; Hans's call.

    What the width does not do. It never enters the likelihood as a prior. The posterior re-used as a prior on the same particle image would count the data twice; the pick prior stays the only prior in the disk's draw and in the 3D insertion's loss, and the particle's width chooses the domain of the search, not the weight of its cells. This is what keeps the rule mathematically clean: coverage of the posterior at three standard deviations, the truncation correction from the move, and the prior untouched.

    The record. shincr keeps the move for the diagnostics; shsig holds sigma_next (a new slot beside shincr). The master prints, per iteration, the disk set by cause (never searched, bound reached, wide) and the median and 90th percentile of the widths, the retained-mass measurement the review asked for. A class change (2D) or a direction change beyond the neighbourhood (3D) needs no guard of its own: the polish at the new class or direction measures a new width and move, and a wrong-class centring shows as a bound hit or a wide posterior at the next search.

    6. Rulings (Hans, 2026-11-02)¶

    1. The sentinel resets per workflow. The 2D shifts stay the 3D's start.
    2. A particle that needs the disk is prepared under the box's mask, whatever the stage (B).
    3. The re-centring rule is the move that reached the box's bound (the rule in force), now the truncation correction of the width (section 2.6).
    4. The neighbourhood table's disk candidates come from the subspace pool.
    5. trs_window is retired with the stage.
    6. Both metrics are recorded, the last move and the posterior width, combined as section 2.6: the width chooses the next box at three standard deviations, the move at the bound inflates it to the radius searched, the prior in the likelihood stays the pick's.

    Left open for the build: the ladder's rungs (two to start), the bound's tolerance (half a map step), the width's calibration between the coarse and the full band (an upper bound until measured).
