    The 2D student: amortized class assignment for the stream and for solve2D at scale¶

    Status, 2026-11-12: approved by Hans ("yes to all"), carried out interactively phase by phase, a report and his go-ahead after each. Paused 2026-11-13 after Phase 0 for completed/center_ptcls.md, which centres the particles the student needs, and resumed the same day with center_ptcls in the working tree: unseen particles are centred by it before they are labelled (decision 9). Ideas outside this plan's supervised scope are in planned/polar_student_future_directions.md.

    1. Why¶

    Clustering particles in 2D is the stream's main recurring cost, and the cost of solve2D on the data sets of millions of particles the maintainer has to process. Three places pay for it: - the sieve (p05) runs a whole solve2D per coarse and per fine chunk; - the pool (p06) runs a distributed refine2D iteration over a sample of up to 500,000 particles (STREAM_NPTCLS_MAX) against 150 classes, and gives each new particle a random populated class before its first, greedy alignment (draw_new_classes); - solve2D at scale searches every particle against every class at every rotation in its dense stages.

    The 3D student shows what an amortized search costs instead. On bgal (1,500 training particles, 24 threads) it trains in 22 s, predicts at 670 particles per second (1.5 ms each), and its poses alone, with registered shifts, reconstruct a map within the seed spread of the base's (validate_pose_student, 2026-11-11). Its readout already scores every class at every in-plane rotation: a 2D alignment against fixed references is the same computation with class averages for templates. Phase 0 measures where the 2D time goes; no saving is claimed before Phase 4.

    2. Contract¶

    • Supervised and per data set. A 2D student learns to assign particles to one frozen set of class averages, the teacher's, from the 2D probability table's rows against those averages, as the 3D student learns from the 3D table's rows against a frozen map. It discovers no class. Finding the classes stays with solve2D on a subset, or with the pool's own iterations.
    • Off state. Without a 2D model, and without its candidate artifact, the sieve, the pool, refine2D and solve2D behave byte for byte as on the base at a fixed seed.
    • The 3D student is unchanged by the refactor. Phase 1 moves code. The 3D programs' models, weights, rows, prediction tables and project records stay byte-identical at a fixed seed, at one thread and at 24.
    • One copy of everything shared. The network, the features, the training loop, the held-out split, the confidence calibration, the design choice, the model file's common part, the provenance, the part ranges and the benchmark records exist once. The 2D and 3D students differ only in what their classes are, how a candidate set is formed, and how a pose is read out.
    • The library's object rules hold. Every new stateful module is an encapsulated class: private components, new and kill, behaviour as type-bound procedures, a type named after its module. Large components are allocatable. Commanders are thin execute(cline) wrappers of strategy routines run_<x>(cline) and <x>_exec(params, build, ...) (doc/policies/layering_and_project_ownership_policy.md).

    3. Decisions and rulings¶

    1. (2026-11-12, Hans) Supervised only. Embeddings, self-supervision, shift-invariant front ends and generative models are recorded in planned/polar_student_future_directions.md and are not part of this plan.
    2. (2026-11-12, Hans) The stream is the target application; solve2D at scale on data sets of millions of particles is the second.
    3. (2026-11-12, Hans) Mirror the 3D path as closely as possible: a learn program, a predict program, a validation program, a candidate set handed to a sparse search, fixed-teacher yardsticks over seeds.
    4. The shared core is composed, not inherited. A new class polar_student (src/main/learn/simple_polar_student.f90) owns the features (polar_feats), the network (hhcnn), the frozen shell weights, the geometry (box, crop, sampling, mask, band, CTF treatment), the training on soft class targets with shift and rotation augmentation and early stopping, prediction, the readout's template initialisation, the confidence threshold and its record, provenance, the training record, and the model file's common header and weights. pose_student (3D) and the new class_student (2D) each hold one polar_student as an allocatable component and delegate to it. Reason: a Fortran extension in another module cannot reach its parent's private components. A shared parent would have to make its state public or grow accessors for every field, and the record shows parallel routes beside an owner being rewritten wholesale within weeks (how2write_agent_plans.md, section 2).
    5. Candidates are class lists in the core. The core's calibration works on a candidate list of classes per particle and an eligibility mask. In 3D the list is the folded cone's directions, and eligibility adds the cone-radius bound (ruling R1 of the pose student). In 2D the list is the classes holding 99% of the posterior, at most KMAX_CLASS of them, with no geometric bound. The held-out "recovered" test, the threshold and the design choice are then one routine for both.
    6. The network's reflection branch is a construction property. hhcnn%new gains a logical argument for the reflected scores: 3D passes true (mirror pairs), 2D false, because the 2D table searches no mirrors. The arithmetic stays a property of the build and the shape, never of the team.
    7. Classes and templates. A 2D student's classes are the teacher's class averages, all of them, rejected classes included. The readout's templates start from the class averages' polar transforms, as 3D starts them from reprojections (ruling R3 of the pose student). The model records each class's state, so a prediction into a rejected class is a rejection signal.
    8. Teacher rows. eul_prob_tab2D gains the posterior-row read the 3D table has (get_posterior_row): the class posterior marginalised over in-plane rotation and shift at the particle's physical temperature, written with the existing row file (prob_row_file). The 2D learn program fills the dense table against the frozen class averages in one pass, as learn_pose_student fills the 3D table against vol1.
    9. Shifts and centring (2026-11-13, Hans: mirror the pose student, and centre the unseen data with center_ptcls). The student works on centred particles, as the pose student always did.
    10. Training particles are the teacher's, centred by the teacher's solve2D. The student trains on them with the 3D student's narrow augmentation (shift_sigma 2 Å).
    11. Unseen particles are centred by center_ptcls before they are labelled: in validate_class_student, in Phase 4's route at scale, and wherever raw picks meet the student. The stream's pool needs no such step, because its particles arrive centred by the sieve's chunk 2D.
    12. After prediction, the shift is registered against the predicted class average at the predicted rotation, with the 3D route's search object (pftc_shsrch_grad new_fixed, coarse grid and L-BFGS-B, shared memory).
    13. The two frames differ. center_ptcls's centres and the teacher's differ by a common translation and, per view, by each set of class averages' own centring. The registration absorbs both. Phase 3 measures them (section 6).
    14. The candidate artifact. predict_classes writes, per particle, the top class, its rotation, its registered shift, the confidence, the route (seeded or dense) and the candidate list. The particle record carries the first five in its existing slots. The candidate lists go into a side file that is registered in the project's out segment with the particle-layout digest (ptcl_layout_digest), as other per-particle side files are. The sparse 2D table reads it when the artifact is present and its digest matches; otherwise it runs as on the base. No command-line key reaches the consumer.

    Fixed 2D teachers (frozen in Phase 0, never moved; /data2/agent_runs/class_student/teachers/).     The frame offset: center_ptcls's shifts against the teacher's on the training particles, as their common translation and the residual after it.

    Reads, per set and seed (seeds 1, 2, 3): - top-1 agreement with the teacher's class; - the teacher's class in the candidate list, overall and among seeded particles; - the median candidate count, the seeded fraction, the in-plane angle error against the teacher's; - the registered shift's error against the teacher's; - class averages rebuilt from the student's assignments alone (no search), compared with the teacher's by FRC and by cavg_quality's acceptance.

A read is outside when the arm's three-seed mean differs from the reference arm's three-seed mean by more than the larger of the two arms' seed ranges and a floor of 0.01 for fractions (1° for angles). Phase 0 applies the rule to the base against itself by splitting six base seeds into two groups of three, both ways. The rule is satisfiable only if no read is outside. This replaces the leave-one-out self-check that stopped student_crop.

    7. Common rules and stop conditions¶

    • Variants are built and measured outside the tree, with their patches kept; only the kept change enters the diff. A file outside the table gets its table row before the edit.
    • OpenMP: a new or changed parallel region gets a tester that forces a team and compares bit for bit with one thread, repeated at 16 threads by hand; no string temporaries in a region; work arrays sized by the team in use.
    • Timings on an idle machine with the load recorded; a timing taken under load is repeated. The maintainer runs other work on this machine.
    • Thread identity of training (the HHCNN suites' team checks) on both BLAS builds.

    Stop when: - Phase 0's data preconditions fail (a set's checksums differ, a teacher run fails); - the base fails Phase 0's self-check; - the refactor cannot keep the 3D outputs byte-identical; - decision 4's composition cannot express a needed piece without making the core's state public; - the feasibility verdict fails; - the student passes the feasibility verdict on the unseen particles with converged shifts but fails it on the same particles centred by center_ptcls (record both tables; the options are a better centring or the future note's shift-robust front end, and the choice is Hans's); - the consumer needs a key beyond decision 10; - a unit suite of the base fails on the tree and the cause is outside the table.
