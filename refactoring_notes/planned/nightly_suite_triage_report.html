    Nightly suite triage: report of the run of 2026-11-01¶

    The run of the standing plan on the Dell (24 cores, Oracle Linux 8, GCC 15.2, Release -O3 -march=native), base eefc7e1a1, with the CI history of ci_test.yml read through gh. Scratch, logs and tools: /data2/agent_runs/nightly_2026-11-01/ (journal progress.md). Nothing is committed.

    Summary¶

    • The nightly has been red since 2026-10-01: 42 of the 45 ci_test.yml runs since 2026-09-29 failed. In the week of 10-25 to 11-01 three things kept it red: one entry that can no longer pass (multistate_assignment), one floor that sits inside the spread of its metric (species_discovery), and timeouts of multistate_workflows on the 4-core runner. Three nights broke earlier, in the build.
    • None of the CI failures has an evident repair. Each is a decision for the maintainer: production changed under one test, and two floors are not admissible by the floor rule of the test policy. The evidence and a proposal for each are in section 3 and the decisions in section 8.
    • One-thread runs had stopped being reproducible (found by test=identity): since 2026-10-26 the PCG reconstructor and the wave projector took timing-chosen FFTW plans also under a fixed seed. Repaired in production and verified (section 3.5); two stale rules of the identity check repaired with it.
    • The workflow hid most of this. macOS was cancelled whenever Ubuntu failed first, so it has not finished a test step since 10-01; no report was kept; the serial test step took 3 h 20 min to 5 h 10 min of the 6 h limit. ci_test.yml is rewritten (section 4.3): four parallel groups per platform, both platforms always finish, reports uploaded.
    • Two of the build breaks were forgotten files. The fast gate now warns about source files git does not track (section 4.2).
    • The review of the nightly test code found checks that could not fail. The simulated workflows' distributed-run and wrong-truth control rows, and the negative controls of mini_stream, stream_preproc and single_atoms_stats, never reached the exit status. These and about thirty conformance items are fixed (section 4.4). The only production change is the FFT planning repair (section 4.1).

    3. The failures¶

    3.1 multistate_assignment: production changed under the test (class 3)¶

    The entry stops at its first iteration: refine=prob|prob_neigh needs objfun=euclid (simple_eul_prob_tab.f90). 811934ca5 (2026-10-24, posterior assignment on every table) made the tables' constructors require euclid, "the loss the posterior is defined by". The test runs multi-state refine3D (refine=prob and prob_neigh, three blob states from random labels and the truths low-passed to 20 Å) under cc on purpose: its header records that under euclid, from starting maps without a noise model, two true states merged in most runs, while cc resolved every state in all 20 runs of its profile.

    Evidence: the test with objfun=euclid (a scratch build of HEAD, seeds 0-3) fails every run in both modes and the states merge, as its header says: smallest recall 0.00, 0.18, 0.25, 0.21 (floor 0.93), adjusted Rand index 0.26-0.70 (floor 0.89); prob_neigh index 0.65-0.74 (floor 0.80). Production's multi-state route is now refine3D_states (euclid, flex_pca initialisation), which multistate_workflows covers and which passes.

    Proposals: (1, recommended) retarget the test at the route production uses, euclid from a consensus solution with its noise model, floors from a 20-seed profile; (2) keep the scenario as a robustness profile that records the limitation; (3) a cc path in the tables, which undoes part of 811934ca5.

    Ruling (2026-11-02): retired for now. Production never runs multi-state under cc, so the test checked nothing in use. The CTest entry, its registry row, router case, test UI program and source are removed (SIMPLE_CTEST_BUDGET 42 to 41, 18 high-level entries); the policy's card says where the code is in git (eefc7e1a1) for a revival on the euclid route.

    3.2 species_discovery: a floor inside the metric's spread (class 5)¶

    density_gradient: largest error of the measured transfer gave 0.0510 on CI Ubuntu against the floor 0.050, 0.0486 here. The gate came with 8e0ea79a9 (2026-10-27, measured transfer in rounds) and has failed on every CI Ubuntu night since. Its floor is section 12's "within 0.05" of atomic_density_model.md, whose run recorded 0.046 and noted that the measured curve met 0.05 "by a few thousandths". A profile over 8 seed offsets (test=profile): median 0.0454, spread 0.0055, range 0.0295-0.0826, one run in eight above the floor; the uniform particle's error has median 0.024 and maximum 0.0485. The floor is less than one spread above the median, not admissible by the floor rule. A negative control: a transfer not measured at all errs by up to the notch's depth, 0.6.

    Proposal: a floor from a 20-seed profile by the floor rule (about 0.1 would still separate a measured transfer from none), or a measurement that settles better. The floor is not touched. Note also the entry's time on CI: about 90 s before 8e0ea79a9, 220-370 s since.

    Ruling (2026-11-02): change the floor. A 20-seed profile on the final build: the gradient particle's error median 0.0499, s 0.0114, worst 0.0826 (10 of 20 runs above 0.05); the uniform particle's median 0.0269, s 0.0077, worst 0.0497. By the floor rule (a ceiling at least at the worst plus s and the median plus 3s) each case now has its own: 0.10 for the gradient particle (admissible from 0.094) and 0.06 for the uniform one (from 0.057); the origin is written beside the call, and the policy's card says so. Both pass at seed 0 (0.0486, 0.0253) and at the worst seed offset of the profile, 6 (0.0826, 0.0212).

    3.3 lib_cart_align3D: a floor inside the spread on this platform (class 5, local only)¶

    Here one of 112 checks fails: shift_then_joint euclid lp 8 A: fraction inside the basin width meets its floor (F2), 0.944 against 0.95. CI passes it. Deterministic (0.9440 at 1, 4 and 8 threads, with and without OPENBLAS_CORETYPE); not a regression (the same 0.9440 at 4114f9f4a, which CI passed, and the same failure at fd5b5bc8e, where the floors were declared); not the code generation (0.9450 without -march=native). Over seed offsets 0-8 the value is 0.939-0.955, median about 0.945, and 7 of 9 offsets fail a check (F2 in six, an euclid lp 4 A "error falls" check in two). CI's library stack (FFTW 3.3.10, its compiler) lands just above the floor at offset 0.

    Proposal: the band-1 F2 floor of the euclid routes, and the lp 4 A "falls" checks, from a profile on two platforms by the floor rule, or reported rather than gated. The floor is not touched.

    Ruling (2026-11-02): redesign around stable numerics, 10°. The cause was the case itself: the test perturbed every pose by 15°, exactly the continuous refinement's rotation bound (athres_cont, the total rotation allowed from the seed; production's default is 10°), so every true pose sat on the edge of the search region. The particles that missed never moved (their final error equals their 15° start; most were kept seeds), and which ones missed changed with the rounding of the platform. With the perturbation inside the bound (scratch build, perturbation from the environment): 12° gives 0.987-0.991 on six seeds; 10° passes every check on 20 of 20 seeds, the weakest basin fraction median 0.999, worst 0.996, so the floors 0.95 and 0.90 are admissible by the floor rule with a wide margin and stay. ROTATION_ERROR is now 10°, the origin beside it; the F3 Phase 0 values, measured with the 15° case, still pass and are marked so (dropping them stays a proposal). Through CTest here: 112 of 112 checks, 16.9 s.

    3.4 multistate_workflows: too slow for the runner (class 2)¶

    The four routes on 4 pinned cores here, at the registered 8 threads (8 threads were faster than 4 on 4 cores: cavgs 837 s against 1 078 s): states 693 s, cavgs 837 s, consensus 1 638 s, shc 1 698 s, 81 min in all with the fixture cached. On CI, with the fixture built and a slower and variable runner, the median is 5 498 s and slow nights pass the 7 200 s deadline. The time is production solve3D and refine3D_states; the test wastes none of it.

    Proposals were: split the entry by route, shrink shc and consensus (70 % of the time), or a deadline sized for the runner.

    Ruling (2026-11-02): the cost stays; a 4 h deadline (14 400 s, registry and CTest); Linux only in CI. The entry has a CI job of its own (section 4.3).

    3.5 One-thread runs not reproducible: measured FFT plans (class 4, repaired)¶

    Found by test=identity between the baseline and the verification run. The single_workflow_wurtzite outputs differ from autorefine3D_nano's first iteration on, while every 2D output is identical. Two runs of the same build, each pinned to one core at one thread, still differ in 114 of 164 outputs, so threads are not the cause. Since 65a3b6ae6 and 7d8586b88 (2026-10-26) the PCG reconstructor's and the wave projector's padded work image take FFTW_MEASURE plans when the padded box is 200 or more (320 here). FFTW chooses such plans by timing, so the rounding of the transforms changes from run to run, and the low bits grow through the refinement (first visible in the atom-detection threshold search). The polar Fourier code has had the rule for this since before: under a fixed seed (has_global_seed, which every test run and its workers hold) it plans with FFTW_ESTIMATE, because "measured plans defeat exact replay". The two new callers lacked it.

    Repair (production): both callers take measured plans only without a fixed seed. Production runs (no SIMPLE_SEED) keep the measured plans. Verification: two runs of the repaired build, each pinned to one core, are byte-identical in all 164 declared outputs and both pass (docking correlation 0.990); 667-671 s against 654-663 s with measured plans.

    The same check found two stale rules of test=identity (class 1, repaired): projinfo's solve3D_run_id (the manifest's run identifier, a time and process id) failed every solve3D project; and single_workflow_fcc declared three output patterns that its route (trajectory_solve3D) never writes. With both repaired, the simulated workflows (184 and 216 files) and single_workflow_fcc (52) are byte-identical between the baseline and the verification run.

    3.6 The build breaks (class 2)¶

    Two of the four were commits without a file the local build had compiled: the library globs its sources, so an untracked file builds here and is missing from the commit. The fast gate now warns about such files (section 4.2), and ci_push.yml (added by ruling, 2026-11-02) builds every push to master that touches code from a clean checkout and runs the fast gate (Ubuntu, about 8 minutes; a newer push cancels an older build), so a forgotten file shows minutes after the push and GitHub emails the pusher.

    Proposed tests¶

    For the maintainer to choose from, in order of churn times gap. Each uses an existing suite or entry; none needs a new CTest entry. Rulings of 2026-11-02: 6 (without symmetry_test) and 8 written and passing; 1 and 2 turned out to be workflows or library-tier fixtures, not quick tests, and stay proposals; 3, 4 and 5 not now; 7 left to Joe and Ruben with the stream tests.

    1. classify3D_refs, the hottest file's untested route. Guarantee: given the three blob truths of the state fixture low-passed as references and the particles' true poses, the labels recover the planted states. Where: a route suite=classify3D_refs of multistate_assignment, which already builds the fixture and holds the state metrics (match_states, adjusted Rand index, recall, the shuffled-label and duplicated-reference controls). Expected values: the simulation's labels. Negative control: the duplicated references, which cannot separate states. Cost: classify3D_refs is a whole workflow (refine3D with neighbourhood tables, 10-50 iterations over 5 000 directions, ML regularisation, a final reconstruction): minutes, a high-level route (with multistate_assignment retired, a by-hand route of multistate_workflows); floors from a 20-seed profile.
    2. The probability table's posterior and its helpers. Guarantees: deterministic_best is the arg-max of a row; rank_in_row agrees with a sort; the tempered posterior draw over a fixed synthetic row has the softmax frequencies (a chi-square bound from the multinomial standard error at a fixed seed); a synthetic particle at a known shift is found within a pixel on the shift-map path, and the no-shift path returns zero shift. Where: the 3D probability table sub-suite of unit_pftc_align2D3D. Negative control: a flat row draws uniformly. This is the code 811934ca5 changed. Cost: the uncovered paths are procedures contained in fill_tab_range, reached only by filling a table from particles and references with euclid noise spectra: a library-tier fixture of minutes, not a unit test.
    3. solve2D_chunks. Guarantee: every particle ends in exactly one class (counts kept through the chunk merges), and class purity against the known views is above a floor. Where: a step of simulated_workflow_6vxx on its extracted particles, with a chunk size that forces two chunks, or a library sub-suite. Negative control: purity of shuffled labels. Cost: minutes; on the one-thread critical path if put in the workflow.
    4. solve3D_cavgs with conditional restarts (nrestarts_collapse=). Guarantee: a run whose states collapse restarts (the path record shows it) and the result meets the cavgs route's floors. Where: a by-hand route of multistate_workflows (suite=cavgs_restarts); a nightly route would add about 15 min on a 4-core runner. The internal helpers (reseed_weak_states, validate_cavg_ini_ext_states) become unit-testable only when moved out of the commander.
    5. The distributed class expansion. Guarantee: cls_expansion with nparts=2 gives the shared-memory allocation and sub-averages (identical at one thread), every particle merged once. Where: a step of flex_pca_blobs, as simulated_workflow_1jxy runs its distributed solve3D. Cost: minutes.
    6. The volume programs, written (sub-suite volume programs of lib_reconstruction, simple_commanders_volops_tester.f90; symmetry_test left out by ruling). On phantoms of Gaussian blobs placed analytically: symaxis_search aligns a D2 phantom turned by a generic rotation to a map invariant under D2 (self-correlation 0.9946 against the turned input's 0.4066); dock_volpair docks a turned and shifted copy back (correlation 0.9996 against 0.2146); centervol brings a displaced phantom's centre of mass back to 0.69 Å (from 8.78 Å). Floors 0.95 and 0.8 and one voxel; 3 s.
    7. The stream's initial analysis in a nightly chain. Guarantee: the stage selects a non-empty class set whose accepted classes are those of the true states. Where: the movies to 3D chain of lib_stream, whose simulation knows the states. Cost: minutes.
    8. The PCG density support, written as a unit test (masks of unit_image, test_density_support_envelope), since the support is automask3D as build_pcg_density_support calls it (not tight, low-passed at envmsklp), which no test ran: the blob centres lie in the binary core; the support holds at least 0.999 of the phantom's mass (it reaches 10.5 Å, 4.2 sigma, beyond the core, outside which a Gaussian keeps less than 5e-4); solvent beyond 4 sigma + envmsklp + 10.5 Å is outside it; built from the phantom shifted by 60 Å it covers less than half of the unshifted one (the control).
    9. Decision logic out of the orchestration. As was done for the solve3D_cavgs stage rules and the state assignment rules (unit tests of pure functions), the solve3D controller's stage transitions and the refine3D strategy's per-iteration decisions could move into pure routines with unit tests on synthetic convergence records. That is a refactoring, not a test; it is the route to fast-tier coverage of the code that changes most.
