## Cross-cutting gaps in this cluster

1. **Who owns the project file, and how often it is written, for the whole solve3D family.** Facts that exist only in code: solve3D owns it between stages while each refine3D master owns it inside a stage; solve3D_cavgs writes the original once at the end; the add-on rewrites its working project 3 times in full during the final reconstruction and publishes by tmp+rename; chunks and pyramid copy the input into an execution directory and never write the original; reconstruct3D writes eo flags, sigma2 registration, the field, the NU handoff and `out`. `layering_and_project_ownership_policy.md` (draft) states the general rules; each workflow policy needs its own two-line "project reads/writes" block. Deviations missing from that draft's ledger: PCG master field write (`simple_rec3D_pcg_strategy.f90:931`), solve3D `symmetrize` stale write (`simple_solve3D_utils.f90:620-623`), add-on membership switch full writes.
2. **Strategy routines with a `write` switch.** `volassemble_exec` and `filter_pcg_nonuniform_maps` take `write`, `execute_rec3D_pcg_distributed_master` does not. The rule "a routine on the caller's `(params, build)` writes only when asked" has no home; layering policy section 5.3/5.4 is the place.
3. **Per-particle fields as an inter-stage contract.** `res`, `res05`, `cfar` (written by assembly, read by the solve3D controller from the file), `lp`/`lp_est` (NU handoff), `updatecnt`/`sampled`, `eo`, `state`. No document lists which module writes and which reads, nor which routes clear them. The `res05` stale-entry case above is a consequence.
4. **Optional PCG priors** (solvent, atomicity) and `pcg_mskfile` have no policy; the decision log is the only record. They are the only PCG features whose contract touches NU (label field applied to the prior'd pair).
5. **Stage plan as a table** (solve3D and solve3D_cavgs): see solve3D_policy GAP. Without it, a constant edit in the controller has no document to update and the stage statements in importance_sampling_fractional_update_policy.md drift (it already has).

The names live in `simple_commanders_test_class.f90` tables, not the registry; a single "Tests" pointer to `test_environment_policy.md` plus names that are generated from the tables would stop this.
7. **History in policies.** About 200 lines of solve3D_policy, 150 of the NU policy and 200+ of the PCG policy are benchmark logs, decision logs or plan narrative (listed per document). All three already have an `implementation_notes/completed` log to receive them (`pcg_decision_log.md`, `pcg_priors_history.md`); solve3D has none.
