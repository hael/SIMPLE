{% comment %} _cls3D_state_selector.html — state tile list for 3D volume viewers. Viewing and selecting are separate: a click on a tile shows that state (selectState() drives data-state-selector/data-state-panel/data-volume-panel-title and calls the page's applyVolumeSelection()/lastinteraction when present); the checkbox in the tile's corner (data-state-include) puts the state in or out of the selection, and a tile left out is dimmed. Every state starts in. The states left out are kept in sessionStorage per job, page and stage group, so the streaming zoom page's periodic reload keeps them; submitting clears them. Each form's button names the states it sends, is disabled when none is in, and is disabled after one click. Two contexts: - streaming multistate zoom page: flat `jobstats.state_volumes` (reprojtiles). While multistate 3D runs, "create snapshot of states ..." posts the states in to snapshot_stream_solve3D (a 3D snapshot the stage writes); once it has finished, "create final particle set of states ..." posts them to select_stream_solve3D, which launches the same `selection oritype=ptcl3D` job as the batch viewer on the stream's multistate 3D project. - non-streaming per-stage panel (_cls3D_viewer.html): `cls3d_stages` (list of {key, states, volume_outputs, volume_kinds}, one entry per cls3D stage - see _batch_overview_context/_cls3d_stages in batch_views.py), grouped into .cls3dstagegroup tile groups AND .cls3dstagedetail detail panels (in _cls3D_viewer.html), both toggled by the "stage" select via selectCls3DStage(); that select is rendered in _cls3D_viewer.html's header, not here. "save selection of states ..." posts the states in of the active stage group to batch_cls3D_selection, which launches a `selection oritype=ptcl3D states=...` job to keep only those states. {% endcomment %}
