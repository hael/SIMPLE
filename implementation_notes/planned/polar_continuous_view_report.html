    Continuous view refinement on polar sections: review report (Phases 0-2)¶

    Status, 2026-11-19: the run polar_continuous_view (arun, FWM-S116118, unattended) carried out Phases 0-2 of polar_continuous_view.md on base 7e88a551e, under rulings R1-R4. Phase 3 (the PorF arms and the verdicts) is Hans's (R1); section 8 gives him the commands and the rule. The plan stays in planned/. Nothing is committed. The plan's Progress section holds the detail; evidence paths are under /Users/elmlundho/agent_work/arun/polar_continuous_view.

    1. What was learned¶

    • The B-spline sections are as good as the KB bank. On the 4-blob phantom (shells 2-17, the 20 directions of the nspace=20 spiral), the section's relative error is 5.1-8.3 % against the KB bank's 4.9-7.9 %, and the normal derivative's 5.6-7.7 % against the closed-form KB derivative's 9.4-12.7 %. T1 and T2 pass with margin (floors 2× KB, frozen in Phase 0). The gather is the 3-tap quadratic B-spline of cartft_calc, on references pre-weighted to reproduce the KB gather on the grid; a section costs what a KB section costs (153-160 µs against 153-157 µs, 72 × 30 samples, one thread).
    • The five-variable cost is exact. Its gradient matches central differences (worst 1.5e-4 against a tolerance ≥ 3e-3), its Gauss-Newton matrix is the Hessian at zero residual (≤ 2.6e-5 relative), and both checks fail on planted defects (a sign-flipped dV/dn, a flipped Gauss-Newton column).
    • The view is recovered, and LM is far cheaper here. A noiseless particle 3° off the nearest of 1,000 cells, 17.3° in-plane and (1.4, −0.6) px off is recovered to 0.055° (lbfgsb) and 0.060° (lm) in view (floor 0.25°); with the view off it keeps its 3.00°. Sections per solve on that fixture: 65 (lbfgsb) against 4 (lm). On the cont_refine3D_1jxy smoke runs (1,000 particles, 4 iterations, nspace = nspace_sub = 1,000): 53-56 sections per solve for lbfgsb against 15 (iteration 1) and 10 (iterations 2-4) for lm, 53 s against 35 s of wall time, and the same final resolution, FSC 0.143 / 0.5 = 3.17 / 3.60 Å, against 3.47 / 3.98 Å with the view off at the same nspace (30 s). A repeat gave the same counts and resolutions. These are smoke runs on a simulated set, not a verdict.
    • A base defect in the polar sigma2 (not changed; section 7). calc_sigma2_pftc gets the stored-frame shift increment and applies it as the native shift. At a nonzero in-plane angle the residual is that of a wrongly placed shift: 1.17e-2 against the joint cost's 6.26e-3 on the recovery fixture. View-committed particles take the cost's own residual, so the H arms' sigma2 differs from A's and C's by this, not only by the view.

    3. Decisions taken under delegation¶

    All are recorded in Progress, under each phase.

    • Phase 0. T1/T2 on the unmasked phantom (the masked one is not analytic) at shells 2-17 (analytic RMS ≥ 1e-6 of shell 2's), lp 2.7 Å; the KB reference of T2 is the closed-form derivative of the local KB interpolant; uptime.log joins the exclusions.
    • Phase 1.
    • Decision 1's "cubic B-spline" is gather_packed_bspline3_window_grad, the gather section 1 names (3 taps, quadratic).
    • The cost's variables are the joint search's (shift x, shift y, rotation index) plus the tilts of T(w) = exp([wx, wy, 0]x) about the drawn reference's in-plane axes, so that grad(1:3) is T3's.
    • The tilt columns are the exact derivatives of the section in that chart (left Jacobian of the exponential map).
    • The Gauss-Newton matrix is a direct sum like gen_joint_curv_at_angle's, not 15 angular-FFT columns.
    • The new tester runs from the "continuous in-plane" suite: a suite of its own needs the test UI's list (ui/simple_test/simple_test_ui_class.f90), outside the table.
    • Fixture and FD constants are copied into the new testers (no entity public for a tester; a shared fixture module would be outside the table).
    • Phase 2.
    • Under the key, nspace defaults to 1,000 (decision 8's "(1,000)") and the commander sets nspace_sub = nspace.
    • The pose commit: assign_ori and its caller are outside the table, so the matcher calls strategy3D_srch%commit_view right after the search. It overwrites the Euler triplet with euler2m(assigned)·R_refᵀ·T(w)·R_ref and sets proj to the nearest grid direction. The convergence distances stay on the discrete cells (assign_ori's own rule).
    • The view pass reads the B-spline references into a calculator of its own (read_cart_refvols_view), not build%cftc. References there make every batch prepare Cartesian particle slots (build_batch_particles3D, outside the table); the first smoke runs stopped on exactly that.
    • Sigma2: only a committed (improving) view solve takes the residual of its final evaluation; every other particle keeps the bank's, as before. gen_sigma_contrib was split into a loader and one shared body, so the bank path runs the same operations (the no-change case is byte-identical).
    • A non-improving view solve stores the B-spline re-score of the drawn pose as corr (the contract's "B-spline scores only"); an invalid solve keeps the table's.
    • cont_view with trs=0 is refused (it would do nothing but force the grid).
    • The recovery fixture takes the first tilt direction (30° steps) that keeps the drawn cell the truth's nearest direction (the plan's "off the nearest of 1,000 cells"); the 40° first try did not, and the fixture's own precondition caught it.

    4. Deviations from the plan¶

    • Plain refine3D accepts the key on refine=prob_neigh with one state and objfun=euclid. It is the stage program refine3D_local runs in process, and its workers run as refine3D with the key; only the router (exec/simple_exec_refine3D.f90, outside the table) can tell a user from the stage. cont_prior, another refine3D_local key, has the same standing. Every other program, route and setting is refused before workers launch (scratch/logs/refusals.txt: refine=cont, nspace_sub ≠ nspace, a bad value, two states, refine3D_auto), and other programs' parameter construction ignores the key (the master's reconstruction children inherit its command line).
    • The Gauss-Newton matrix by a direct sum (Phase 1, decision 4 above), not the plan's "15 extra angular-FFT columns".
    • Rows of the table not used: simple_eul_prob_tab_neigh.f90 (with nspace_sub = nspace the subspace map is the identity; nothing to change), commanders/test/simple_commanders_test_class.f90 (the suite).
    • The chart's sign. dS/dwx = −k_y dV/dn in the code's chart (section 1 writes +k_y): a sign convention, verified by T4 and by the recovery.

    5. Evidence against the baseline¶

    8. For Hans: the PorF arms and the verdict rule¶

    Run each command twice per arm, on the build of this change, from PorF_refine3D_local_tests (the local4_best command, ~/for_claude/PorF_refine3D_local_tests/local4_best/log_local):

    • C (control): A's command plus nspace=1000 nspace_sub=1000
    • H-lm: A's command plus nspace=1000 nspace_sub=1000 cont_view=lm
    • H-lbfgsb: A's command plus nspace=1000 nspace_sub=1000 cont_view=lbfgsb

    (nspace_sub may be left out under cont_view: it is set equal to nspace, and a different value is refused.)

    What to compare. For each run: the final FSC 0.143 resolution and the FSC at 4.22 Å; from BENCH/REFINE3D_BENCH_PART*.txt at the last iteration, match3D view sections per solve (H only), match3D view evaluations per solve, match3D total thread-s and match3D orientation search; peak RSS; wall time; paired pose agreement with A (porf_pair.py); the maps.

    The verdict rule (section 4 of the plan, pre-registered):

    • s is A's two-run spread, in final FSC 0.143 and in FSC at 4.22 Å.
    • View refinement: an H arm is a lead if its mean FSC 0.143 is within max(s, one shell) of A's and it beats C by more than s. If C is within s of A, the data cannot judge the view refinement: stop and report.
    • Solver: the better FSC 0.143 wins when the two solvers differ by more than s; otherwise the solver with fewer sections per solve wins.
    • With two runs per arm the result is a lead, not a finding.
    • Section 7's sigma2 frame is fixed in the build all four arms use, so it no longer confounds H against A and C.
