    Policy-set audit and gap register, 2026-10-21¶

    Planned work. Source: a read-only audit of every document under doc/policies/ against the code at fd5b5bc8e (working tree, 2026-10-21), by six auditors, one per cluster (refine3D, solve3D/PCG/NU, 2D and particle I/O, stream and sieving, infrastructure, preprocessing). Each concrete claim (names, ownership, defaults, file names, tests, links) was checked with rg and by reading the code; nothing was built or run. The raw reports, with file:line evidence for every finding below, are in policy_set_audit_2026-10-21/. They are the record before the corrections; the register below says what was done.

    The audit followed the restructuring of 2026-10-20 (structure2D_and_project_owner.md). Result: the names and files the policies cite almost all resolve, and the refine3D and solve2D ownership text of that restructuring is accurate. The drift is in the claims around it (defaults, stage tables, who calls what, test names), in absolute statements the code no longer keeps, and in what no policy says. Section 1 lists what was corrected, sections 2 and 3 what needs the maintainer, section 4 the gaps, section 5 the structural debt.

    1. Corrections made (documents only)¶

    Confirmed STALE, DRIFT, OLDNAME and CONTRADICTS findings were corrected in place, fixing the side the code supports: 51 existing documents edited (+1137, -759 lines; git diff --stat -- doc shows each). No code, skill or instruction file was changed. Gap and structure findings, and every decision in section 2, were left alone except where an existing sentence was false. Larger examples: refine3D_policy.md said matching references are never multiplied by an envelope (they are) and that automsk=yes needs an NU filter mode (only automsk=nu does); solve3D_policy.md section 11 described the add-on entering at stage 3 with a full bootstrap (it enters at the base run's last stage after a registration pass, with an incremental final reconstruction); segdiampick_mics_policy.md described a version of the code that was reverted the day after it was committed and is rewritten; class_average_bootstrap_policy.md had not followed the command since its rework of 2026-06-12; the persistent-worker policy said batch scheduling reads only JOB_FINISHED*; false "implemented, not yet compiled or run" status lines were removed. The three "tests are performed by the user" sentences now name registered tests.

    New files: doc/policies/README.md (index of every policy) and layering_and_project_ownership_policy.md (the developer guidance; its section 8 is the ledger of today's deviations).
