    trajectory_cavgs_rejection: the nanoparticle class-average rejection as its own program, scored jointly and trained on the maintainer's selections¶

    An unattended run on the Dell (trajectory_cavgs_rejection), 2026-10-31. It takes the rejection of nanoparticle class averages out of model_cavgs_rejection, gives it the alignment of each class average to a simulated particle as a second measurement beside the lattice features, scores the two together, and sets that score on the maintainer's manual selections. It follows the trajectory_solve3D run of 2026-10-29/30, whose two gates (the lattice gate at an absolute grade and a model gate cut relative to the survivors' median) kept, by the maintainer's eye on 71-17/traj01 and traj02, far too many poor class averages: in situ they removed 6 to 25 percent of the classes made mostly of frames he had rejected.

    1. What the maintainer fixed (2026-10-31)¶

    • The nanoparticle rejection is separated completely from the model_cavgs_rejection workflow: its own program, its own module, its own policy note and tests. model_cavgs_rejection goes back to the cryo-EM contexts (chunk, pool, sieve) and nothing of the nano context stays in it.
    • The alignment of the class averages to the simulated particle (the start model simulate_nanoparticle builds for the estimated diameter; refine3D_nano on the cls3D field, one iteration, a global search) is a measurement of quality in the new program, beside the lattice features.
    • The decision is one joint score of the lattice features and the model correlation, trained on the maintainer's manual selections, with its threshold set by the recall-first rule as before; it replaces the two coupled gates and their constants (LAT_PEAK_MIN, LAT_PEAK_MIN_RAW, K_MODEL, model_gate_states).
    • As before: the evidence is independent of the data being judged (the lattice is intrinsic to the class average, the model is simulated; no map made from the frames enters the score); the constants are set once on labelled data and never moved to let a trajectory through; bugs are fixed in the phase that finds them.
    • Added 2026-10-31, 01:40: the training data are new. The maintainer will select by eye on the class averages of the workflow's own 2D (the raw frames, centred, refine2D_nano), not on his earlier classes, so this run produces those class averages for every trajectory, and at several class populations: the raw averages of about twenty frames are likely too noisy to judge or to measure, and populations at least three times higher are to be tried. The experiments come first and are done by the morning of 2026-11-01; the training on his selections is the next run. The redesign (the program of section 2, with the model correlation in the feature bank) follows the experiments in this run.

    What the tables of the previous run say, and this plan builds on (its result/keep/ on the maintainer's Mac, read in the night of 2026-10-30; the detail is in section 3): on the three labelled trajectories rebuilt on raw frames, the correlation to the simulated particle separates his rejected classes (median 0.15 to 0.22 on every set, 90th percentile at most 0.30) from his selected ones (median 0.47 to 0.61), and the level transfers between start models. The relative cut failed because, where he rejected most of a trajectory, the survivors' median is the junk level. At the 0.98-recall rule nothing removes much on 71-6/traj01 (a few of his selected classes sit at the junk level); at 0.95 a joint logistic score on peak and correlation removes 96, 57 and 88 percent of his rejected classes while keeping 97, 98 and 94 percent of his selected frames, and it is robust where an absolute correlation floor is not (71-17/traj06, the largest particle, has a lower correlation level throughout). By eye, the lattice peak alone and the correlation alone are each fooled on some classes (a granular blob with a strong Fourier peak; a fringed class the sphere does not match), which is why the score is joint.

    Open decisions (the maintainer rules before launch; the plan's proposals are in force until he does)¶

    1. The program's name: trajectory_cavgs_rejection (a single_exec program of the trajectory_* category, after the program it replaces).
    2. The lattice bank (simple_cavg_quality_lattice.f90, with its tester) moves to the SINGLE domain as src/main/nano/single_cavg_lattice.f90, so that src/main/cavg_quality/ is cryo-EM only. (Alternative: it stays where it is as shared code; then only its nano-context plumbing leaves cavg_quality.)
    3. The recall target of the threshold rule: 0.95 (the plan's proposal, from the table above); the learn report prints 0.98, 0.95 and 0.90 beside each other so that the choice is visible.
    4. The features of the joint score: the six lattice features and the model correlation. The class population is a validity gate only (empty classes), not a feature: his labelled classes and the workflow's time chunks have different population semantics, and a learned population weight would not transfer. The learn report gives the single-feature baselines (lattice peak alone, correlation alone, the two together) beside the joint score, by the same rule.
    5. Training data (settled by the ruling of 01:40): the maintainer's selections on the class averages of the workflow's own 2D, made after phase 0 at the population he chooses; the earlier labelled sets may join them in the next run if their populations are comparable. The class populations tried: 20, 40, 60 and 100 frames per class.
    6. The alignment of class averages to a model exists three times (autorefine3D_nano's final pass, validate_cavgs_vs_model and cavgsproc_nano in single_commanders_experimental.f90, the model gate of trajectory_solve3D). This run makes it one routine and uses it in the new program, in trajectory_solve3D and in autorefine3D_nano; the two experimental programs keep their own copies unless the maintainer rules that they take the routine too.

    Terms. A trajectory is one tracked particle: a stack of graphene-subtracted frames (subtr_frames.mrc), 0.358 Å. refine2D_nano groups the frames into class averages that are time chunks of about twenty frames. The lattice features are Fourier measurements of one class average (its strongest Bragg peak over its ring mean, the spot count, arc, diameter, signal-to-noise, and the band presence). The start model is the face-centred cubic platinum particle simulate_nanoparticle builds for the estimated diameter. Aligning a class average to the model means finding the model projection it matches best and its correlation to it in the band 3 to 1 Å. Recall is the fraction of the maintainer's selected classes a rule keeps; removal is the fraction of his rejected classes it removes. The labelled trajectories are 71-17/traj04, 71-6/traj01 and 71-6/traj04, whose class averages he sorted by eye in October 2026 (cls2D_field.txt, ptcl2D_field.txt beside the frames).

    2. The program¶

    single_exec prg=trajectory_cavgs_rejection projfile=<p> vol1=<start model> mskdiam=<A> nthr=24, on a project whose class averages are registered (refine2D_nano or make_cavgs), in its own execution directory. Its modes are the quality_mode key the cryo-EM program uses, with the same meanings:

    • apply (default): measures every class average, aligns the valid ones to the model, scores them, sets the class states (cls2D, cls3D) and the particle states through map_cavgs_selection, writes the project; prune=yes drops the rejected frames. thres=<probability> moves the decision threshold for one run, as the lattice gate's thres did. The model is the built-in preset unless infile=<model file> is given.
    • analyze: the same measurements and score, with the project's cls2D state as the manual reference; writes the training table and the products below, leaves the project unchanged.
    • learn: fits the joint score from the training tables of filetab, writes the model file (fname) and the learn report (section 3). evaluate scores a model on training tables; promote emits the Fortran of a built-in preset from a model file.

    The measurements, per class average, in apply and analyze:

    1. Validity. A class is rejected outright when it is empty, holds invalid pixels, or has no measurable spectrum (the nano validity gates as they are now, flatness on the unfiltered average).
    2. The lattice bank, the six features of single_cavg_lattice (the measurement as it is now; mskdiam sets the soft mask of the spectrum).
    3. The model alignment. The valid classes are aligned to vol1 (refine3D_nano oritype=cls3D maxits=1 lp=1.0 trs=10 mskdiam=<mskdiam> volrec=no, the pass autorefine3D_nano runs against its final map); the correlation of each to its best reprojection is corr of cls3D, with the orientation and shift. One routine, align_cavgs_to_model in single_commanders_nano3D.f90, serves the new program, trajectory_solve3D (its commander is single_commanders_trajectory_solve3D.f90, a module of its own since 2026-10-31: a workflow commander of that size gets its own module) and autorefine3D_nano's final pass.
    4. The score. A logistic score over the seven features: the lattice peak enters raw (an absolute log10 ratio), the correlation raw (its level transfers, section 3), the other five by median and MAD over the valid classes, clipped, as now. The preset carries the weights, the intercept, the normalization choice and the threshold; a class is accepted at probability at least the threshold.

    Products in the execution directory: cavgs_rejection_classes.tsv (class, population, the seven raw features, score, state, manual state when there is one, best orientation and shift); cavgs_ranked.mrc with cavgs_ranked_reprojs.mrc, every valid class average and its model reprojection sorted by score, lowest first, and cavgs_ranked.txt naming the class of each slot (the maintainer judges from these; the twenty-pair sample of the previous run is gone); cavgs_selected.mrc and cavgs_rejected.mrc; in analyze, the training table cavgs_rejection_training.txt (the same # key=value header and one row per class as the cryo-EM table, so that the same reader serves). apply writes diam (the spot diameter) and lattice_peak on every scored cls2D row and the diameter's median over the accepted classes on the class-average entry of os_out, as now.

    Ownership: the program is ui -> exec -> commander -> domain: the UI entry in single_ui_trajectory.f90 with every key named here, the router case in single_exec_trajectory.f90, the commander commander_trajectory_cavgs_rejection in single_commanders_trajectory.f90, the domain in src/main/nano/single_cavg_rejection.f90 (feature assembly and normalization, the score and the decision, the model file and the built-in preset, the training table, the learner on simple_quality_logistic, the ranked products) with single_cavg_rejection_tester.f90, and the lattice bank in single_cavg_lattice.f90 (decision 2). The program's checks (mskdiam required by apply and analyze, vol1 by apply and analyze, filetab by learn and evaluate, thres a probability in (0,1)) are the commander's, on the command line before params%new, beside its defaults: the parameter class holds no program-specific checks (the maintainer's ruling of 2026-10-31; the block trajectory_solve3D had put there was moved into its commander the same night). Follow doc/policies/layering_and_project_ownership_policy.md: the commander reads the project once, the domain never reads a project, the commander writes the changed segments once.

    What leaves model_cavgs_rejection and src/main/cavg_quality/: the nano context (quality_context=nano, the nano validity gates and local-variance measurement, the nano bank size and names, the nano relational band), the nano preset (quality_model=nano, nano_model_spec, set_nano_threshold, thres on that program), the lattice feature policy of learn, the nano annotations of apply (diam, lattice_peak), the lattice comments of the training table, and the cavg quality nano sub-suite of unit_numerics. The cryo-EM program's analyze, learn and evaluate outputs are byte-identical before and after (section 5).

    trajectory_solve3D changes with it: its stages 6 and 7 (the lattice gate and the model gate) become one stage, 6_trajectory_cavgs_rejection, run with prune=yes; the stage count becomes 10; its contained model-gate code, its model_gate_nsd key and its sampled pairs go; thres passes through as the score threshold. Its records and resume are unchanged otherwise.

    3. The class averages for the maintainer's selections, and the class-population experiment (phase 0)¶

    For every trajectory the run makes the class averages he will select from: the workflow's own 2D on the raw frames, exactly as trajectory_solve3D runs it up to its 2D stage (trajectory_import of subtr_frames.mrc; center2D_nano ncls=10; estimate_diam on the centred chunk averages, mskdiam = 1.5 x max_diam, moldiam = min_diam; simulate_nanoparticle for the start model; refine2D_nano mskdiam=<mskdiam> center=no), with one difference: the class count. refine2D_nano at its default gives time chunks of about twenty frames (649 classes for the 71-17 trajectories, 684 for 71-6: the ceiling of frames over twenty). The experiment runs it at four populations, P = 20, 40, 60 and 100 frames per class (ncls = ceiling(nframes / P); the centering, the diameter and the start model are made once per trajectory and shared), so that the maintainer can judge at which population the raw averages show their lattice and choose the one to select on, and so that the numbers below say what each population buys.

    Per trajectory and population, in scratch/keep/popstudy/<dataset>/<trajectory>/P<frames>/: the project after the 2D and its class averages (cavgs_iter*.mrc of the last iteration; kept, these are what he selects on); the features of every non-empty class: the six lattice features (model_cavgs_rejection quality_model=nano mskdiam=<mskdiam> prune=no thres=0.01, which writes cavgs_quality_features.txt and rejects only the invalid classes) and the correlation, orientation and shift from the alignment to the start model (refine3D_nano oritype=cls3D maxits=1 lp=1.0 trs=10 volrec=no, as the previous run's model gate did; its scripts under gate_raw/ and the model-gate code in single_commanders_nano3D.f90 are the templates), merged into classes.tsv (class, population, the six features, correlation, e1 e2 e3 x y); the class average / reprojection pairs of every non-empty class as one stack, pairs_by_corr.mrc, sorted by correlation lowest first, and the class averages alone sorted by correlation and by lattice peak (cavgs_by_corr.mrc, cavgs_by_peak.mrc), each with a .txt naming the class of every slot (pure-Python MRC writing as the previous run's mrcpairs.py; the contact sheets are made on the maintainer's Mac after the fetch). The maintainer's selection on one of these stacks becomes a cls2D state of that project (selection with the kept classes), the training set of the next run.

    The numbers, in scratch/keep/popstudy/summary.tsv, per trajectory and population: classes with frames, the population's median and range, the wall time of the 2D, the fraction of consecutive frames sharing a class, the distribution of the lattice peak (median, 10th and 90th percentiles, the fraction at or above 0.52 and 0.65) and of the correlation (the same percentiles; the gap between its lower and upper modes, if the histogram shows two). On the three labelled trajectories, where every frame carries the maintainer's earlier state (ptcl2D_field.txt, through the frame's stack index), each class gets the majority label of its frames and a purity, and the table adds, per population: the fraction of frames in classes of purity at least 0.8; what a perfect class gate at that granularity could do (reject every majority-rejected class: the recall of his selected frames and the removal of his rejected frames, the ceiling any class-level rule has at that population); the AUC of the lattice peak and of the correlation against the majority label; and, by the recall-first rule at 0.95 on the three sets together, the removal of the lattice peak alone, the correlation alone and both. The Progress entry says, in plain words, at which population the features separate his labels best and what it costs in granularity.

    The training of the score, in the next run¶

    When the maintainer's selections exist (the stacks above, one population per trajectory, cls2D states on the projects), trajectory_cavgs_rejection quality_mode=analyze on each gives the training tables, and the three labelled trajectories' earlier classes, rebuilt on the raw frames as the previous run's raw study did (its scripts under ~/agent_runs/trajectory_solve3D/scratch/keep/gate_raw/, read only), may join them as further sets if their populations are comparable.

    learn fits the joint score on the tables of filetab: the logistic problem of simple_quality_logistic (L-BFGS-B, the dataset roles and class weights the cryo-EM learner uses, its lambda grid), features as in section 2. The threshold is the recall-first rule of the previous runs: the strictest threshold at which the recall of the maintainer's selected classes is at least the target on every training set (decision 3), the frame-weighted recall reported beside. The learn report (cavgs_rejection_learn_report.txt) holds, for the target and for 0.98, 0.95 and 0.90: per set and pooled, recall, removal, frame-weighted recall, the threshold; the same for the baselines of decision 4 by the same rule; the leave-one-trajectory-out check (the score refitted without each set, its recall and removal on that set at the threshold the others give); the feature weights and the AUC of every feature. The score is promoted as the built-in preset (quality_mode=promote, the snippet pasted into single_cavg_rejection.f90), and K_MODEL, LAT_PEAK_MIN, LAT_PEAK_MIN_RAW and model_gate_states are deleted with the old gates.

    A stop condition for that run: if at the target recall the joint score removes no more of the rejected classes than the lattice peak alone on every set (within 0.02), the alignment carries no evidence beyond the lattice; stop and report, the maintainer decides. The tables of the previous run say it will not come to that.

    5. Rules of the run¶

    • The repository's rules as in the previous run: release 4 keeps no backwards compatibility; THROW_HARD and THROW_WARN are macros, never continue their argument with //&, build the message in a string variable; Fortran does not short-circuit, never test a flag and an element of an allocatable array in one condition; no goto; no reliance on integer overflow; dead code met during the work is deleted; use ..., only: imports; large types never as plain components; new OpenMP code tested at 11 and 24 threads; git diff --summary checked for mode changes before a phase ends; no new CTest entries, SIMPLE_CTEST_BUDGET unchanged; python3 scripts/check_test_registry.py . --verbose and python3 scripts/check_descr.py . whenever a suite table, a tester registration, a router, a test-UI help or a file header changes; a new program needs its UI entry, router case and commander together, and its checks are the commander's, on the command line before params%new — nothing program-specific goes into the parameter class.
    • Nothing is rejected on evidence the denoiser has touched, and nothing in the score comes from a map made of the frames being judged.
    • The constants come from the labelled data by the rule of section 3, set once in the next run, and are not revisited on the downstream numbers; no threshold or default moves to let a trajectory through. The provisional preset of phase 1 is a placeholder that lets the program and its test run; it is not a result and the policy note says so.
    • Tests: single_cavg_rejection_tester (the score in closed form on a small table; the threshold rule on a toy training set with known recall and removal; the model file written and read back; the ranked products of a synthetic stack) and the lattice tester, moved, as sub-suites cavg lattice and cavg rejection of unit_single; unit_numerics loses cavg quality nano; unit_ui, unit_project; the single_workflow fcc suite runs trajectory_solve3D with the new stage and keeps its assertion that the gate loses at most 5 percent of the simulated trajectory's frames beside the map's floors and the pose checks; wurtzite as it is. The cryo-EM byte gate: before any source change, phase 1 runs model_cavgs_rejection quality_mode=analyze on a cryo-EM project with class averages (a refine2D of the bgal set under /data, its cls2D states as they are, quality_context=chunk and pool) and learn on the resulting table, and keeps the outputs under scratch/keep/byte_gate/; after the separation the same commands give byte-identical files (cmp), and the fast gate, unit_numerics and unit_single pass.
    • The data under /data2/NanoX_trajs and /data/NanoX/zenodo are read only; the previous run's directory ~/agent_runs/trajectory_solve3D is read only; python without numpy (python3.12).
