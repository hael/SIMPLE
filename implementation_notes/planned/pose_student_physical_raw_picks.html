    The pose student revisited: the physical teacher, raw picks and alignment over the cone¶

    Status, 2026-11-18: done; the centring policy adopted (ruling R7; report: pose_student_physical_raw_picks_report.md). Phase 2 kept the alignment over the cone (SK) and removed the mode's pair (S1); Phase 3 adopted the physical teacher P3 on the maps (ruling R5); Phase 4's raw-pick route was not adopted (its map coarser than the 2D-centred route's on streptavidin), and under ruling R7 the policy (training and predicted particles centred by the prior solve2D, shift refinement only after the prediction, over the cone) was measured as C3SK and adopted. Phase 4 ran on master's 68638be28 (ruling R6), where the gates, the identity and the validations ran. Measurements of Phases 0 to 3 are on fd9af0add (ruling R4, 2026-11-17), the commit that removes the 3D in-plane posterior insertion and simulated_workflow_1jxy; the run launched on 1ea0f8340 (the 3D in-plane insertion the cone, inpl_posterior removed), whose Phases 0 and 1 are recorded below as superseded. Depends on: the pose student (doc/algorithms/pose_student.md), what the class student established (planned/student_inplane_posterior.md with its report: the physical teacher P; planned/class_student_raw_picks.md with its report: no centring, alignment after prediction over the candidate list), and the current solve3D, whose solutions are the best yet (Hans, 2026-11-16).

    1. Why¶

    The pose student has not changed since its training-speed and working-grid runs except its prediction seed (euler_inpl_closest, 2026-11-15). Meanwhile the class student showed four things that carry over:

    • The teacher should be what the run ends at, the physical posterior. The class student's tempered teacher (prob_beta x T) made it over-cautious (97.7% where its threshold was calibrated for 95%); the physical teacher held the target and shortlisted 5 to 10 more particles in 100. 3D refinement has no in-plane insertion (removed in fd9af0add, ruling R4: each pose is inserted at its own angle), so the pose student's matching teacher is the one-rotation table rows read at the physical temperature T, not the in-plane marginal (R, rejected for 3D on 2026-11-15).
    • Judge against the physical posterior's best pose, not a tempered draw or the teacher's stored pose: the class student's old reference made safe shortlists look unsafe (72% against 97.7%).
    • No centring; predict, then align over the candidate set. On raw picks the class student's mode matched the reference for 23 to 35% of particles while its list held it for 90 to 96%; aligning over the list beat the centred route on every set. The pose student's cone of directions (with their mirrors) plays the list's part, each direction's reprojection searched at every in-plane rotation over the shift disk (Hans's neighbourhood idea, planned/polar_student_future_directions.md section 6). Today's registration (a 5 x 5 grid over 25 Å at the read-out pose) cannot cover picking errors of 16 to 22 Å at the 90th percentile.
    • Costs on small sets are measured and reported, never gated (Hans, 2026-11-15).

    What is known about the pose student on uncentred particles: it seeds about 1% (doc/algorithms/pose_student.md), and a 2 px shift moves its posterior by a total variation near 0.7. Whether its cone still holds the truth on uncentred particles was never measured; that is Phase 0's first question (decision 9).

    2. Contract¶

    • Off state: refine2D and refine3D byte-identical at one thread with a fixed seed (simulated_workflow suite 6vxx, test=identity; 1jxy no longer exists on the base, ruling R4); the class student byte-identical to the base (model header and weights).
    • The training set stays the teacher's (solve3D's) particles at the teacher's poses and shifts; only the unseen particles change.
    • No new command-line key or parameter; no second code path kept for a rejected variant.
    • The networks keep their symmetries (rotation equivariance on the grid, mirror invariance, the conjugation symmetry) and training stays bit-identical at a fixed thread count.
    • The refine3D route that consumes the student's cones (prob_neigh geom) is unchanged; whether the alignment over the cone can replace it is reported, not decided.

    3. Decisions¶

    1. (2026-11-16, Hans) The physical teacher P3 for the pose student: the dense 3D table's rows read at the physical temperature T, no prob_beta, one rotation per direction as today; the threshold calibrated on it as now. The teacher audits itself on real rows as the class student's does (the row's masses against exp(-T (L - L_min)) normalised over the directions at T; exact up to single-precision rounding).
    2. (2026-11-16, Hans) The validation's reference is the physical posterior's best pose: from the dense 3D table at T against the teacher's map on the same unseen particles, with its shift disk, the direction and in-plane angle of the largest mass (mirror pairs folded as the student folds them). How the run computes it (Phase 0, for the maintainer's review): learn_pose_student's rows (the dense table at the student's grid and band, prob_beta 1, the class reference's disk of three 10 Å widths, every unseen particle not yet centred) against the teacher's map, started from the most centred position the particle has (the 2D solution's shift on the real sets, the stored shift of the centred test half on the truth sets); the row's mode with its in-plane angle and refined shift. One reference per particle and teacher, frozen, serves every arm: the arms see the same images. Started from the raw picks instead, the same table put its mode within 6° of the truth for 72.5% of the truth sets' test half against 99.1% from the centred start (it agreed with the centred reference for 64%): the dense search's screen misses off-centre modes, so a raw-pick start would judge every raw-pick arm against a broken reference. The raw-pick dense pass is kept as a read of its own (D0, decision 7).
    3. (2026-11-16, Hans) No pre-centring of unseen particles: they enter at their picked positions (shifts zero). Withdrawn by ruling R7 (Hans, 2026-11-18) and replaced by the centring policy, in Hans's words: "(1) centering comes from the solve2D run prior to solve3D on both particles used for training and particles being predicted, (2) shift refinement only enters after the prediction on the reference obtained from the training set, thus mirroring the class student. This is the policy most consistent with what we want to accomplish in the stream with the HHCNN." (In the stream the unseen particles' 2D shifts come from the class student's own alignment after its prediction; in this run they are the solve2D shifts, as C0's.)
