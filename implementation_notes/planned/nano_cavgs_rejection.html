    A nano context for model_cavgs_rejection: class-average rejection for nanoparticle time series¶

    Proposal, 2026-10-28, written for one autonomous run. Written against the working tree of that day, which holds, uncommitted, the three things this plan builds on: the rank-based AUC in src/main/cavg_quality/simple_cavg_quality_stats.f90, the bank-free logistic core src/main/quality/simple_quality_logistic.f90 that the class-average learner now calls (with its tester and unit_numerics sub-suite "quality logistic"), and the lattice measurements of src/main/frame_quality/single_frame_quality_{types,feats,floor}.f90, which this plan re-homes and the run deletes from where they are.

    simple_exec prg=model_cavgs_rejection quality_mode=analyze quality_context=nano projfile=... mskdiam=...
simple_exec prg=model_cavgs_rejection quality_mode=learn filetab=... fname=...
simple_exec prg=model_cavgs_rejection quality_mode=apply quality_model=nano projfile=... mskdiam=... prune=yes

    1. What this is¶

    The nanoparticle workflow (tseries_track_particles, trajectory_denoise, trajectory_import, analysis2D_nano, autorefine3D_nano) selects frames by hand on the class averages of analysis2D_nano: classes whose aligned average shows atomic contrast are kept, their frames go to 3D. This plan makes that selection a model, the same way the cryo-EM path does it: model_cavgs_rejection gains a quality_context nano, with its own hard gates, a feature bank extended by a lattice family, a normalization against the project's floor of lattice-free classes, and a learned preset nano trained from the maintainer's class labels and promoted when it reproduces them and the 3D reconstruction from its selection matches the one from the hand selection. Nothing of the per-frame approach survives except the measurements it developed; section 9 records what it established.

    2. Why the class average is the unit¶

    The maintainer's judgement works on aligned averages, the labels he makes are class states, and the reconstruction he validates runs from class selections. The per-frame experiment of 2026-10-27 showed the other side: frames below single-frame detection that a class selection keeps cannot be told apart from the ones it rejects by anything measured on a frame, raw or denoised (section 9). An aligned average of twenty frames has the signal a frame lacks, and the cryo-EM machinery — analyze from manual cls2D states, the learner, evaluate, promote, apply mapping class states to particles and pruning — already does the whole loop. The development is a context and a bank, not a program.

    3. The nano context¶

    quality_context=nano is the fourth context beside chunk, pool and sieve (simple_cavg_quality_types.f90, validate_quality_context, cavg_hard_reject_for_context in simple_cavg_quality_feats.f90; the UI choices of quality_context and quality_model in simple_ui_params_common.f90; the per-mode checks in simple_parameters_phases.f90). It differs from the cryo-EM contexts in three places.

    Hard gates. The population gate of the cryo-EM contexts would reject the good classes: on the delivered labels the selected classes are the smaller ones (the lattice-free frames pile into large junk classes; section 5), and the FRC "resolution" of a nanoparticle class is quantized at a few shells and carries nothing. nano keeps the validity gates only: invalid pixels; an empty class (pop <= 0); both local variances at or below EPS. The foreground-geometry gates (Otsu after a 30 A low-pass, centroid outside the mask, component outside the mask) are not applied: at 0.358 A per pixel in a 57 A box a 30 A low-pass leaves two shells, and the particle fills the mask.

    Feature bank. The bank is per context. chunk, pool and sieve keep their fourteen features, unchanged in number, order, extraction constants and presets. nano has those fourteen plus a lattice family of six, measured on the class average by the code of single_frame_quality_feats.f90 re-homed as src/main/cavg_quality/simple_cavg_quality_lattice.f90 (a shared module, as the backend it serves; section 10 has the naming question): lattice_peak (log10 of the largest 3 by 3 smoothed power over its ring mean, over 3 A to Nyquist with the graphene shells excluded), spot_count (local maxima of that ratio above the floor's threshold), spot_arc (the strongest spot's extent along the ring over across it; straight fringes give a round spot), spot_diam (the projected-sphere diameter fitted to the strongest spot: the particle's size per class), spot_snr (the fit's amplitude over background), band_presence (band-passed energy in the central disc over the annulus, unmasked). The fourteen cryo-EM features are computed for nano with their resolution constants scaled to the specimen: FOREGROUND_SEG_LP, SIGNAL_METRIC_LP and the overfit band are the cryo-EM values divided by a context factor so that the foreground segmentation sees the particle and the band-pass sees the lattice — the factor and the values are fixed by the study of section 6, not guessed here; the fallback is to drop the features that stay degenerate.

    Bank size: CAVG_QUALITY_NFEATS becomes the maximum (20); the active feature list is a property of the context (cavg_quality_nfeats(context), the names, families and directions by context), and the model spec, the training-table writer and reader (columns keyed by name, the quality_context line selecting the bank), the normalizer, the feature-policy masks and the model-file reader (feature_index_from_token) all follow the context's list. The cryo-EM presets' coefficient arrays are written for fourteen (spec%linear_coefficients(1:14) = [...], set_pairwise_interactions_for_feature_count(spec, 14, ...)), and the gate of section 7 holds them byte-identical.

    Normalization. The lattice family is normalized against the project's floor: a two-component mixture (xd_gmm, the two starts of fit_k, the code of single_frame_quality_floor.f90) over the classes' lattice_peak; the lower component is the floor, the classes without a lattice; lattice_peak enters as distance from the floor's mean in floor sd, the other lattice features by median and MAD of the floor classes with a minimum scale (one count for spot_count, a tenth of the interquartile range otherwise); the guard (floor weight at least 5% of the classes, separation at least 4 floor sd) falls back to the cryo-EM median/MAD normalization and records it in the table header. The fourteen cryo-EM features keep their median/MAD normalization. The clip is the bank's CLIP_Z.

    The relational feature (simple_cavg_quality_relations.f90, each average correlated against its k nearest neighbours after in-plane alignment) is kept: averages of one particle correlate, junk does not. Its correlation band is a property of the model spec (relational_corr_hp, _lp, _trs), so the nano preset carries nano values (high-pass about 15 A, low-pass at the lattice, 1.5 A, shift range a few pixels), fixed by the study.

    Policies. The learner's feature policies for nano are lattice, lattice_plus_signal, lattice_plus_microchunk, all; the cryo-EM policies are unchanged.

    Presets. nano, the learned model, added by promote when section 8 is met; until then analyze and evaluate run with the context and no preset (the hard-gate-only decision), as the program already allows.

    4. Pipeline position¶

    tseries_track_particles → trajectory_denoise → trajectory_import → analysis2D_nano → model_cavgs_rejection quality_mode=apply quality_model=nano prune=yes → simulate_nanoparticle → autorefine3D_nano. apply annotates cls2D with quality, quality_cluster and accept, maps the class states to ptcl2D and ptcl3D, prunes, and writes the project once; the frames keep their original index in indstk through the prune, and any time-windowed consumer reads it (the project's row order is no longer time). The particle diameter per class (spot_diam) is written to the class rows and its median over the accepted classes to the table header and os_out, beside the mask diameter, which the 3D stage takes from there instead of from estimate_diam.

    5. Inputs and labels¶

    A labeled dataset is one analysis2D_nano run with the maintainer's selection. As delivered (2026-10-28, ~/Processing/NanoX/NanoX_trajs/<dataset>/<trajectory>/), each holds trajectory_denoised.mrcs (the stack the 2D ran on; its header pixel size is wrong, 0.0358 A, so every import passes smpd=0.358), subtr_frames.mrc (the raw frames, not needed here), trajectory_denoise_flex.txt, and the two project fields written as oris text, one row per entry: cls2D_field.txt (class pop res corr state; the maintainer's selection is the state) and ptcl2D_field.txt (the full particle record per frame: class, the in-plane alignment e3 x y, state, indstk, eo, corr). No project file and no class-average stack are delivered; the run rebuilds both: import_particles stk=trajectory_denoised.mrcs smpd=0.358 ctf=no oritab=ptcl2D_field.txt (the oris reader takes the text table), make_cavgs ncls=<classes> refs=cavgs.mrc from the stored assignment and alignment (which fills cls2D with population, resolution and correlation and registers the stack in os_out), then the class states from cls2D_field.txt through selection oritype=cls2D infile=<the 0/1 column>, checked against the delivered populations class by class. The regenerated averages are the originals up to the even/odd weighting of make_cavgs; if the original cavgs_iter*.mrc stacks become available they are used instead and the regenerated ones compared to them.

    Checked on delivery (2026-10-28): all three are complete and consistent: 71-17/traj04 (649 classes, 185 selected, 12,971 frames), 71-6/traj01 (684 classes, 425 selected, 13,662 frames) and 71-6/traj04 (684 classes, 264 selected): every class's population equals its frame count in ptcl2D, every frame's state equals its class's state, the alignment is present on every frame, indstk is the row, populations run from 0 to 50 (two empty classes in each 71-6 set). The classes are not time chunks: only 0.5% of consecutive frames share a class, the frames of one class are a median of 270 to 300 frames apart and span most of the trajectory. That is not what the code reads as doing: refine2D_nano sets tseries=yes and nptcls_per_cls=20, the class assignment starts as consecutive chunks (init_tseries_refs), and with nptcls_per_cls defined the greedy search is strategy2D_tseries, which lets a frame move only to the nearest populated class before or after its own per iteration, so after the 15 iterations of the run (updatecnt) a class should hold frames from within a few hundred frames of its chunk, not from everywhere. Either the labeled runs were made with other settings or an earlier build, or the constraint does not hold in practice; Phase 0 settles it with one analysis2D_nano on a hold-out trajectory and the same statistic. For this plan the scattered assignment is the easier case: a class is then a group of alike frames from the whole trajectory, as a cryo-EM class is, and its average is a cleaner object than a 20-frame time chunk. Three labeled trajectories are the minimum for leave-one-out; more make the held-out numbers mean more.

    What the delivered cls2D fields already say, before any image is read (class-level AUC against the selection, empty classes excluded): the stored class correlation corr (the frames' mean correlation to their average, what the cryo-EM bank reads as corr_frc_proxy) 0.965, 0.907, 0.908 on the three sets; population 0.23, 0.44, 0.32, that is inverted: the selected classes are the smaller ones (median 15 to 19 frames against 20 to 22), because the lattice-free frames pile into large junk classes; the FRC resolution 0.47 to 0.62, nothing (its values are quantized at a few shells). Two consequences are already fixed by this: the population hard gate of the cryo-EM contexts would reject the good classes and is off for nano (section 3), and the stored correlation alone is the baseline every model has to beat (section 6). Labeled datasets train and are evaluated leave-one-out; every other trajectory of ~/Processing/NanoX/NanoX_trajs is a hold-out whose analysis2D_nano output the run makes itself and scores for review.

    6. The study, before the Fortran¶

    On the labeled datasets, in order:

    1. model_cavgs_rejection quality_mode=analyze quality_context=chunk as it is (the population gate off through the existing threshold_pop switch, mskdiam from the project), to see what the fourteen cryo-EM features do on nanoparticle averages: the training table's per-feature AUC against the labels, and which features are degenerate at these resolutions;
    2. the same with the resolution constants scaled (a numpy reproduction of the six affected features at two or three candidate factors, on the class-average stacks), to fix the factor of section 3;
    3. the lattice family in numpy on the class-average stacks (single_frame_quality_feats.f90's definitions, the prototype scripts of the previous run in ~/agent_runs/runs/frame_rejection_mac and the trajectory folder's trajectory_focus_prototype/ are the references), the floor over classes, per-feature AUC against the labels, and the floor's separation per dataset;
    4. a numpy pairwise-logistic fit mirroring the learner, leave-one-dataset-out, with and without the lattice family, against three baselines: the stored class correlation alone (AUC 0.91 to 0.97 on the delivered labels, section 5: the bar), the lattice floor alone (lattice_peak of the class at 4 floor sd) and the relational feature alone. If the stored correlation alone matches the fit, the honest product is a per-project threshold on that one number with the floor as its reference, and the bank is diagnostic.

    Decision rules, written in the journal: a feature family enters the nano bank when its best member's leave-one-out AUC against the labels is at least 0.6 and the fit's class-level balanced accuracy drops by at least 0.01 without it; a cryo-EM feature that is degenerate on nano averages at the chosen factor (robust spread zero on more than half the datasets) is dropped from the nano list.

    7. Tests and gates¶

    • The cryo-EM path is byte-identical: model_cavgs_rejection learn (model file and report) and evaluate on saved class-average training tables, and analyze on a class-average project, before and after, all outputs compared byte for byte; the saved tables are made in Phase 0 from the repository's test fixtures, failing that from synthetic tables as the previous run did (~/frame_rejection_mac/scripts/cavg_gate.sh); unit_numerics' three sub-suites ("cavg quality relations", "cavg quality selection", "quality logistic").
    • A nano sub-suite of unit_numerics (no new CTest entry; scripts/check_test_registry.py): the lattice family on synthetic class averages (a lattice at a known spacing, the same low-passed, noise at the SNR of a 20-frame nanoparticle average): band and spot shell, separation, the floor and its guard (an all-lattice project and an all-empty one reject nothing), the diameter against disc and sphere objects (the calibration of the previous run as assertions), spot_arc on straight against bent fringes, band_presence mask-free, the per-context bank sizes and name lists, the table round trip for both bank sizes, a model file round trip for a 20-feature spec, and the gates of the context.
    • The commander: analyze on a labeled nano project writes the table with the context's columns and the floor in its header; apply with a nano model maps states, prunes, keeps indstk, writes once.

    8. Acceptance and promotion¶

    The learned nano model is promoted when, on every held-out labeled dataset: its class-level agreement with the maintainer's labels (balanced accuracy at the model's threshold, and AUC) is not below the best of the three baselines of section 6, step 4 (the stored correlation alone being the one to beat); and, on at least one held-out trajectory, autorefine3D_nano from its selection gives a map and atomic model that match the hand selection's (the FSC bands outside the lattice band within the run-to-run spread, atoms within 1 A of the hand selection's model at the same fraction). A model that matches the baselines but does not beat them is promoted only if it beats them on the 3D criterion; one that fails either is reported, not promoted, and the floor baseline is recorded as the gate the maintainer can use by hand.

    9. What the per-frame experiment established (2026-10-26 and 27)¶

    Kept here because it fixes several design choices above; the full record is in ~/agent_runs/runs/frame_rejection_mac/ and in the prototype folder of 71-17/traj01.

    • The lattice measurement: the maximum of the 3 by 3 smoothed power over its ring mean, over a species-agnostic band with the graphene shells excluded, is the right statistic; the smoothing matters (a Bragg spot of a 30 A particle spans two Fourier pixels at this sampling); the plain band maximum (the collaborators' quantity) does as well on raw frames and is dose-dependent.
    • The floor: the classes or frames without a lattice form one tight population; a two-component mixture finds it, Otsu's midpoint does not (it splits the broad visible population), a white-noise null sits below the real floor, and the floor's left side underestimates its spread. Normalize against the fitted floor; guard on its weight and separation.
    • On single raw frames the floor is flat along a trajectory; on flex-denoised frames it drifts with each frame's pooled population and no threshold holds, while the rank still does. On class averages the question does not arise: the floor is over classes of one project.
    • Lattice visibility flickers frame to frame as the particle tumbles (runs of consecutive strong frames have a median length of one); a running median alone rejects the flashes. Class averages of time chunks absorb this by construction.
    • Frames below single-frame detection that a class selection keeps are indistinguishable from the ones it rejects by every per-frame feature tried, on raw and denoised frames, and by their correlation to the 3D map (AUC 0.46 to 0.60); the 3D from the floor rule's half of the frames matched the hand selection's outside the lattice band. The class average is where the judgement can be made.
    • The Bragg-spot diameter (projected-sphere model fitted to the spot) is unbiased on discs and 12% low on spheres at a single frame's SNR; on an average it is the better size estimate and replaces estimate_diam.
    • Defects found and left outside the per-frame work: make_cavgs in time-series mode re-splits ptcl2D halves by row parity even when halves exist (simple_make_cavgs_strategy.f90), so center2D_nano cannot follow a prune; the MRC writer sets a stack's z cell before its frame count (cosmetic); the particle record has no slot for a per-row probability or diameter (class rows do, in cls2D, which this plan uses).
