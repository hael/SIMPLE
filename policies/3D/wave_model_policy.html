    Wave-model policy¶

    Contract of the code that runs today: SIMPLE's wave operators, which predict a particle image from a three-dimensional electrostatic potential with wave optics, their hand-written derivatives, the program reconstruct3D_wave that reconstructs half maps with them, and simulate_scattering and the wave mode of simulate_particles, which draw data with the same operators. The design came from Ghostbuster, the reconstruction inside the specter package, and was rebuilt on SIMPLE's own machinery; the plan with its measurements is doc/implementation_notes/planned/ghostbuster_operators.md (sections 3 and 4, and section 8 for the evidence of every number below), the run's summary doc/implementation_notes/planned/ghostbuster_operators_report.md; the run that added the wave mode of simulate_particles and made the operator faster is doc/implementation_notes/completed/star_origins_and_wave_simulate_run_report.md. The user guides are doc/user_guide/reconstruct3D_wave.md, doc/user_guide/simulate_scattering.md and doc/user_guide/simulate_particles.md.

    Terms used throughout:

    • Potential V: the specimen's electrostatic potential on the native box N^3, voxel size a (smpd), in volts.
    • Padded lattice: the 2N grid on which SIMPLE's PCG (preconditioned conjugate gradients) reconstruction does every Fourier operation (pcg_lattice). The unknown stays on the native box.
    • Gather and scatter: the Kaiser-Bessel (KB) interpolation that reads the volume's transform at a 3D point, and its exact transpose.
    • Ewald paraboloid: the surface of the volume's transform that an image of a thick specimen samples, kz = kappa lambda |k|^2 / 2 rather than the central plane kz = 0.
    • Exit wave psi: the complex electron wave leaving the specimen. Lens transfer T: the objective lens's complex factor.
    • Weak-phase model: SIMPLE's image model elsewhere, image = CTF (contrast transfer function) times the central section, which is the first-order limit of every wave model.

    1. Scope¶

    Fixed by the maintainer (rulings of 2026-10-23, in the plan):

    • reconstruct3D_wave is a standalone shared-memory program. reconstruct3D, refine3D, the reconstruction service and their strategies do not use the wave operators; rec_backend=wave is reserved for that later integration. nparts is refused.
    • Alignment stays on SIMPLE's flat references. Poses and CTF are inputs.
    • No tomography.
    • simulate_scattering runs on the same operators, with Kirkland's sign conventions and the defocus at the specimen midplane.
    • Keys: wave_model=projection|first_born|rytov|multislice, ewald=neg|pos|no. No aliases of retired keys.

    Not modelled: beam tilt and trefoil (the project has no fields for them), anisotropic magnification, per-particle contrast scale, inelastic absorption, detector response, ice and coherence envelopes. Specter itself is never a dependency: nothing in the repository calls Python, and no test needs specter; its outputs enter the tests as data.

    3. Image formation¶

    For particle i with rotation R_i, shift s and CTF parameters:

    S_i(h,k) = gather(padf [h, k, kz] R_i) exp(+2 pi i (h s_x + k s_y) / N)    native disk, both signs of h
kz       = kappa wl (h^2 + k^2) / (2N),  wl = lambda / a,  kappa = -1 (ewald=neg), +1 (pos), 0 (no)
u        = IFFT_2N[ embed(S_i) ]
psi      = exp(c u)       projection (kappa = 0); Rytov (curved, see below)
psi      = 1 + c u        first Born
psi_T    = IFFT_2N[ T FFT_2N[psi] ]
I_hat    = crop_N( FFT_2N[ |psi_T|^2 ] )                                    native disk
p_i      = -g I_hat / N^2  (k /= 0)                                         SIMPLE's particle units (1/N^2: the unnormalised transform)

    with c = i sigma a (sqrt(1 - alpha^2) + i alpha), sigma the interaction constant and alpha = fraca, so the amplitude contrast of the wave models is the project's per-particle fraca. The rules:

    • Native disk. S_i is zero beyond the native Nyquist radius; the residual is formed on the native disk in Fourier space, whitened by the particle's sigma2 when objfun=euclid, where the PCG backend forms its right-hand side.
    • Oversampling 2. The exponential and the intensity are formed on the twice-finer grid, which is the padded lattice's size. Oversampling 1 errs by 0.7 to 2.7% on the protein and Pt sets against 3e-4 to 8e-3 for 2; no key offers it.
    • Rytov at the exit face. On the curved models the field is carried half a box downstream with Kirkland's propagator P = exp(-i pi lambda (N a / 2) |k|^2), exponentiated there, and carried back to the midplane for the lens (P^-1 folded into T). Without curvature Rytov is projection, bitwise.
    • Lens. T = exp(i eval_phase(phshift)) exp(-bfac |k|^2 / 4), with T(0) = 1: a phase plate's shift acts on the scattered waves only. SIMPLE's eval_phase is -chi in Kirkland's sign, so this is Kirkland's (and specter's) exp(-i chi). Amplitude contrast lives in c, not in T.
    • Wavelength. The CTF's, 12.26 / sqrt(V + 0.9784e-6 V^2), 3.4e-4 short of the exact relativistic value; shared with the CTF by design.
    • Multislice. The particle's cube C(h,k,l) is gathered at padf [h,k,l] R_i S_g on the Nyquist sphere (|l| < N/2), times N^3, the shift phase and the symmetry mean; one transform along l gives the sections' spectra s_j = (1/N) sum_l C exp(+2 pi i l z_j / N), z_j = j - (N/2 + 1). Each section is embedded in the fine grid, transmitted by exp(c u_j) and propagated by exp(-i pi lambda a |k|^2) to the next; the exit wave is carried back to the box centre (N/2 - 1 sections when the beam enters through the first section, ewald=neg; N/2 through the last, ewald=pos) and passes wave_imaging's lens and detector. No real-space rotated cube is formed: the cube gather and scatter are exact transposes, the same lattice and window as the reconstruction. The adjoint is the reverse sweep with conj(P) and conj(t_j), checkpoints every ceil(sqrt(N)) sections. Multislice needs a curvature sign (the beam direction); ewald=no is refused.

    The weak-phase identity ties every convention to code refinement has used for years: at zero potential the linearisation is 2 g sigma a N times the PCG backend's forward_plane times its build_transfer (CTF with shift, defocus, astigmatism and amplitude contrast). The wave projector and wave imaging tests hold it to 1e-5.

    3.1 The field's transforms¶

    A wave_field holds an n x n complex field (x the first index), in real or Fourier space as its owner keeps track; Fourier arrays are in FFT order (index i holds frequency wave_freq(i,n)). The transforms work in place on the field's own FFTW buffer with plans made once (FFTW_ESTIMATE, the same arithmetic in every run): fwd is the unnormalised forward DFT F (sign -1), bwd its inverse F^-1 = F^H / n^2, so fwd^H = n^2 bwd and bwd^H = fwd / n^2. One field belongs to one thread; its plans are made and destroyed in the unnamed critical section; a field is never copied (a copy would alias the buffer).

    A native-disk spectrum S(h,k), -R <= h,k <= R, h^2 + k^2 <= R^2, is the transform of a native image of box nat = 2R. embed places it on the fine grid scaled by (n/nat)^2, so that bwd gives the native image's band-limited interpolant at its own amplitude; crop takes the disk back scaled by (nat/n)^2, so crop(embed(S)) = S and the crop of fwd(f) is the native transform of f sampled at the native pixels; embed_t and crop_t are their transposes under Re<a,b>. On a grid of the native box itself (n = nat, the oversampling 1 of measurements) the disk's two Nyquist points of an axis share a bin: embed adds them and crop gives the bin to both, so the pairs stay transposes.

    The optional real field (new(n, with_real=.true.)) has out-of-place r2c and c2r plans: fwd_real_crop is crop(fwd(f)) of the real field, the negative h from Hermitian symmetry; crop_t_real sets the real field to n^2 Re(bwd(crop_t(S))), building the Hermitian part of crop_t(S) on the half grid (each disk point adds half its value to its bin and half its conjugate to the mirrored bin, which covers the shared Nyquist bins). The image chain uses them for the intensity, its derivative and the real part taken in the adjoint.

    5. Units and the scale link¶

    • Images. SIMPLE's particles are contrast-inverted and normalised to unit background noise; relative intensity is 1 - x / g with the gain g = sqrt(total_dose) smpd at the particles' native pixel size (the normalisation precedes any Fourier crop, so box_crop keeps it). total_dose is required.
    • Maps. By the weak-phase identity a SIMPLE map is 2 g sigma a N times the potential in volts, the scale link, computed in one place, electron_scale_link(total_dose, kv, smpd, box) (simple_math_ctf), by reconstruct3D_wave and simulate_particles. The solver works in volts; the program writes its maps at SIMPLE's scale, so they compare directly with reconstruct3D maps.
    • Prior in volts. The PCG backend's normal operator, density and ML (maximum-likelihood) prior, the per-shell regularisation it builds from the half maps' FSC (Fourier shell correlation), carry a factor boxpd^3 (boxpd = 2N, the padded box); the prior in volts is kappa_s^2 P / boxpd^3 with kappa_s = 2 g sigma a N (the wave projector test holds the link to 1e-4).
    • The fitted gain (gain_fitted in the diagnostics) is the one-scalar fit of the nonlinear part of the prediction to the data. It is a diagnostic, never used. It is meaningful for Rytov, projection and multislice; first Born's fit is not a gain. It falls short of the dose's gain when the particles' noise normalisation sees delocalised signal in the background (3.5 to 9% on the run's sets); it is the signal that the scale link of real data is off.

    6. reconstruct3D_wave¶

    Per state, in shared memory:

    1. The halves come from the project's one maker, ensure_field_halves. The state's particles are read once into the in-memory pass buffer (fill_pass_buffer) when it fits the memory budget, and every later pass copies from it; otherwise (the log reports the buffer's status) every pass reads the stacks.
    2. Start: each half's flat PCG solve (maxits_pcg), the ML prior from the FSC of the pair (tau, hp), the regularised flat map in closed form (maxits_ml coupled iterations on top). It is written as recvol_stateNN_<half>_flat.mrc.
    3. Wave solve of each half from its start, by nlcg_solve with maxits (default 4) iterations: preconditioner the PCG backend's, step length from the exact slope and the Gauss-Newton curvature ||J d||^2 on a subset (every k-th particle of the half, 5% with at least 256 and at most 1000 particles), refined by a parabola through the subset's loss. Support: pcg_mskfile when given, else the mskdiam sphere.
    4. Outputs: the regularised halves recvol_stateNN_even.mrc, _odd.mrc, their mean recvol_stateNN.mrc; the unfiltered halves _even_unfil.mrc, _odd_unfil.mrc (the Wiener filter undone isotropically, by the PCG operator's own shrink factor averaged over each shell); the FSC of the unfiltered pair (fsc_stateNN.bin, RESOLUTION_STATENN); per half reconstruct3D_wave_stateNN_<half>_diagnostics.txt (model, gains, solver history, passes).
    5. Project: read once by the builder; the merged map and the FSC are registered in the out segment, written once at the end. The particle field is not written.

    Refused: nparts; automsk other than no; pcg_solvent=yes; a missing or non-positive total_dose; objfun=euclid without the project's noise spectra; wave_model=projection with a curvature and multislice without one; a state with particles in one half only; particles of a state at different voltages; a project in which no state has particles.

    Defaults: wave_model=rytov, ewald=neg (no for projection), maxits=4, winsz=1.5 (the PCG backend's three-tap KB window; winsz=3 gives seven taps, ten times smaller interpolation errors, at 1.4 times the run time, and changed no band of the run's ribosome set).

    6.1 The solver¶

    nlcg_solve minimises a client's objective (data misfit plus prior), held as contiguous rank-1 views; the engine owns only its search vectors. The client (nlcg_objective) supplies: size, the length of x; eval, L(x) and its gradient (a full pass over the data); curvature, the Gauss-Newton curvature along d, ||J d||^2 plus the prior's d^T P d, which may be estimated on a subset scaled to the whole; line_losses, the losses at x + t(j) d on the step subset (the same data in every call of one iteration, so its values compare with each other but not with eval); precond, z = M r with M symmetric positive definite; dot, the inner product in double precision; project, v = P v, the projection onto the constraints (a support, P^2 = P).

    The method is preconditioned Polak-Ribiere conjugate gradients, beta clipped at zero; a direction that does not descend restarts from the preconditioned steepest descent. The step along d is the Gauss-Newton estimate t0 = -<g,d> / curvature(d), the minimiser of the quadratic model; with refine_step it is refined by the parabola through the subset losses at 0, t0 and 2 t0 when that parabola is convex, kept within [tmin_frac, tmax_frac] t0. A step that raises the full loss is halved up to max_backtracks times (each trial P(x + t d) from the same start); one that still raises it is not taken: the iterate stays, the search restarts from steepest descent, and a steepest descent that cannot descend ends the solve (no_descent). The gradient tolerance applies to the projected gradient P g. On a quadratic objective without refinement this is linear preconditioned CG in exact arithmetic.

    7. Numerical invariants¶

    • Zero curvature is the PCG backend. gather_plane and scatter_plane at kappa = 0 are the PCG code, bitwise; every PCG result is byte-identical to the code before the move (the identity case of the plan's Phase 1, rerun in every phase).
    • Curvature bound. ewald_curv refuses a paraboloid that would take a padded point more than 0.49 voxel past the Nyquist sphere, so no window reaches past the wrap table. The rim prefilter of the scatter includes kz.
    • Thread determinism. Results are identical at any thread count: per-thread wave_imaging, wave_multislice, work buffers and FFT plans in arrays sized by omp_get_max_threads() before each parallel region; FFTW plans made and destroyed in the unnamed critical section (the one lock of every FFTW planner call in SIMPLE); scatters through the coloured h-strided pass with the serial rim pass; losses and dot products in double precision per particle, reduced in particle order; multislice gradient passes in chunks of one particle per thread, each chunk's cubes scattered in particle order. The projector refuses to run inside a caller's parallel region, and the lattice's gather opens no nested team inside the particle loop.
    • Single precision fields, double precision sums.
    • Lattice accuracy. The three-tap window interpolates a section to 0.7 to 1% of the image contrast (1.3 to 1.8% of a gradient); seven taps reach 4e-5 to 9e-4. Both lie far below a particle's noise; parity with specter is bounded by them.

    9. Known limits¶

    • The prior near the Ewald limit. The ML prior is built from the flat start pair's FSC, which collapses exactly in the band where the flat model fails, so the prior is strongest there. On the run's Pt nanoparticle the wave maps repair only part of that band (truth FSC 0.07 to 0.20 between 1.12 and 1.04 A against 0.40 to 0.63 for specter's converged Rytov); a ten times weaker prior (tau=10) recovers most of it. A prior from a weakly regularised first wave solve is the candidate; the program keeps the plan's prior until the maintainer decides.
    • Strong scatterers filling the box. The native-disk design drops the content specter's native square holds beyond Nyquist; for a box-filling Pt particle under Rytov (2.6 rad) this moved images by 1% against specter.
    • Cost. One gradient pass at 24 threads costs about 0.65 ms per particle at box 128 and 3.6 to 4.3 ms at box 256 for the single-scatter models, about 120 ms and 1 s for multislice (the star_wave run, on a 24-core Core Ultra 9); multislice suits nanoparticle boxes and small sets. Of a pass, the gather and the scatter of the lattice take about a fifth and a quarter of the wall time; the scatter is limited by memory traffic. In reconstruct3D_wave on a 4000-particle box-320 set the operator's passes are a fifth of the run; the preparation of each particle at every pass (read_rec_obs_batch, a serial loop) costs as much.
    • Scale of real data. Dose-weighted sums do not have Poisson background noise, and background-based normalisation sees delocalised signal; the fitted gain shows by how much the gain from the dose is off.

    10. What waits for integration¶

    rec_backend=wave in reconstruct3D and refine3D through the reconstruction service; distributed execution (workers publishing gradient and curvature sums, the master stepping); the nonuniform (NU) filter, trailing chains, state weight sets and fractional updates; Ewald correction in the PCG and gridding backends; CTF and pose refinement; tomography. The objective's particle pass is the piece a worker would run, and nlcg_solve needs only gradient sums and subset curvatures.

    11. Simulation¶

    Both simulators run wave_projector on the particles' orientations, shifts and CTF parameters, turn each predicted plane into the relative intensity of the native box with plane_to_image, and detect it with detect_intensity (Poisson counts at the dose, then Gaussian detector noise, kept nonnegative).

    • simulate_scattering takes a PDB model (converted with atoms%pdb2mrc) or a potential in volts, writes relative intensities with its own keys total_dose and noise_sdev, and is the route from atoms.
    • simulate_particles with wave_model takes an ordinary SIMPLE map per state, masked as in its Fourier-projection mode, and divides it by the scale link of total_dose; one operator per state predicts the particles in batches in the output order, each with its lens B-factor (bfac, bfacerr). simimg_wave adds Gaussian noise of standard deviation sqrt(var(E) / snr) for snr below 5 (the rule of add_gauran, E the noiseless counts) and writes SIMPLE's particles, x = -(counts - D a^2) / sqrt(D a^2). Without wave_model the program is the Fourier-projection simulator, unchanged byte for byte; that mode's noiseless particle is half the wave mode's weak limit (it normalises the projection over the twice-padded box and does not deapodise), a scale it never relies on.
    • Scale. A wave-mode stack reconstructed by reconstruct3D (PCG) returns its map's scale to within the second-order terms at low and middle resolution when the particles' background is free of signal; delocalised signal in the background normalisation lowers it (0.84 of the scale for a 64 A box at 1.5 micrometres of defocus).
