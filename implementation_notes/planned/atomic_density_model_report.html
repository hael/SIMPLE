    The atomic density model: report of the unattended run¶

    Report of the run "atomic_density_model" on dell, 2026-10-25, for the maintainer's review of the complete raw diff. It reads on its own; the plan, doc/implementation_notes/planned/atomic_density_model.md, is the contract, and its Progress section (section 12) holds every number and log path. Each phase's diff is in /home/elmlundho/agent_runs/atomic_density_model/review/phase_N.diff (N = 0 to 4). The tables, scripts and logs of the validation are in /home/elmlundho/agent_runs/atomic_density_model/scratch/keep/validation/ (and the earlier phases' in scratch/keep/baseline, phase2, phase3); this report does not reproduce them.

    Terms. SINGLE is SIMPLE's nanoparticle workflow; detect_atoms finds the atoms of a 3D map. The density model (plan section 2) renders every atom as a Gaussian of its own integrated intensity I and width B, seen through the reconstruction's halo: the transfer 1 - a exp(-B_h s^2/4), two numbers per map. Profiles describe how ln I and ln B change with an atom's number of neighbours (coordination, 12 when fully surrounded): beta per neighbour for the intensity, gamma for the width, B_p the width of a fully coordinated atom. Strong atoms are those the first detection finds (level 1); weak atoms are those the residual recovery adds. d_NN is the nearest-neighbour distance. The residual over noise is the root-mean-square of map minus model inside the particle over the noise standard deviation (1 would be a perfect fit).

    What changed, phase by phase¶

    Phase 0, the baseline (review/phase_0.diff: the plan's Progress entry only). The base build at ca33f927d, its tests, and detect_atoms on the 66 maps without an element and with element=Pt, plus the mask-factor scan. Today's detection reproduces the stored coordinates on 35 and 33 maps, not the species validation's 47 and 56. One earlier commit explains the whole change: b57538b7b made the size rule of discard_atoms act, and it now removes about five surface atoms per map. One map's result depends on the thread count, so every later run used nthr=24.

    Phase 1, the model as a module (review/phase_1.diff). New simple_nano_density.f90 with its tester (the density sub-suite of unit_single): the kernel and the atom seen through the halo, rendering, the per-atom fit with measurement variances, the position update, the profile regressions, the transfer search, the noise estimate and the background. atoms%convolve gained the halo term. gauss_filter3D and robust_spread moved there from the species module. Particles rendered with known parameters at the real maps' noise come back within every floor.

    Phase 2, the fit on the present path (review/phase_2.diff). fit_density_model runs on every detect_atoms after discard_atoms, and writes the products of plan section 5 (_SIM, _FIT, _RES, _atoms.csv, _model.txt; _ATMS.pdb with occupancy I/S and B column B_i). The strong atoms and the binary products are identical to phase 0's on all 66 maps. The model beats the equal-atom render on every map. The phase stopped at the fade item: the particle's edge was not reproduced. The first ruling accepted that as a finding and moved the item to phase 3.

    Phase 3, the weak atoms (review/phase_3.diff). The species branch's residual recovery was moved onto the present path, between the provisional and the full fit: a calibrated threshold, two stages, pruning by contacts with strong atoms only. Labels go in every product (_ATMS.pdb chain B, _atoms.csv). discover_species was reduced to the class call on the fitted intensities. Map correlations (third ruling) and residual peak and well counts (second ruling) go in _model.txt, positions in _resid_extrema.csv. species_discovery keeps every floor, and the pure synthetic particle gets no weak atom.

    Phase 4, the real maps and this report (review/phase_4.diff). The validation of section 6 on the 66 maps and on FePt, and the detection-gap table. The second detection pass with the fitted kernel was written and measured, then removed (below), so phase 4 leaves no code change: simple_nanoparticle.f90 is in its phase-3 state and simple_nanoparticle_utils.f90 is at HEAD. The diff of the phase is documents only: the two species notes' pointer to the moved recovery, the plan's Progress, and this report.

    Decisions taken under delegation, and where they are recorded¶

    Every decision is recorded with its measurement in the phase's journal (state/journal_phase_N.md) and in the plan's Progress entry for the phase (section 12). The ones that shape the result:

    • nthr=24 on every run (phase 0, then the first ruling). nthr is a required key, and one map's atoms depend on the thread count through a near tie in the binarisation threshold.
    • The transfer is fitted between the atoms, without the background (phase 1). Over the atom cores, a and the intensities trade off, and the plan's two fits stop at 0.54 for a true 0.6. A background smoothed at d_NN absorbs the halos' flat plateau, so with it any a fits.
    • Strong atoms for the profiles are those above ten times the fixed-width intensity noise (phase 1), as the species branch counted. Against the joint-fit variance, no atom of the correlated-noise particle qualified.
    • The no-halo check uses the species measurement's estimator (phase 1), chosen after the production variant gave 2.13 Å² against the floor's 2.2. Both numbers are in Progress for that reason.
    • The products (phase 2). _ATMS.pdb keeps the detection positions, so they compare with phase 0, and the fitted positions are in _atoms.csv. _SIM is the module's render, which makes single_atoms_stats' density correlation 0.994 instead of 0.9997 on its Pt particle. detect_atoms stops when level 1 finds fewer than two atoms.
    • The moved recovery (phase 3). It runs between the provisional and the full fit, calibrated on the map's own empty region or the residual between the atoms; half maps no longer feed it. Distances are measured from the provisionally fitted positions. Weak atoms are pruned by contacts with strong atoms only.
    • One species_discovery control changed form (phase 3). "With element=Pt the present path finds fewer than 0.90 of the aluminium" assumed a path without recovery. It now counts the strong atoms of that run, the same quantity as before. Its floor and name are unchanged.
    • The third ruling's "fraction below the pruning floor" (phase 3). The present code has no valid_corr floor, so the fractions below 0.5 and 0.7 are reported instead.
    • The second detection pass is removed (phase 4). By the second ruling's real-map measures it helps. It fails the synthetic floors, and no floor is relaxed. Recorded in the phase-4 journal and Progress.
    • The mask factor stays at 1.2 (phase 4). 1.3 is better by the same measures but by little, and the constant also sets the element path's mask, which was not measured. Left as a recommendation.
    • How the phase-4 table was measured. Through two temporary environment switches in one build, both removed; four maps at a time, each at nthr=24, so its wall times are not single-run timings.

    Deviations from the plan, and why¶

    • Phase 0's ten short maps became 31. The plan's ten came from older code. Today's code is short on 31 maps without an element, and the scan ran on all 31. The hand-set mskdiam is rounded to an even pixel count, so the hand factors are effective 0.97-1.34. Phase 4's table set the factor exactly.
    • The 47 and 56 of the species validation were not reproduced. Commit b57538b7b (the size rule of discard_atoms, 2026-10-15) explains the whole difference: two bisect builds, and today's atoms are an exact subset of the older ones. The second ruling then made the stored coordinates a reference only.
    • Section 4's text was changed in three places in phase 1 (the transfer's residual region, the background left out of it, the definition of strong atoms), each because a measurement showed the text's version missing the floors. See the decisions above.
    • The density sub-suite takes 1.9 s on one thread, more than the test policy's "well under a second" per sub-suite. The plan puts two particle fits in it, and they are the cost. The fast gate's wall time is unchanged.
    • Phase 2 stopped at its fade item. The first ruling accepted the stop and moved the item to phase 3. It is still not met at the particle's edge (below).
    • The ruling-2 yardsticks replaced the matched fraction from phase 3 on, including section 6 item 3's "the matched fraction per map decides".
    • FePt's beta is not compared with their 5.2 percent per neighbour. The species-free detection finds only 60 percent of their platinum and none of their iron, so the profile comes from an incomplete, single-element set.
    • Phase 4 has no code change. The plan foresaw the second pass as one "if the table says it helps". On the real maps it did; on the synthetic particles it failed.

    The evidence against the baseline¶

    The tables are in scratch/keep/baseline/ (phase 0), phase2/, phase3/ and validation/ (phase 4); each has a README or its script list, and the Progress entries carry the numbers.

    • Level 1 is unchanged. The strong atoms equal phase 0's in count and coordinates on all 66 maps, and _BIN, _CC and _MSK are byte-identical. Every earlier difference from the species validation's numbers is the size-rule commit named above.
    • The model fits every map better than today's render. The residual over noise inside the envelope has a median of 1.54 (1.13 to 2.96), against 2.11 for the equal-atom render at 13.9 Å² and 1.92 at the best single width. It is below the plan's 1.3 on 7 maps. Of the map correlations (third ruling), _FIT gives 0.783 against 0.681 for the identical atoms, and the per-atom widths help in every resolution band.
    • The interior is reproduced, the edge is not. The radial profile follows the map in every shell inside the particle on 35 maps (30 before the weak atoms). At the edge no map passes. There the map lies above the model (median of the largest deviations 14 standard errors), and 4679 of the 5041 residual peaks above the threshold lie in that band: atoms still missing.
    • The weak atoms are real-looking, and the pruning removes the rest. Synthetic particles of one species get no weak atom against an expected false count of 4.6 to 6.1. The real maps get 1379, a median of 11 per map, almost all one bond from a stored atom, weaker (occupancy 0.57 against 0.89) and as sharp (36 against 37 Å²). 287 of the 305 surface atoms the size rule removes sit on a residual peak, but the strong-only contact pruning removes them again; 11 survive.
    • The wells between the atoms are too shallow in the model. The mean level there is right (within 0.25 noise units on 58 of 66 maps), but where the model has its wells the map is a median 1.26 times deeper (58 of 64 maps).
    • Across time points the atoms agree and the parameters do not. Consecutive time points share 0.953 of their atoms, and ln I of matched atoms correlates beyond noise on 41 of 51 pairs. beta, a and B_h vary within a particle by about their own size; only B_p holds (range 6.5 against 37.8 Å²).
    • The detection-gap table. The second pass lowers the residual peaks and wells by a median of 10.5 per map and the residual by 0.018, and fails species_discovery's synthetic floors (beta, width rise, B_h, false weak atoms). The mask at 1.3 lowers them by 3 at the same residual.
    • FePt. The model fits a map from outside SINGLE without an empty region: residual-based noise, the transfer fitted, 55 weak atoms, all on their platinum and none on their 618 removed peaks. The species-free detection finds 9909 of their 16 627 platinum atoms and none of their 6569 iron, missing about 30 percent at every radius.
    • Tests at the end. Fast gate 15 of 15; the five CTest entries pass with species_discovery's floors as in phase 0 (its metrics listed beside phase 0's in scratch/keep/phase3/species_discovery_beside_phase0.tsv); unit_single passes in Release and in Debug with bounds checking; products are identical at 11 and 24 threads.
    • Time. detect_atoms takes a median 6.1 s per map at 24 threads (1.1 s in phase 0), the fit and the recovery being largely serial.

    What is left open for the maintainer¶

    • The size rule and the pruning of weak atoms. The recovery finds 287 of the 305 surface atoms the size rule removes, and the strong-only contact score prunes them again. Counting weak neighbours, or letting the residual peak vouch for a surface atom, would change that. It is the first ruling's open decision.
    • The edge. Atoms are still missing there (4679 residual peaks), and the outside band is negative: halos the model does not match.
    • The transfer. a reaches its cap of 0.99 on 15 maps, B_h ranges 21 to 682 Å² and varies within a particle by its own size, and the model's wells are a quarter too shallow. The phase-1 probe found B_h and the intensity scale on a flat valley. A transfer shared by the time points of a particle, or fitted with the edge's atoms in, are the obvious next measurements.
    • The measured spreads (tau_B, the intensity spreads) come out zero on many maps, because the correlated noise inflates the measurement variances beyond the observed scatter.
    • The mask factor. 1.3 is recommended for the species-free path. Measure it with element=Pt before changing MSKDIAM_OVER_DIAM.
    • A second pass that keeps level 1's atoms. The tested pass replaced level 1 and merged neighbours. A variant that uses the fitted kernel only to propose candidates for the recovery was not tried.
    • The neighbour gate (min_nbrs = 3, plan section 9). It admitted 12 percent of the weak atoms and no false atom on the synthetic particles. It is left on.
    • image%win2arr_rad returns one pixel too many (src/main/image/simple_image_geom.f90). Every valid_corr includes one leftover pixel of the previous window. It was found in phase 3 and not changed, being outside the run's files.

    The second run: the measured transfer, a species-free detect_atoms, detect_atomic_species¶

    Report of the run "atomic_density_model_2" on dell, 2026-10-26 to 2026-10-27, phases 5 to 9 of the plan (its section 12). It reads on its own. The plan's Progress (section 13) holds every number and log path, and the maintainer's rulings 4 to 7 are recorded in section 12, each where it applies. The tables are under /home/elmlundho/agent_runs/atomic_density_model_2/scratch/keep/: validation/ holds the phase-5 baseline, and its README_second_run.txt and ../README.txt name the later phases' tables beside it. The diffs are in /home/elmlundho/agent_runs/atomic_density_model_2/review/.

    Terms. The transfer T(s) is what the reconstruction does to an atom at each spatial frequency s. The first run modelled it as a notch with two numbers; this run measures it from each map, shell by shell, as the projection of the map onto its fitted atoms rendered without any transfer. A round is one measurement of that curve followed by a full fit of the atoms with the curve held fixed. The gauge is the convention that fixes what a map alone cannot: the curve's scale and its Gaussian tilt are set to unity over 3.1-2.0 Å, so the atoms' absolute brightness S and width B_p are numbers in that gauge. A class call sorts the atoms into intensity classes (candidate species). A posterior is an atom's probability of belonging to a class. The forced two-class fit is the call with a two-element list; admissible means its classes are separated by at least 3 noise widths (D ≥ 3) and each holds enough atoms.

    Deviations from the plan, and why¶

    • Phase 6 is not "measured once and fixed". The plan's single measurement left the synthetic particles' scale and slope outside their floors. The rulings replaced it with rounds, each holding the curve fixed during its fit.
    • Phase 7's "the 66 maps equal phase 6" did not hold on 14 maps. The data-derived ceiling applies to every run, not only to runs that gave an element. On all 14 maps the residual is lower, and they have fewer residual peaks.
    • Phase 8's coordination spread is not the one detect_atoms writes. The reason is above; the sixth ruling confirmed it.
    • Phase 8's element check became "no confident call is wrong" (the sixth ruling), and the softness check's floor became the softness gate times the class gap.
    • The species work's planted-class runs were not repeated. Phase 9's scope is the species call on every map; the unfinished note says so.

    What is left open for the maintainer¶

    • The curve oscillates on many real maps. The rounds reach their limit on 31 of 66 maps and on FePt, and the guard keeps an earlier round on 40, so a measurement from the fitted model is not a fixed point on many real maps. A damped update, a curve shared by a particle's time points, or the product of the first two rounds would be the next things to measure; the plan's per-map rule rules out the second without a new ruling.
    • EFC2_time10s keeps the profiles of its provisional fit. After the first measurement its atoms are about five intensity-noise units bright, below the strong-atom threshold of ten.
    • The posterior's standard error is linearised. It exceeds 1 on two forced fits (ESC5_time08s 1.08, FCHCS1_time06s 1.05). That happens where the class gap is several noise widths and the posterior is near one half. A bounded form (in the log-odds) would read better.
    • An inadmissible forced fit still writes its labels. On FePt, element=Pt,Fe names 8730 platinum atoms iron, 3404 of them with a posterior of at least 0.9, while _species.txt says the fit is inadmissible. Whether the program should refuse to label, or fall back to one class, is a product decision.
    • FePt's iron is not detected. Species-free detection finds 60 % of Miao's platinum and none of his iron, so species on FePt is first a detection question.
    • S and B_p are gauge-dependent. B_p ranges from 1.1 to 93 Å² across the maps. B by coordination now falls from about 39 Å² at 4-5 neighbours to 31.9 Å² at 12, where phase 5 was flat.
    • The species work's planted-class validation (the open note's expectations 2 to 4) has not been run with the new program.
    • The first run's open items that this run did not touch:
    • the size rule and the strong-only pruning of weak surface atoms;
    • the missing atoms at the particle's edge;
    • the mask factor.
