    The nanoparticle pipeline on every platinum trajectory: report¶

    The unattended run nano_pipeline_pt on the Dell, 2026-10-29. The plan, with every detail and number, is nano_pipeline_pt.md beside this report (its section 8, Progress, has one entry per phase). Run files are under /home/elmlundho/agent_runs/nano_pipeline_pt/ (below, "the run directory").

    1. What happened, in short¶

    All ten platinum trajectories in /data2/NanoX_trajs went through the present SINGLE pipeline and have a time-integrated map. No trajectory had to be stopped. The ten are six of dataset 71-17 (12,971 frames each) and four of 71-6 (13,662 frames each).

    The pipeline is the chain of SINGLE programs from tracked frames to a map: - trajectory_denoise, with its new default pca_mode=flex, denoises each frame from the frames that look like it. - refine2D_nano groups the frames into class averages. Its time-series search keeps neighbouring frames together. - model_cavgs_rejection quality_model=nano prune=yes rejects the class averages without a crystal lattice and removes their frames. - center2D_nano centres the remaining frames. - autorefine3D_nano refines the 3D map in five stages, with the species-free detect_atoms building an atomic density model after each stage. It runs without the atomicity prior and with icm=no, as ruled.

    Two bugs were found, both on the first trajectory, and fixed in the code. The other nine trajectories ran without intervention through a resumable driver script. The whole run took 4.5 hours of compute at 24 threads.

    What the run did not do. It did not judge the maps. Ruling 1 reserves that for the maintainer. No map was compared with the older maps in /data/NanoX/zenodo, and the numbers below are what the programs printed, not quality verdicts.

    2. What changed in the repository, phase by phase¶

    The diff of each phase is in the run directory as review/phase_N.diff.

    • Phase 0 (review/phase_0.diff). Both fixes (section 3), the new check in the SINGLE workflow test, and the phase 0 Progress entry. Four files changed:
    • src/main/strategies/parallelization/simple_make_cavgs_strategy.f90 (one line and its comment);
    • src/main/commanders/single/single_commanders_nano3D.f90 (the transfer table in the copy and delete lists);
    • src/main/commanders/test/simple_commanders_test_single.f90 (one assertion);
    • the plan.
    • Phase 1 (review/phase_1.diff) and Phase 2 (review/phase_2.diff). Progress entries only. Eight trajectories ran in these phases, none failed, and nothing needed fixing.
    • Phase 3 (review/phase_3.diff, written by the driver at the end). This report and the phase 3 Progress entry.

    No file-table row was added, no test entry was added or registered, no threshold, floor or default was changed, and no file mode changed.

    3. The fixes¶

    Centering after the rejection kept failing on the even/odd halves. Exposed by 71-17/traj01 at center2D_nano. - Symptom. The run stopped with "particle 4893 has different halves in ptcl2D and ptcl3D". SIMPLE splits the frames into two halves, even and odd, so that each half can be reconstructed independently. Both particle fields of the project (ptcl2D for 2D, ptcl3D for 3D) must agree on that split. - Cause. With tseries=yes, the class-centre step of the chunk averaging (make_cavgs) re-split ptcl2D by row parity every time it ran. After the rejection has removed frames, rows are no longer frames, so from the first removed frame on the new split disagreed with the frames' own halves and with ptcl3D. - Fix. Split only when no halves exist yet, the rule the same module already applies in setup_evenodd. Unpruned projects, including every analysis2D_nano run, are split exactly as before. - Check. No tester exists for this module, and the SINGLE workflow test never prunes before centering. The check was therefore the rerun on the real trajectory that exposed the bug: it failed before the fix (log in pipeline/71-17/traj01/failed/6_center2D_nano_eo_bug/) and passed after it, with no frame whose halves differ. - Trajectories changed. Only 71-17/traj01, which was at that stage; every later trajectory ran with the fix.

    autorefine3D_nano threw away the transfer table of the atom detection. Exposed by 71-17/traj01 in 3D. - Symptom. Each round of detect_atoms writes _transfer.tsv, the map's measured transfer per Fourier shell. The detection program's own UI text names it as a product, and the plan lists it among the final results. The file was missing from every iteration folder and from final_results/. - Cause. The copy and delete lists of autorefine3D_nano predate the table, and its final clean-up deleted the file. - Fix. The table is added to the copy and delete lists, including the copies made after an atomicity-prior stage. - Check. A new assertion in the SINGLE workflow test requires the table in final_results/. Both single_workflow_fcc (1091 s; this suite also runs the atomicity-prior path) and single_workflow_wurtzite (722 s) pass (scratch/ctest_single_workflow.log). - Trajectories changed. 71-17/traj01 was rerun from the 3D stage. Its pre-fix text products are kept in pipeline/71-17/traj01/failed/9_autorefine3D_nano_before_transfer_fix/. All ten trajectories now have the table in final_results/ and in every iteration folder.

    4. Evidence against the baseline¶

    The baseline is the maintainer's HEAD 585ce2192 (Release build with tests, gfortran 15.2.1 from gcc-toolset-15, system FFTW). - Fast gate. 15 of 15 before any change (scratch/build_clean.log) and after each fix. - Workflow tests. The two SINGLE workflow tests pass with the new assertion. - Fix 1. The failing real-data stage fails before the fix and passes after it. - Repository checks. check_test_registry.py and check_descr.py pass. - Run-to-run variation. The 3D refinement does not repeat exactly. 71-17/traj01 ended with 303 strong and 82 weak atoms before fix 2, and with 364 strong and 31 weak atoms after it. Fix 2 copies files only, so the difference comes from the refinement itself; the second run also used 22 threads instead of 24. - Denoiser. Regenerating a denoised stack gives a bit-identical file.

    5. The numbers over the ten trajectories¶

    Median, with the range in brackets. The full table is pipeline/table.tsv and these figures are in pipeline/distributions.txt, both in the run directory's scratch/keep/.

    • Wall time per stage (seconds, 24 threads):
    • denoise 80 (49–104);
    • the three short stages before the 2D 7 (4–9);
    • refine2D_nano 118 (82–171);
    • rejection 1 (0–1);
    • center2D_nano 71 (13–126);
    • autorefine3D_nano 1370 (456–2254);
    • the whole chain 28 minutes (10–42).

    The 3D is 74 to 90 percent of every trajectory's wall time. The longest 3D runs are on the 160- and 200-pixel boxes with many kept frames. - Denoising. Every trajectory got the largest rank offered (32) and tau 8. The temporal bandwidth was 8 frames, except on three 71-6 trajectories where it was 16. z, the denoiser's gain over the parity mean, is 91 (28–121): 87–121 on 71-17 and 28–43 on 71-6. - 2D. The fraction of consecutive frames that share a class is 0.75 (0.65–0.86). This is the time-chunk behaviour tseries is meant to keep; before the fix of 2026-10-28 it was 0.5 percent. - Rejection. - Kept fraction of frames: 0.86 (0.32–0.99). On 71-17 it is 0.74–0.99, on 71-6 0.32–0.86. - Classes accepted: 528 (212–604) of 649 or 684. - The hard gates reject 31 (6–100) classes. Where checked, every one is a class that refine2D_nano left empty. - Diameters (Å). - First estimate, on unaligned chunk averages: minimum 23.4 (16.2–29.2), maximum 28.5 (23.9–38.5). - Second estimate, on centred averages: minimum 20.9 (17.0–27.0), maximum 24.6 (23.0–32.8). - Masks: 2D 42.8 (35.8–57.8), 3D 37.0 (34.5–49.2). - The rejection's median particle diameter: 24 (22–32). - 3D. - Resolution at FSC 0.143 between the final half-maps (FSC: Fourier shell correlation): 1.28 Å (1.10–1.65). - Atoms: 273 strong (172–553) and 16 weak (3–76). - Residual over noise 1.39 (1.11–1.94); corr_fit 0.79 (0.75–0.86). - Transfer rounds 6 (2–6).

    6. Where the products are¶

    Everything is under the run directory's scratch/keep/pipeline/, one folder per trajectory, with README.txt describing every file and table column. One trajectory's map is <dataset>/<trajectory>/<trajectory>/9_autorefine3D_nano/final_results/recvol_state01_iter005.mrc. Beside it are the half-maps, the atomic model (_ATMS.pdb, _atoms.csv) and the density model (_SIM, _FIT, _RES, _model.txt, _transfer.tsv). The accepted and rejected class averages for review are in 5_model_cavgs_rejection/ (quality_selected_cavgs.mrc, quality_rejected_cavgs.mrc). Every stage keeps its log, its wall time and its project. The ten trajectories occupy 5.7 GB.

    7. Decisions taken under delegation¶

    Each is recorded in the plan's Progress entry of the phase named. - Run order (phase 0). Frame count, then box, then name. - 2D input (phase 0). refine2D_nano reads the import's project, not the chunk-average project, whose ten-class assignment serves only the first diameter estimate. - Stage layout (phase 0). Each stage is SIMPLE's own numbered execution directory. estimate_diam and the denoiser get directories of their own, because they write into the working directory. - Montages (phase 0). They are the rejection program's own quality_selected_cavgs.mrc and quality_rejected_cavgs.mrc, so no stackops or selection call was needed. - Parallelism (phase 0). One trajectory at a time at 24 threads. The 3D keeps about 20 cores busy and dominates the wall time. - Keep policy (phase 0). Keeping every denoised stack would have left about 19.5 GB free, below the plan's 20 GB. So the stacks of the seven trajectories with box 160 or 200 are not kept, and the driver regenerates them bit-identically if a stage must be rerun. The three 120-pixel stacks are kept. Every stage's project is kept so that a rerun can start anywhere. The table column stack_kept records this per trajectory. - Disk (phase 1). When /home fell to 24 GB free during the 200-pixel trajectory, the 5.1 GB of binary outputs of the two workflow tests, which had already passed, were deleted and their logs kept.

    8. Deviations from the plan, and why¶

    Each deviation is the code deciding where the plan's command was refused, as the plan's section 2 provides. All are recorded in the phase 0 Progress entry. - new_project belongs to simple_exec, and estimate_diam to single_exec. - make_cavgs needs nparts=1. - center2D_nano needs ncls=10: its UI makes ncls required, so the commander's default of ten cannot be reached from the command line. - autorefine3D_nano needs lp: its UI makes it required, with a documented default of 1.5 Å, which was used.

    The FSC for the table is computed with simple_exec prg=fsc on the final halves, as the phase notes direct.

    9. Left open for the maintainer¶

    • The maps themselves. Their validation is the maintainer's, as ruled.
    • The 2D mask rule on small boxes. On 71-6/traj03 and traj04, 1.5 times the largest diameter of the first estimate exceeds the 120-pixel box (43.8 and 43.3 Å against 43.0 Å). The parameter layer then warns and uses the box's default mask, 40.8 Å, as designed. The first estimate is made on unaligned chunk averages and is larger than the centred one on every trajectory (the 3D mask is 2 to 21 percent smaller than the 2D mask). Whether the 2D mask should come from a centred estimate is a choice for the maintainer.
    • Empty classes. refine2D_nano leaves 6 to 100 classes empty after its 15 iterations; the rejection's hard gates remove them.
    • Large 2D shifts. The shifts refine2D_nano gives the frames, from class centering repeated over its iterations, reach 22 to 83 pixels on some frames, against medians of 3.5 to 8.9. The largest sit on frames with little particle signal.
    • Transfer rounds. The final atom detection reached its cap on transfer rounds on five of the ten trajectories (transfer_rounds_capped = yes in _model.txt).
    • 71-6 behaves differently from 71-17. It denoises with a smaller gain and keeps fewer frames (0.32–0.86 against 0.74–0.99). Its trajectory traj03 kept the fewest frames (4,345) and has the coarsest half-map resolution (1.65 Å).
    • Repeatability of the 3D. The 3D refinement is not repeatable run to run (section 4), so two runs of one trajectory differ in their atom counts.
    • The UI requirements. The required ncls of center2D_nano and lp of autorefine3D_nano were met, not changed. Whether those UI requirements are intended is the maintainer's call.
