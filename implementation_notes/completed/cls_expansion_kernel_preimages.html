    cls_expansion: sub-class averages as kernel pre-images¶

    simple_exec prg=cls_expansion projfile=... ncls=<total subclasses> mskdiam=... nparts=10 nthr=8

    Status (2026-10-14): implemented first as an optional route (subcls_avg=kernel) beside the model's conditional averages (flex_cls_expansion_2026_10_01.md); later the same day the conditional averages were removed (placement, flex_cls_place_states, flex_cls_conditional_averages, the half models) together with subcls_avg, and the kernel pre-images are cls_expansion's only sub-class averages. Section 1 describes the removed route as the motivation. Unit-tested, checked end to end (shared memory and two parts) on the three-state phantom of the flex_pca_blobs gate. Revised the same day after the first real-data run: the first version kept a class whole unless the CV gain was significant and capped the representatives by minpop and noise overlap, and delivered 54 of 500 budgeted averages. The route is a generative model (a pre-image per member), so it now fills the budget with the pre-images of that many members (section 2, steps 4-5; section 7).

    1. Why¶

    Since 2026-10-12 the sub-class averages are the model's conditional averages, P_h + U_ext,h (I - S_h)^-1 (zbar_s,h - zbar_h) per subclass s and half h. solve3D_cavgs and refine3D_states_cavgs read each of them as two independent rows (even, odd), plan their low-pass ladder from the registered frc2D, and build state maps from the rows. That breaks three of their assumptions:

    1. Shared noise. Every sibling carries the same half parent P_h, i.e. the same noise, plus a combination of the same neigs noisy basis columns. Siblings at similar poses add the same noise in the same place: the 3D solve sees structure that no particle subset holds. A class with fewer than 2 * 3 (neigs + 1) members has no half models at all and uses the joint deviations in both halves: there the even-minus-odd images of all siblings are identical (section 5).
    2. Borrowed resolution. The even/odd FRC of P_e + D_e against P_o + D_o is essentially the parent's (the deviation is small next to the mean). The ladder and every resolution claim of a sub-average, and of state maps built from siblings, are the parent's.
    3. Exaggeration. The members are selected on their posterior means m; by the tower property E[z | m in cell] = E[m | m in cell], so the deshrink (I - S)^-1 inflates every deviation, and P + U z at an inflated z extrapolates a variation that need not be linear into an image no object projects to.

    Plain hard averages are no fix: the latent is a function of a member's own noise along span(U) in the fit band, so selecting on it averages selected noise ("Einstein from noise").

    The argument for hard assignment over soft weights (with many subclasses the soft averages converge to each other) holds for responsibilities normalised across components. A kernel weight is a responsibility without that denominator: it does not shrink when representatives are added. That decouples the weight distribution of a particle from the clustering.

    2. Method¶

    Per 2D class with at least two subclasses in the budget, after the class model of the default route is fitted (bands, nuisance columns and all; flex_cls_expansion_2026_10_01.md section 1, steps 1-2):

    1. Coefficient split. The global fit band is split into two interleaved sets of 4x4 Fourier blocks in a checkerboard. Blocks, not single coefficients: the class-frame soft mask correlates direct neighbours at about 0.2, which would leak the selection noise into the validation. A model is fitted on each set (flex_cls_fit, the class's nuisance columns, rank neigs, lower when a set holds fewer than 3 (neigs + 4) coefficients; a band too narrow for any rank keeps the class whole).
    2. ML latents. Every member's maximum-likelihood latent zhat_i under each of the three models, without the latent prior and with the nuisance profiled out, and its covariance L_i^-1, the latent block of the inverse of the joint normal matrix.
    3. Directions. Per model, the generalised eigenvectors of the members' latent scatter against their mean ML covariance, normalised so that the mean covariance is the identity (noise-whitened), ordered by scatter over noise. The in-sample fit makes every direction look like signal in the members' own scatter (a rank-16 basis fitted to noise sits at the top of the Marchenko-Pastur spectrum of the coefficients, far above the edge for 16 latent dimensions), so the rank is not read from these eigenvalues but chosen by the cross-validation.
    4. Kernel and cross-validation. A member at location c weighs w_j(c) = N(zhat_j; c, L_j^-1 + tau^2 I), its likelihood of being there, in the leading r directions; an uninformative member weighs little anywhere. For r in {1, 2, 4, 8, 16} (up to the rank of the set models) and tau in {0, 0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, 4, 8, inf} (noise units; inf is the class mean) the members selected on set A's latents predict every validation member's set-B coefficients, leaving the member out, as the CTF-Wiener kernel mean sum_j w_j c_j W_j y_j / (sum_j w_j c_j^2 W_j + eps), and vice versa (the target's own latent noise enters as the identity, the class's mean covariance). The validation noise is independent of the selection, so the whitened error per held-out coefficient is 1 plus the pre-image's error. The rank r with the smallest error at a finite bandwidth is kept, with the CV optimum tau* there (inf included) and the best finite bandwidth tau_g (tau* itself when finite). The paired per-member gain of tau_g over tau = inf gives a z-score, reported (log, sep) and not acted on: below about 3 the class shows no reproducible variation at its SNR and its pre-images are near copies of its mean.
    5. Pre-images and representatives. On the full model's latents in its leading r directions. Member i's pre-image is the kernel mean of the members with weights w_ij = N(zhat_j; zhat_i, L_j^-1 + C + tau_g^2 I), C the members' mean covariance (the latent uncertainty of the centre, as in the CV): the generative model, one image per member, at the bandwidth the CV chose (preimage). The representatives are the pre-images of nsub members, the class's budget (select). Candidates are the informative members (an even stride of at most 1000); they are taken greedily by coverage: each step the candidate that adds most to sum_j max_r L_rj, L_rj the members' likelihoods under the candidate's kernel (a submodular facility-location objective: the densest region first, then what the accepted ones leave uncovered, so a second state comes before the gap between two and a near copy of an accepted pre-image comes last), first among the candidates whose pre-image holds a Kish effective population (sum w)^2 / sum w^2 of at least minpop, then among the others. The budget is delivered whenever the class has that many members. Kept whole: a class with a budget of one, a fit band too narrow for the coefficient split, or latents that give no CV (too few informative members, a covariance not positive definite).
    6. Averages. Each representative's full, even and odd average is the kernel-weighted, CTF-corrected mean of all, the even and the odd members with the same weights (average). On the fitted coefficients the latent is a function of the member's own noise, so the weighted mean is biased towards the representative there. Stein's identity removes it: for Gaussian noise E[w(zhat) e_k] = -c_k w (U_k . L^-1 g + N_k . S_nz g), g = W (L_r^-1 + C + tau_g^2 I)^-1 (zr - c), W the kernel's directions (zr = zhat W), S_nz the nuisance-latent block of the inverse; the sample version is added to the numerator. Above the fit band the noise never entered the latent and nothing is corrected. The full average is filtered by 2F/(1+F) of its own even/odd FRC, as on the default route.
    7. Labels and readouts. A member's hard label is the representative of its largest weight (the ptcl2D contract is unchanged). Per subclass: pop (hard members), neff (Kish), sep (the parent's CV z-score) and repro, the even/odd correlation of the deviations from the half parents over the fit band (the default route's is over every coefficient; beyond the band the deviation of a small subclass is noise).

    What the 3D programs get: ncls rows, FRCs of the representatives' own particles, deviations supported by the members, and noise shared between siblings only through the members both weigh. That sharing is not capped any more: a budget larger than the class's variation supports gives near copies (the largest overlap is logged per class; neff and the z-score say how much each row and class carry).

    5. Tests and checks¶

    Unit tests, simple_test_exec test=unit_heterogeneity suite=flex_cls_expansion_kernel (65 checks, about 2 s). Synthetic classes on the half-plane of a 32-pixel box, CTF-like factors per member and coefficient, complex Gaussian noise of unit power:

    • the coefficient sets are disjoint, cover the band, balanced, constant on a block;
    • noise-free members: the ML latent is the generating latent and its covariance the inverse information (closed form), also with a profiled nuisance column;
    • two clusters of latents, a budget of four: four representatives, each at a member's latent, the first two at the cluster centres, every member labelled with a representative of its cluster, every one holding minpop; the budget still filled when no pre-image reaches minpop; with one allowed, the denser cluster;
    • a homogeneous class weighted around an off-centre location: the plain weighted mean deviates along the latent direction by c cov / (2 cov + C + tau^2) (0.500 measured, 0.5 expected with C = 0; 0.334 and 1/3 with C = cov); the Stein-corrected one by -0.005 and -0.002;
    • a homogeneous class: no significant CV gain (z below 3), still four pre-images at a finite bandwidth; with a budget of one it is kept whole and delivers its CTF-corrected mean, also as every member's pre-image;
    • two states (60/40, rank-2 models, budget two): z at least 3, kernel rank 1, the pre-images at the CV optimum, labels at least 90% pure (the best possible is about 96%), the averages on either side of the class mean along the state direction;
    • the same two states with a budget of six: six pre-images of members, each with at least minpop effective members (120-260 of 400), both states represented, every representative's average equal to its member's pre-image.

    Over five further seeds all pass: the Stein residual stays within 0.045 (threshold 0.12), the homogeneous class's CV z-score at most 0.9 (threshold 3), purity 0.95-0.97.

    Three-state phantom, first version of the representatives (the z gate, the overlap cap and the minpop floor; not re-run after the revision) (flex_pca_blobs' states_cavgs fixture: 60 views, each an exact 2D class of 20 particles per state, SNR 1, box 64 at 2 A, one 4 A lobe differs between states; ncls=180 lp=12). Sibling noise: correlation of the even-minus-odd images of two sub-averages of one class inside the mask. Deviations: c (A_s - class mean) against the noise-free truth of the sub-average's members, below 21 A.

    6. Open¶

    • Real-data checks. On a class split by both routes: the correlation of the even-minus-odd images of two siblings inside the mask (near 0 expected here, high on the default route), and refine3D_states_cavgs / solve3D_cavgs on both outputs (fake states, resolution claims). solve3D_cavgs cannot run on the phantom (its ladder asks for a crop box of 88 at a native box of 64).
    • Redundant rows. A budget beyond what a class's variation supports now gives near copies with shared noise (logged overlap near 1). If refine3D_states_cavgs / solve3D_cavgs count them as independent evidence, a weight per row (its neff over the shared part) or an overlap-aware budget is the next step.
    • Calibration. The kernel and the Stein term take the ML covariances at face value; a sigma2 off by a factor scales them. The CV picks rank and bandwidth regardless; the Stein term would be off by the same factor.
    • Junk. The default placement uses the log fit residual as a junk axis; here junk members only lower their weights through their covariances. A residual coordinate in the kernel is a candidate.
    • Budget on capacity. The budget is allocated before the classes are seen; the delivered counts could feed a second allocation.
