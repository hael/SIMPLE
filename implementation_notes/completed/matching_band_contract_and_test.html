    The matching band: its contract, a broken clamp, and the test that was missing¶

    Implementation note, 2026-10-18; status updated 2026-10-20: done. The revert (section 2) is implemented (fb4e653ac). The FSC=0.5 promotion change (section 3: res05 gates the advance, the ladder sets its size, stage omission, mid-stage exit) is implemented (cd13708eb). The test (section 4) was dropped by ruling 1 of planned/testing_and_benchmarking_plan.md ("no matching-band test"); the stage shift is judged on real datasets. Two small optional extras are listed under "Not folded in" in section 3; nobody has committed to them, so no unfinished note exists. Written after a PfCRT regression at commit 25d25688 that was caused by a change made two days earlier to the matching low-pass limit, and found, as the two before it, by a maintainer looking at density rather than by a test.

    Three separate matters are kept apart below, because conflating them is what made the diagnosis take three attempts:

    1. who owns the matching band, and the contract that ownership implies;
    2. the clamp that violated it (closed: reverted);
    3. the FSC=0.5 promotion (closed: implemented as the res05 gate);
    4. the test that would have caught (2), and would bound (3) (dropped by ruling).

    1. Who owns the matching band in solve3D¶

    The band is params%kfromto(2). In solve3D it is owned entirely by the controller:

    • stage_matching_lp returns the static stage ladder lpinfo(istage)%lp (simple_solve3D_controller.f90:706);
    • promote_stage_lp_from_fsc05 may sharpen it at a stage boundary (:718, section 3);
    • the result is set on the refine3D command line unconditionally (:636).

    l_lpset is cline%defined('lp') (simple_parameters_phases.f90:597), and no route in the controller strips lp for a solve3D stage, so every solve3D stage reaches set_bp_range3D with lp defined.

    Not established: whether that makes the else branch's FSC-derived routes -- the NU handoff, lplim_crit, the project lp field -- unreachable in solve3D. An earlier revision of this note asserted it. The latest6 logs contradict it: eight >>> NU filter promoted matching low-pass to command line lines, a write reached only from inside that branch, against a single occurrence of the >>> NU filter matching low-pass limit line that encloses it. One process cannot produce that ratio, so distributed log aggregation is involved and the control flow was not traced further. Needs checking against a build.

    Nothing below depends on the stronger claim. The clamp of section 2 was wrong on the gold-standard grounds and on get_find_at_crit's first-crossing behaviour independently, and the ladder-clamping argument needs only that lp is set, which is verified.

    The contract: the band leads achieved resolution. The ladder deliberately runs ahead of the current map's FSC so the map has somewhere to climb into. Clamping the band to the achieved FSC makes the two chase each other: the band can only advance as the FSC advances, and the FSC only as fast as the band permits.

    This contract is currently expressed in exactly one place, implicitly:

    self%l_incrreslim = trim(self%incrreslim) == 'yes' .and. .not. self%l_lpset

    (simple_parameters_phases.f90:953) -- the +10-shell headroom is switched off whenever lp is set, because when the caller owns the band the FSC must not second-guess it. Nothing states the rule in prose, and nothing tests it.

    2. The clamp that violated it (closed)¶

    65bee3073 added a per-iteration clamp of kfromto(2) to the current FSC=0.143 crossing in set_bp_range3D, intending to stop matching from running past the resolution the data supports. Reverted in fb4e653ac, byte-identical to the pre-clamp file. Three defects, the first fundamental:

    • FSC=0.143 must not decide any matching resolution in a run that is not gold-standard. solve3D has no gold-standard stage by design (simple_solve3D_controller.f90:416: "gold-standard refinement belongs to refine3D_auto"), so its halves share alignment and the 0.143 crossing -- the criterion that presumes independent halves -- is inflated. Feeding it back into the band closes a loop: matched noise raises the FSC, which sharpens the band, which matches more noise. The 0.143 crossing "improving" from 4.44 to 4.03 A while the maps visibly softened is that loop's signature. The invariant was already stated at simple_commanders_refine3D.f90:66 -- "banded via lpstop (never lp: l_lpset would break gold standard)" -- and the clamp set exactly that band.

    • It clamped the ladder. Per section 1 the band in solve3D is the controller's ladder, so the clamp did not restrain an FSC heuristic; it restrained the mechanism whose job is to lead. It fired on 14-29% of iterations across ten PfCRT restarts (18-38 of ~133 each).

    • First-crossing on a non-monotonic FSC. get_find_at_crit (simple_math_ft.f90:190) returns the first shell scanning outward below the criterion. PfCRT's FSC dips near 5.97 A and recovers by 5.17 A, so a single noisy shell collapses the band for that iteration.

    Recorded in doc/policies/NU/nonuniform_filtering_policy.md. Do not reintroduce it in set_bp_range3D.

    The separate change in the same commit -- restoring the density window on the Guinier B-factor fit -- stands, and is unrelated.

    3. The FSC=0.5 promotion (implemented 2026-10-18; the analysis below led to it)¶

    This is independent of section 2 and predates it.

    Past FSC05_PROMOTE_MIN_STAGE = 2, every stage boundary re-derives the band from the project's FSC=0.5 crossing, taking the best-resolved populated state (simple_solve3D_controller.f90:718, :740):

    res05  = project_best_fsc05_resolution(params)   ! minval over populated states
lp_new = max(min(lp, res05), lp_cap)

    Promoting from 0.5 rather than 0.143 is required, not merely conservative: solve3D is not gold-standard, so 0.143 is not available to it (section 2). FSC=0.5 is the pre-gold-standard criterion and is legitimate on a correlated pair.

    The guard in that comment -- "only an FSC beyond the previous band is clean" -- is implemented, through the direction of the test: promotion fires only when res05 is finer than the current band, so the crossing it trusts always lies outside the matched region, where overfitting cannot have reached. That is what makes promotion safe at all.

    A second bound: lp_cap = LPSTOP_BOUNDS(1) = 4.5 A, and promotion only ever sharpens, so it stops firing once the ladder itself is finer than 4.5 A. PfCRT's final 3.98 A band is the ladder's own floor, not a promotion. The instability below therefore moves the mid-stage trajectory -- search cost and how fast the climb advances -- not the final band.

    The problem is dataset-specific. PfCRT's FSC does not cross 0.5 steeply; it runs nearly parallel to the threshold:

    res (A) 11.9 9.7 7.96 6.9 5.97 5.17 4.85 4.70
    FSC .893 .865 .711 .563 .522 .594 .555 .519

    So the reported crossing is hypersensitive. Applying a uniform offset to the Sep16 curve (box 300, 1.035 A):

    offset FSC=0.5 crossing FSC=0.143 crossing
    +0.00 4.63 A 4.37 A
    -0.02 4.70 A 4.37 A
    -0.03 6.09 A 4.44 A
    -0.05 6.21 A 4.44 A
    -0.08 7.06 A 4.44 A

    A 0.03 FSC difference moves it 1.5 A; FSC=0.143 barely moves. PfCRT's band is therefore re-sampled at every stage boundary from the least stable statistic in the pipeline, on its optimistic tail (minval), for reasons unrelated to the map improving. That is a plausible standing contributor to PfCRT's run-to-run spread, and it is why PfCRT and not beta-galactosidase is the dataset that keeps moving.

    Implemented 2026-10-18: res05 gates the advance, the ladder sets its size. When the FSC=0.5 crossing is finer than the planned lpinfo(istage)%lp, the stage runs at the next rung lpinfo(istage+1)%lp instead of at res05 itself. No new constants. It is less machinery than what it replaces: the band is no longer set to an arbitrary measured value.

    • A noisy crossing can now cost at most one rung early. The gate itself still flips with noise -- res05 wandering 4.6-7.1 A against an lp of 6 A flips yes/no -- but a wrong yes is bounded.
    • It does not accumulate: each stage starts from its own planned rung, so the lead is at most one rung at any boundary.
    • The ladder is monotone decreasing by construction (frc_crit - frc_stepsz per stage in lpstages, and the linear fallback likewise), so the next rung is always finer.
    • Everything in section 1 and the two guards above hold: steers on 0.5 only, the res05 >= lp return keeps the sharpen-only direction, monotone, floored at lp_cap.

    Stage omission, same day, and the real value of the approach. Borrowing the next rung's lp while staying at the stage's own crop is close to inert: set_bp_range3D ends with kfromto(2) = min(kfromto(2), nyqcrop_ind), so the advanced limit is clipped back to the crop Nyquist of lpinfo(istage)%box_crop. The stage banner then prints a limit the search never used, and the stage still spends its full budget at a sampling that cannot represent the resolution already achieved.

    So where a stage can be omitted, omit it: solve3D_stage_behind_fsc05 gates a cycle in the frequency-marching loop. That drops the whole stage config -- box_crop, smpd_crop, trslim, nspace, maxits -- which is where the wall-clock goes. Unlike the rung advance this compounds naturally: a map three rungs ahead omits three stages. Excluded are the stages carrying one-time work: start_stage, the last stage, and SYMSRCH_STAGE when an axis is actually searched. NOSYMSRCH_STAGE can be omitted, because l_symran is set only when pgrp_start outranks pgrp and so implies l_srch4symaxis: with no axis search, symmetrize does nothing at all. The rung advance is retained for the excluded stages.

    Mid-stage exit, referenced to the next rung. The internal lpexit key carries lpinfo(istage+1)%lp and check_conv3D ends the stage once res05 resolves past it. Same eligibility as the omission; only ever adds convergence.

    A first version tested params%lp, the band in use, and was reverted the same day after PfCRT latest8 converged in 2 of 6 restarts. The error was the reference quantity, not noise: a band-limited map's FSC=0.5 crossing sits at its band, so res05 < params%lp is true at the first opportunity in nearly every stage. The log shows it firing on 12.937 vs 12.938 A and 11.089 vs 11.089 A; stages 3 and 4 got 3 of 17 and 3 of 15 iterations in all six restarts, and the final FSC=0.143 spread over 4.03-7.96 A against a 4.05 A baseline. Asking "has the map got ahead of the band?" is vacuous when the band is what limits the map.

    What the benchmark says about the premise. BENCH for latest8 restart 1 gives measured per-iteration partition times of 3.02, 3.36, 9.01, 10.61, 12.37, 13.26 and 18.10 s for stages 1-6 and 8, at box_crop 88 to 160. box_crop never reaches the full 300, so per-iteration cost varies only ~6x across the ladder, and with the front-loaded budget stages 3-5 are ~33% of partition time at full budget and stage 7 another ~14%. Mid-stage iterations are worth real time. But the 46% cut latest8 showed was not slack: it came from the reverted test truncating stages the map needed. The legitimate boundary test fired 0-1 times per restart.

    So the PfCRT success criterion is that the gate declines: few or no shifts, 9/10 converged, ~4.05 A. A large saving on PfCRT would be the warning sign. The scheme is known to find slack on export_gate and streptavidin, whose maps converge faster than the ladder plans.

    Compatibility was free: emitted_lp is initialised to -1. and the stage report already does if( emitted_lp(i) < 0. ) cycle, while the add-on manifest already prints "NOT RUN BY THE BASE RUN" for that sentinel. Unrun stages were already modelled.

    This is a real behaviour change -- no constant recovers the old output, so there is no identity test for it. It needs a before/after robustness profile on beta-galactosidase (steep FSC: the band should land close to where it lands today) and PfCRT (plateau: the across-restart spread of the mid-stage band should drop). Note it is also strictly more conservative than what it replaces: a map far ahead of schedule no longer leaps, it gains one rung.

    Rejected: promoting from FSC=0.143 (with or without a backing-off margin). Not a cost question -- 0.143 presumes gold-standard halves, which solve3D does not have, so it must not decide any matching resolution here. See section 2. The absence of a res0143 project field is moot.

    Not folded in, to keep the change single-purpose: project_best_fsc05_resolution's minval over states (the optimistic tail), and logging the per-stage res05/rung/actual triple so the trajectory is diffable. The second is worth doing, with the run-summary sidecar.

    This changes results. It is judged by comparing robustness profiles before and after on cases with known truth, per doc/implementation_notes/planned/test_suite_truth_and_robustness.md, not by a single PfCRT run. A corollary applies either way: FSC=0.5 must not be a pass/fail gate for PfCRT, since on a plateau it is a ~40x amplifier of noise. cFAR and the FSC=0.143 crossing are the stable metrics.

    3a. This had been tried twice before, and written down¶

    doc/policies/3D/solve3D_policy.md already recorded the experiment, in the NU-stages paragraph: "The band therefore leads the FSC by 1.25-1.5x, which is what carries the climb: with the band at the previous FSC=0.143 crossing the alignment was starved of the shells where the merged reference still holds signal and PfCRT converged in 0/4 and 1/9 restarts (2026-09-16/18), with a 1.5x lead in 10/10 (2026-09-16), and a ceiling only pins the map."

    And two band ceilings had already been tried and retired: the class-FRC lpfinal (6.0 A on PfCRT) pinned the NU stages at 5.97 A on 2026-09-07, and the ladder's hard 4.5 A bound pinned the 2026-09-08 run at exactly 4.50 A for 30 iterations while the handoff asked for 4.14 and 3.98 A.

    The clamp of section 2 was the third attempt at the same thing. The knowledge existed, in writing, in the policy file that owns the band. That is the case for section 4: the mechanism has to be one that does not depend on someone having read 900 lines of policy before touching set_bp_range3D.

    4. The test (dropped by ruling)¶

    Dropped by ruling (maintainer, 2026-10-18; ruling 1 of testing_and_benchmarking_plan.md): the stage shift is judged on real datasets. The stage rules it relies on are listed for fast unit tests in testing_and_benchmarking_plan.md section 3.

    The broader suite proposal's Progress section reads "Nothing yet", and no tester asserts anything about the band: every kfromto(2) in a *_tester module is a fixture allocation. So a change to the band contract has exactly one detector -- a ~4,500 s run on a real dataset, read by eye, weeks later. That is the whole of the mechanism today.

    Following that proposal's rulings (every high-level test is a SIMPLE commander, no outside driver, correctness on simulated data with known truth, floors with a stated origin):

    Claim. During a solve3D climb the matching band is at or ahead of the achieved FSC=0.143, never behind it.

    0.143 as an observable, not a control input. Section 2 forbids FSC=0.143 from deciding a matching resolution in a non-gold-standard run. The test reads it to detect a regression, which is a different use: nothing in the run's behaviour depends on it. The inflation works in the test's favour here -- an inflated 0.143 reads finer than the truth, so "the band leads 0.143" is a weak lower bound on the band, and a violation of it is unambiguous.

    Fixture. Hermetic: an embedded model or phantom, no user-supplied dataset. A short climb is enough -- the contract is per-iteration, so the assertion does not need a converged map.

    Assertion. At every iteration, params%kfromto(2) is greater than or equal to the FSC=0.143 Fourier index of the current map. Reported per iteration so a failure names where the band fell behind.

    What it catches. 65bee3073 by construction: the clamp set kfromto(2) = fsc0143_ind exactly, so the assertion fails on equality's wrong side on 14-29% of iterations. It would also catch a recurrence of the 2026-09-18 static-bank revert, which tightened the handoff by the same mechanism from the other direction.

    Floor. The assertion is an inequality, not a threshold, so it needs no calibrated floor -- which is why it can be a correctness gate rather than a robustness profile. If a tolerance proves necessary (interpolation at the crossing), its origin is stated in the code and it is set from a profile of at least 20 runs, never from one.

    Bounding section 3. A second, cheaper assertion: feed project_best_fsc05_resolution a plateau-shaped synthetic FSC and fail if a small perturbation moves the promoted band by more than a stated margin. This turns the 40x amplification from a dataset surprise into a test failure, and generalises past PfCRT. It is a test of the promotion's stability and is independent of which option in section 3 is chosen.

    Registration. Reachable through simple_test_exec, wired as the existing *_tester modules and simple_test_ui_class entries are, and passing scripts/check_test_registry.py.

    5. Not proposed here¶

    • No change to the band logic, the controller, or set_bp_range3D beyond the two implemented commits. (When written, section 3 was still a decision to be taken; it has since been taken.)
    • The real-data manifest layer, cFAR and B-factor gates, and the rest of the dataset tier belong to the broader suite proposal, not to this note.
