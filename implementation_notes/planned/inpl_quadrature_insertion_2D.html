    In-plane quadrature insertion in 2D¶

    Current contract (2026-11-14)¶

    The numerical budget is now the integer parameter INPL_POSTERIOR_BUDGET = 64 in src/defs/simple_defs.f90. The former topk_inpl CLI key, typed field, parser binding, UI inputs and controller/chunk forwarding are removed without an alias. Every probabilistic 2D pass uses the fixed budget, including the cls_init=prev seed pass (confirmed by the maintainer). Passes without a probability table, including the terminal greedy pass, remain single-pose. The readout is PROB_TAB_INPL (budget K, m_max M).

    The quadrature, physical temperature, merged-bin weights and class-sum accumulation are unchanged. Verified on macOS with GNU Fortran 16 Release: make -C build -j8, complete UI validation, scripts/run_fast_gate.sh build (all 15 suites, 54.56 seconds), and make -C build install pass. The generated argument list, UI defaults and complete UI JSON contain no retired key; the CLI chain reports 597 accepted keys, with 3D's topk retained. Four compiler warnings concern uninitialized descriptors in the untouched PCG order-prior tester. No new scientific-quality or Linux validation was run. The sections below retain the original design and validation history; their CLI-switch and seed-pass exceptions are superseded by this contract.

    Original implementation record¶

    Status, 2026-11-13: implemented and gated, uncommitted, awaiting Hans's validation of the effect; the report is inpl_quadrature_insertion_2D_report.md beside this note. Revised the same day after review R1 (section 10). Base: db20cde75. Supersedes: none. Depends on: the 2D batch pipeline (eul_prob_tab2D, validated: yes, in production) and the pattern of the 3D weighted insertion's side channel (prob_insert_poses, validated: yes, production since 2026-10-30; its selector and cone are not reused). Rulings 1 to 4 and 14 are Hans's (2026-11-13); the rest are proposed. The plan's gates are the byte identity of topk_inpl=1 with the current code and the unit assertions of the enabled path; the larger-scale testing of the effect is Hans's, after delivery (ruling 4).

    1. Why¶

    Top-view class averages of small membrane proteins (SLC transporters, hemoglobin-sized particles) from solve2D show spurious radial artifacts ("spokes"). They persist with the gold standard from stage 2, in runs with a limited lpstop, and independently of the annealing tail (Hans, 2026-11-13); Joe Caesar has seen them appear as early as stage 2 and never leave. The sigma annealing proposal of 2026-11-10 (not in the repository) does not address them directly: every noise model in it is a function of radius alone, hence circularly symmetric in Fourier space, and cannot produce angular structure.

    The mechanism this plan acts on: a top view in its micelle is close to rotationally symmetric, so the in-plane angle of a particle carries almost no signal and is decided by the particle's noise against the reference's asymmetric residue. The particle's noise then enters the half's sum at that angle and the residue grows; a loop whose gain exceeds one keeps what it has made, which is Joe's observation. The gold standard keeps the half-FRC honest but does nothing within a half. Per half and per shell the restored averages do not depend on sigma2 given the poses (section 2, with its assumptions), so the noise model enters this loop through the search: which rings vote on the angle, and how sharply.

    What the insertion is hypothesised to do. The particle enters the sums at a quadrature of its in-plane posterior, conditional on its committed class and its refined shift, instead of at one drawn angle. The hypothesis is that this lowers the fresh asymmetric noise the loop is seeded with: a K-point quadrature of a flat row, at equally spaced angles, annihilates every angular harmonic of the particle's noise except the multiples of K (review R1, point 1); with K above the band's angular Nyquist at the rim (ruling 15) no resolved harmonic is one of those multiples. The points are snapped to the rotation grid, which leaves a residual unless K divides the number of rotations (review R2, point 2). It is not a rotational average over the full circle, and it does not integrate class or shift uncertainty. Whether the enabled update amplifies or attenuates a small asymmetric reference perturbation is a separate question: the quadrature is taken at the particle's own temperature (ruling 14) while the search draws at prob_beta times it, and the response of a posterior expectation to a small perturbation scales with the temperature (R1, point 4). The plan does not claim the loop's gain is unchanged; the perturbation measurement of section 7 is the way to settle it, if Hans wants it run. The intended benefit must survive the terminal hard pass through better references and assignments: smoother stage averages alone prove nothing (R1, closing).

    2. What the code check changed¶

    Checked at db20cde75 with rg; these premises are fixed by the code.

    • The in-plane draw is already hot. The fill draws each class's in-plane index at ptcl_posterior_temp, which is prob_beta times the particle's temperature (simple_polarft_corr.f90, ptcl_posterior_temp), and the class draw is at the same factor (assign_rows_dense, assign_rows_sparse). The 3D extras are weighed at the particle's own temperature, untempered (select_extras, temperature_or_argmin(self%temps(i_loc))); the 2D quadrature does the same (ruling 14).
    • The 3D extras average over direction within a cone, each entry with its own in-plane index. A 2D mirror over the row (classes) would be soft class membership and would not touch the angle; a cone in angle (rim displacement below the resolution) would exclude the whole undecided circle of a top view. Hence ruling 5: the angle, the whole row, no cone.
    • The committed angle is continuous. After the joint refinement store_continuous_e3 keeps a fractional angle and assign_ori writes it when the class and grid index are the committed ones (strategy2D_srch.f90, has_continuous_e3); otherwise e3 is 360 - get_rot(irot). A quadrature on the grid alone would replace a refined angle by its bin centre (R1, point 2): ruling 16.
    • The row at the committed pose has a public evaluator. gen_euclid_dists_bank(iref, iptcl, bank, dists) returns the nrots losses of the particle shifted once in its own frame (memoize_shifted_ptcl) against the reference; the crvec helpers it wraps are private to the calculator (R1, implementation note). Ruling 7 uses it.
    • The 2D row holds one in-plane index per class (prob_dense_entry%inpl); the full rotation row is computed in the fill (gen_posterior_val, draw_posterior_crvec) and not kept, so it is evaluated again at the committed pose (ruling 7).
    • The sums rotate at insertion with an optional weight. gen_fplane4rec makes one unrotated plane per particle with shift and CTF applied; stack_accumulate_fplane(e3, fpl, icls, weight) rotates and grids it, the data term and the CTF-squared term alike. An extra angle is one more gridding of the same plane: no second read, FFT or phase ramp.
    • Sigma2 and the restored averages, with the assumptions. The plane carries the data term and the CTF-squared term whitened by the same curve (simple_image_ctf.f90, gen_fplane4rec: "CTF*y/sigma2 and CTF^2/sigma2"). Within one half, at one shell, with sums of one iteration, the curve cancels in the ratio, and the Wiener step (add_invnoisepower2rho) forms its terms from the CTF sums and the FRC. The cancellation is not exact beyond that (R1, point 5): the KB window mixes neighbouring radii, the merged average adds the even and odd sums before normalisation (cavger_restore_cavgs, "even + odd"), so it weights the halves by their curves, and the carry-over blend (cavg_sums%blend) adds sums whitened under different generations. The algorithm doc states the cancellation under these assumptions and nothing more.
    • The row's shift frame. gen_euclid_crvec(iref, iptcl, shift) applies the shift to the reference before the rotation, so across rotations it describes a particle shift that rotates with the reference. The bank form shifts the particle once in its own frame and gives a row consistent across the circle; the sums apply the stored shift to the particle before rotation (cavger_update_sums, precs(i)%shift), the same frame.
    • Where the search commits. strategy2D_prob%srch takes the table's class, in-plane index and shift, runs refine_selected_continuously, stores and assigns; nothing after it touches the pose. The quadrature goes there, after the refinement (ruling 7). strategy3D_prob%resolve_extra_poses is the pattern.
    • Which stages have a table. Stage 1 runs snhc_smpl (classic, no table); stage 2 prob; stages 3 to 5 prob_neigh; stage 6 prob (set_refine2D_stage_search_policy); the terminal pass is refine=greedy (execute_terminal_pass). The insertion exists in stages 2 to 6 only.
    • Keyed draws. Every draw of the probabilistic routes is keyed on the run's scope and the particle (rnd_keyed(rnd_draw_key(scope, iptcl, family)), families RND_FAM_* in simple_rnd.f90); a new draw takes a new family, never ran3. Ruling 15's offset is such a draw.
    • No polar class accumulator exists; the sums are Cartesian.
    • The readout's n_eff is over classes (neff_readout_offer on the class distances), not angles.
    • topk is 3D only in the interface (simple_ui_solve3D.f90, simple_ui_refine3D.f90); the 2D programs are all in simple_ui_refine2D.f90 (refine2D, solve2D, solve2D_chunks). A chunk's solve2D command line is built from scratch with an explicit key list (init_chunk_clustering), so a solve2D key reaches the chunks only where that list forwards it (review R2, point 1; the first version of this note assumed otherwise).
    • The 2D bench times the sums separately (rt_cavg_interp_splat in restore_class_averages_for_batch), so the cost of the extras is visible where it lands.

    3. Contract¶

    • Off state. topk_inpl=1 (given explicitly; the default is 64, ruling 17) is today's run bit for bit at one thread: no row is evaluated, no side channel allocated, no readout field written other than zeros. This is the plan's gate and no ruling may waive it; the assertions of Phase 1b cover the enabled path.
    • The committed pose is the hypothesis. For identical incoming references the search, its draws and keyed random streams, the refinement, the project's class, e3 and shift, the sigma2 residual at the committed pose, the convergence statistics and every existing readout field are unchanged at any topk_inpl. The references change under topk_inpl > 1, so the run's trajectory changes from the second iteration on; "unchanged" is a statement about one pass, not about a run (R1, closing).
    • The concentrated-posterior limit reproduces today's insertion (ruling 16): a particle whose quadrature collapses to one entry enters at its refined pose at weight 1, exactly as at topk_inpl=1. Enabling the feature never inserts a well-determined particle less accurately than today.
    • The sums stay thread-count independent. A particle's entries are inserted in its slot of the bucket, in batch order (cavger_update_sums); weights of a particle sum to 1 and each weight applies to the data term and the CTF-squared term alike, so populations, the particle's mass and the carry-over's accounting are unchanged. The worker's contribution file format is unchanged.
    • Hard assignment at the end. The terminal greedy pass stays (ruling 1): the final class averages are built at the committed poses and make_cavgs from the project reproduces them. The averages of the iterations of stages 2 to 6 under topk_inpl > 1 are not reproducible from the project; the policy says so (ruling 1, Phase 1).
    • Scope of the device. The weighting is internal (ruling 3): nothing downstream reads it, no file carries it, and 3D is untouched.

    14. (2026-11-13, Hans) The temperature. The quadrature of ruling 6 is taken at the particle's own temperature, untempered (temperature_or_argmin(posterior_temperature(wsqsum_ptcl_now, pftsz)), not ptcl_posterior_temp), as the 3D extras are. This is the one element mirrored from 3D; the row is the whole circle (ruling 5) and the selection the quadrature (ruling 6). The search draws at prob_beta times that temperature; the inserted image is then the physical posterior's expectation, conditional on the class and the refined shift, approximated by K points, and the committed draw the hot hypothesis. The annealing tail leaves the quadrature's temperature where it is and cools the draws towards it.
    15. (2026-11-13, proposed after R1 point 1) The offset and the harmonic criterion. The quadrature's phase v of ruling 6 is one keyed uniform variate per particle (rnd_keyed(rnd_draw_key(scope, iptcl, RND_FAM_INPLQ)), a new family), so a flat row gives K equally spaced angles at a phase that differs from particle to particle and the surviving harmonics (the multiples of K) carry random phases across the sum, instead of the same K grid angles for every particle. The angular-error criterion of the compressed insertion against the full posterior sum: for a flat row and equally spaced points the K-point rule equals the full sum on every harmonic that is not a multiple of K; the band's highest angular harmonic at the rim is m_max = ceiling(pi * mskdiam / lp) (lp the stage's matching limit in Angstrom, mskdiam the stage's mask diameter), so K > m_max keeps every resolved harmonic off the multiples of K. The points are snapped to the rotation grid (review R2, point 2), so the two agree exactly only where K divides the number of rotations (for a phase that is not a tie); otherwise harmonic m retains up to about m 2 pi / nrots (on 360 rotations K = 64 retains 12.5 % of harmonic 40, K = 32 13 % and K = 16 17 % of those up to 50). The readout prints m_max beside K per stage; Hans chooses K against it, knowing that K > m_max is necessary and not sufficient. For a peaked row the error of the midpoint rule on harmonic m is bounded by m times half the angular width of the widest stratum, which the stage's smallest n_eff makes small; this bound is stated, not gated.
    16. (2026-11-13, proposed after R1 point 2) The continuous-angle limit. The quadrature's points are found on the grid; the point or points that fall in the committed bin are placed at the committed angle as the search commits it (continuous_e3 when has_continuous_e3, else the bin's angle) and merged into one entry. A quadrature that collapses to one entry (every u_j in one bin, the concentrated posterior) inserts the refined pose at weight 1, bit for bit today's insertion for that particle; the readout counts these (inpl_nsingle). No continuous interpolation within other bins: a bin-uniform model of the posterior within a bin is wrong exactly where it would matter, at a peak.

    Defaults taken without a question: the names topk_inpl, prob_insert_inpl, PROB_TAB_INPL, RND_FAM_INPLQ.

    1. (2026-11-13, Hans, after delivery and a run on beta-galactosidase with topk_inpl=64, class averages "almost perfect") The default. The parameter's default is 64 (Hans, the same day: one default every 2D method inherits, rather than commander defaults and callers pinned at 1), so refine2D, solve2D, solve2D_chunks, the stream (initial analysis and 2D pool), the particle sieve and validate all run at 64 unless given the key; the solve2D controller sets 1, not a removal, on stage 1, the seed pass and the terminal pass.     5. Not in this plan¶

    • The exact angular marginalisation. The posterior-weighted average over the whole circle is, per ring, a circular convolution of the particle's polar rings with the posterior, exact for every harmonic and continuous in angle, at a cost independent of K: a polar resampling of the particle's plane and its CTF-squared plane over the full lattice, an angular FFT per ring, the product with the posterior's harmonics, and a gridding of the polar samples into the Cartesian accumulators. It needs a polar geometry over every shell to the lattice's Nyquist (the calculator's covers the band) and a measurement of the two resamplings' blur against the direct insertion. It is the design to take if the K-point quadrature's cost at K > m_max or its surviving harmonics prove unacceptable on Hans's sets; it is not built here.
    • The noise model: no change to sigma2/, to the residual at the committed pose or to the seed. Follow-ups that act on the loop's gain, in the order to try them once the insertion is in:
    • Joe Caesar's early-stage shape: inflated sigmas at the rings beyond the FRC in stages 2 to 3, annealed to the measured curve (the postdocs' sigma2_white code is the instrument; the flat level everywhere is what collapsed their classes). Each ring's gain is the draw's factor times the ratio of the true noise to the assumed noise, so inflating the high rings early takes the top views' vote away from the rings with no angular signal.
    • Posterior-weighted residuals (the sigma annealing proposal's section 7.5): the residual at the committed pose is biased low where the reference has power, which raises the effective temperature throughout.
    • A reference-side Wiener factor per ring on the asymmetric angular harmonics of each class average from their cross-half correlation, which damps the residue at its source.
    • The low-pass ladder (lpstop from the mask diameter, no data-driven cap below the FRC): a separate plan.
    • topk and everything in simple_eul_prob_tab.f90, simple_eul_prob_tab_neigh.f90, simple_strategy3D_prob.f90, simple_matcher_3Drec.f90; the stream (src/main/stream, the pool's refine2D_distr), the sieve, make_cavgs, the cls_init=prev seed pass, the carried sums (cavg_state.bin) and the contribution file; shift extras; class extras; a cone; the terminal pass.
    • Larger-scale testing (ruling 4): fixture campaigns, real-data arms, floors, the real-data read; new fixtures, diagnostics or scripts. The unit assertions of Phase 1b are in.
    • Skill edits (simple-solve2d, simple-main-strategies-probabilistic, simple-main-class): one line each is proposed in Phase 1 and applied only with approval.

    10. Review R1 (2026-11-13) and what it changed¶

    A review of the first version of this plan made five points and one implementation note; all were checked against the code and hold.

    1. A fixed midpoint-CDF quadrature of a flat row is an average over a cyclic group of order K, not over the circle: harmonics that are multiples of K survive at full amplitude, and the same K grid angles would be used for every flat-row particle. Changed: the keyed per-particle offset and the harmonic criterion K > m_max (ruling 15); the "no seed" claim withdrawn from section 1.
    2. A grid quadrature discards the refined continuous angle, and a concentrated posterior would insert a well-determined particle at its bin centre. Changed: the committed-bin substitution and the collapsed case's fallback to today's insertion (ruling 16), now a contract line.
    3. The identity gate at topk_inpl=1 cannot show the enabled path is correct, and a smoke run that prints counts cannot either. The review asks for assertions in the existing suites: normalisation, merging of coincident bins, the invalid-row fallback and periodic wraparound; the angular-harmonic response of the K-point rule against the full-row sum; the narrow-posterior continuous-angle limit; non-zero particle-frame shifts and astigmatic CTFs; both accumulators, mass conservation and several threads. Changed: Hans restated ruling 4 (no larger-scale testing; unit assertions are in) and the list became Phase 1b.
    4. "Unchanged loop gain" and "the physical posterior's expectation" overstated what a finite conditional quadrature gives, and the response of a posterior expectation to a small reference perturbation scales with the temperature, so the temperature change of ruling 14 changes it. Changed: section 1 states the reduced seed as a hypothesis and the gain as open; the perturbation measurement is in section 7 for Hans to order.
    5. The sigma2 cancellation in the restored averages holds per half, per shell, for one iteration's sums, and not through the KB window's radial mixing, the merge of the halves before normalisation or the carry-over blend. Changed: the section 2 bullet and the algorithm doc's correction, under those assumptions.
    6. The row is evaluated through the public gen_euclid_dists_bank, not the calculator's private helpers. Changed: ruling 7.

    The review's closing points, that the benefit must survive the terminal hard pass and that "search unchanged" holds for identical incoming references only, are in section 1 and the contract.

    1. solve2D_chunks accepted topk_inpl and dropped it: init_chunk_clustering builds the chunks' solve2D command line from scratch with an explicit key list, so every chunk ran at 1.
