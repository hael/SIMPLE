    solve3D on small membrane proteins: diagnosis and experimental plan¶

    Archived in completed/ on 2026-11-17 by maintainer decision. The status and open items below are as last written.

    Status, 2026-11-16: in progress. Phase 1 read as Hans reported it (lpstart=16.25 worse on PorF and PfCRT, the export gate unchanged; the start cap is rejected) and the band-versus-sampling control run (Decision 13). On Hans's rulings of 2026-11-16 the tree now holds the release fix (Decision 3, committed in 360fbeb4f), the late release through stage 4 (Decision 4, amended) and the best-first sticky draw (Decision 14), the latter two uncompiled. Base: 603c44490. Supersedes: the first analysis under this name and its independent review, both written and left uncommitted on 2026-11-15 and merged here (section 2.8 lists what the review corrected; the plan adopts its recommendations). Depends on: the logs under ~/Processing/solve3D_regression/{PfCRT,PorF,exp_gate}/{current,last_working_*}/ on the Mac (read); the lpstart=16.25 restarts of PorF, the export gate and PfCRT that Hans started on 2026-11-15 (running, not read when this was written). Related: data-driven stage progression, sections 3-4 of the solve3D policy and "Sticky sampling schedules" in the fractional-update policy.

    Terms. A rung is a stage's planned low-pass limit (lpinfo(istage)%lp); the band is the matching limit an iteration used (MATCHING LOW-PASS LIMIT). Overlap is the fraction of the particles drawn in an iteration whose projection direction stayed within the angular threshold of their previous one (mi_proj); the angular change is the log's DIST BTW BEST ORIS (a running average per particle). Effective poses is the iteration log's count of poses carrying a particle's posterior (1 = sharp). The sticky subset is the class-balanced set of nsample particles that stages 1-2 refine (sticky_sampling); its release is the first stage that samples freely, stage 3 (the add-on's cohort is a different set). A first-time particle is drawn for the first time in the run, so its "previous" pose is its starting assignment. A stage consolidates when its overlap rises and its posteriors sharpen. One Fourier shell is the next coarser shell at the final reconstruction's box and pixel size.

    1. Why¶

    At 603c44490 one of five completed PorF restarts ends at 7.43 Å (FSC=0.143), against 4.11-4.22 Å in the other four and in all ten restarts of fa9b82b5; PorF has to succeed in ten of ten. All three completed export-gate restarts end at 7.17-7.40 Å, against 3.88-3.95 Å in the nine completed restarts of 3fb0b00b (on a different input, section 2); in those runs the helices were visible after stage 3, and now they never appear. PfCRT is back at its reference (4.09-4.14 Å, against 4.14-4.25 Å at db20cde7) since the in-plane posterior insertion was switched off by default (603c44490). Streptavidin, beta-galactosidase and TRPV1 are perfect by Hans's reading. TRPV1 and the hard targets all have mask diameters of 150-170 Å, so the size-planned ladder gives them the same rungs and cannot tell them apart.

    2.1 PfCRT¶

    Same data but for 54 particles, full sampling, no sticky subset. Both builds churn in stage 3 (overlap 0.05-0.10, angular change 44-60 degrees) and consolidate in stage 4 at 12.9-13.5 Å, the FSC=0.5 value. The posteriors sharpen with the stage-3 band: 25-31 effective poses at 16.3 Å (603c4449), 49-55 at 18.3 Å (db20cde7, whose rule could coarsen the stage). PfCRT needs no fix here; it is the control the ladder change must not break.

    2.2 PorF¶

    Same input and ladder in both builds (mask 170 Å: 21.25, 19.07, 16.89, 14.71, 12.54, 10.36, 8.18, 6.00 Å). Every completed success consolidates in stage 4 at a band of 12.0-14.2 Å (overlap 0.33-0.47 and 3.8-6.2 effective poses at its end). The failed restart (restart 5 of 603c4449) left stage 3 at FSC=0.5 15.6 Å, the coarsest of the sixteen, with the worst FSC=0.143 (11.6 Å against 9.2-10.1 Å). Being coarser than stage 4's rung, that FSC kept the rung (a 14.86 Å band): FSC=0.5 stayed at 14.18 Å in all 15 iterations, the overlap reached 0.25 (10.4 effective poses, 38 degrees), stage 5 reached 9.2 Å and stage 6 plateaued at 9.2/7.4 Å. PfCRT failed in the same way at fa9b82b5 (restart 8, described in db20cde75's message): stage 4 at 14 Å, stalled at overlap 0.36 and 31 degrees.

    The rule of 2026-11-13 (set_stage_lp_from_fsc05, the FSC=0.5 value when finer than the rung since a4881e6c4) ran stage 4 coarser than the one-rung advance of fa9b82b5 in three of the five other restarts (13.6-14.2 Å against 12.5 Å).     2.3 The export gate: the band¶

    3fb0b00b planned its ladder from the 2D input's class FRCs (lpstages: the mean class FRC was 0.97 at 20 Å and still 0.86 at 9.1 Å), the inflated kind that gold-standard 2D removed on 2026-11-10: 20.0, 9.1, 8.45, 7.89, 7.40, 7.17, 6.96, 4.5 Å. 603c4449 plans from the mask diameter (set_lplims_from_size, 155 Å): 19.38, 17.44, 15.50, 13.56, 11.63, 9.69, 7.75, 5.81 Å. Honest class FRCs would not give the old rungs (they moved PfCRT's stage 4 from 12.4 to 18.3 Å, the reason the size ladder replaced them), and the 3D FSC=0.5 cannot lead either: after stages 1-2 it sits at or behind the band (18.2-18.3 Å for PfCRT and the export gate, 22-24 Å for PorF at a 19.5 Å band). What worked was a band well ahead of the FSC: the old stage 2 matched at 9.1 Å while its FSC=0.5 was 13-16 Å, and its stage 3 at 8.5 Å took the overlap from 0.04 to 0.78 in ten iterations. The difference starts in stage 2, before the sticky subset is released, so the release (section 2.4) cannot be the whole story.

    2.4 The export gate: the sticky subset's release¶

    Stages 1-2 refine one class-balanced sticky subset of 10,020 particles (9% of 111,099); sticky rescoring (sample4update_rescore) advances its updatecnt every iteration, to 40 after 20 + 20 iterations. Stage 3 samples freely, each class's quota lowest updatecnt first (sample4update_class), and the sampling history is reset only at the run's first iteration (sample_ptcls4prob3D). Three consequences:

    • Never-searched particles take every draw until they catch up with the subset's count. With 60 classes at a quota of 167, the smallest class (574 particles) needs ceil(40 × 407 / 167) = 98 free iterations before its sticky particles are drawn again, larger classes longer; the runs have 48-49. No particle of the subset is searched again after stage 2.
    • The first stage-3 iteration seeds the trailing chain from its own sample (VOLASSEMBLE: SEEDED FULL-MASS TRAILING CHAIN ... THIS ITERATION USES THE CURRENT SAMPLE ONLY, the absent-chain path of blend_trailing_accumulators). The stage-2 map guides that iteration's alignment, but from then on no image of the subset is in any map, while the chain's represented population (20,040) counts the subset and weights the first stage-3 sample twice.
    • Stage 3 is fed by first-time particles aligned against an unconsolidated map, for long: the largest class (6,267 particles) is seen once only after 38 iterations (Draws for one full sweep), and every particle has been drawn only in stage 7.
