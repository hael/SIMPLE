    Stream 3D Ingestion Policy¶

    How the 2D pool hands particles to multistate 3D, how 3D takes them in, and what a change must preserve.

    1. Scope¶

    • The pool's publications (p06): export_pool_state in src/main/stream/stages/simple_stream_stage_pool2D.f90, the pool's publish in src/main/stream/pool2D/simple_stream_pool2D.f90, and build_pool_publication, pool_publication_nselected, build_sieve_publication, combine_sieve_classes and delete_pool_publication in src/main/stream/pool2D/simple_stream_refine2D_utils.f90; the first set from the sieve's 2D, the stage's publish_first_set, first_set_due, collect_sieve_set and publish_sieve_set (sections 3 and 9).
    • Their import (p07): import_sets, take_first_publication, merge_publication and take_cavgs in src/main/stream/stages/simple_stream_stage_solve3D.f90.
    • p07's jobs (next_job): the route to the reference volumes on the first set, their check, the further attempts and the fallback (section 9); each later publication's assignment to the references, by cls_expansion, matching, rejection by fit, mapping and a local refinement of the particles' poses with the states fixed (section 10); what it does with a failed job, and the folders it keeps. The matching itself is simple_cavgs_state_match (src/main/class/), run by the simple_private_exec program assign_cavgs_states (exec_assign_cavgs_states in simple_commanders_cavgs).
    • p07's 3D snapshots (section 8): apply_gui_updates and write_snapshot in the same file.
    • The method's rules, constants, steps on projects and job command lines, without the stage: simple_state_assignment (src/main/solve/, since 15 October 2026), shared with the batch program solve3D_pyramid (doc/policies/3D/solve3D_pyramid_policy.md). The stage keeps its phases, jobs, retries, fallbacks, publications and GUI, and calls the shared module for the rest: the pool model's scoring (score_cavgs_with_model, select_route_classes, model_rejected_rows), the check (check_state_refs, refs_usable, docked_pair_cc, read_refs_record), the assignment (map_assignment, reject_by_fit, flag_ambiguous, write_matched_cavgs), the local refinement (set_refine_seeds, write_refine_project, map_refine_result) and the set_*_cline builders. A change there changes both programs.

    Since 13 October 2026 p07 runs no solve3D, solve3D_addon or final refine3D (doc/refactoring_notes/planned/stream_p07_state_assignment_plan_2026-10-13.md); the inputs nptcls3D_max, nptcls_addon_max, nstages and ini3D_cavgs went with them.

    2. What the pool publishes¶

    The pool publishes its state, not deltas. A publication is the pool's classified set as one completed iteration left it.

    1. The stacks whose particles have been through an iteration (updatecnt > 0), with their micrographs and all their particles. Stacks never classified, such as the sets imported since the last dispatch, are never published. Within a published stack, a particle never updated (a fractional update did not sample it) keeps its row but is published deselected (state 0): its class is the random one it was given on import. A later publication selects it once updated.
    2. 2D parameters at publication time: class, in-plane angle, shifts and state in ptcl2D. ptcl3D is prepared as the pool's final project prepares it: a copy of ptcl2D with the 2D clustering removed (class, in-plane angle, corr, frac), shifts kept, and no updatecnt or sampled. A publication carries the newest optics map's groups (micrographs, stacks, particles) and its optics table (build_pool_publication); the pool keeps none of its own.
    3. Self-consistent indexing. Stacks are renumbered from 1, and their particle ranges and the particles' stack indices follow. Each particle carries its image index in its stack (indstk).
    4. Classes: the pool's cls2D table, all classes.
    5. Class averages and FRCs. The pool's class averages (and their even and odd halves) and FRCs at the native sampling: rescaled when the pool runs downscaled, copied otherwise. They are registered in os_out with the pool's mask diameter.

    3. When and how it is published¶

    1. When:

    2. The first set, in a pool that has published nothing yet, is the sieve's own 2D of the sets it has handed off (section 9, items 1 and 2), with their classes and class averages, from which p07 makes its reference volumes (section 9). It is published as soon as it is due, in the pass that finds it so, without waiting for a pool iteration (publish_first_set, first_set_due), at the first of:

      • the sets handed off, in the pool yet or not, hold NPTCLS_FIRST3D (100,000) accepted (state 1) particles;
      • they hold NMICS_FIRST3D (500) micrographs' worth of accepted particles at their average rate per micrograph, that is, they cover 500 micrographs;
      • the sieve's final set has come, after which no more particles come;
      • pool iteration FIRST_EXPORT_ITER (10) has completed (export_pool_state).

      Decided 12 October 2026; until then the first publication was the pool's (the sieve's only with sieve_ini3D=yes), after the first iteration that selected 100,000 particles or iteration 10. p07 takes the first set whole (section 4, item 3); from 12 to 13 October 2026 it kept at most nptcls3D_max of it. - When the first set cannot be built (section 9, item 2), the pool's classified state goes instead, once (exports_after): after the first iteration whose publication would select NPTCLS_FIRST3D particles (npublishable, counted as build_pool_publication selects: updated and kept by the pool), or after iteration 10, whichever comes first. At iteration 10 it carries the sieve's mask diameter, which the pool takes when it dispatches iteration 10 (MSKDIAM_SWITCH_ITER); earlier, the pool's starting diameter. - Later publications are the pool's, once per completed pool iteration from EXPORT_START_ITER (25) on. Each is published in the pass where that iteration's results have come back and before the next iteration is dispatched, so a publication is one consistent iteration. Iterations 11 to 24 publish nothing.

    3D takes the first publication as published (section 4, item 3). A restarted pool with publications on disk skips the first and resumes at 25, so 3D never takes the early classification of a restarted pool as a later publication. 2. Nothing classified: a pool with no classified stack publishes nothing for that iteration. 3. File names: a publication is <id>.simple (5-digit id) in p06's completed folder (DIR_STREAM_COMPLETED). Its class averages and FRCs are written beside it first (<id>_cavgs.mrcs with _even and _odd, <id>_frcs.bin), and the project last, under a temporary name renamed into place. A reader that finds the project finds it complete. 4. Ids increase by one per publication. A restarted p06 continues after the highest id on disk (restore_export_id). 5. Retention: the newest NPUBLICATIONS_KEPT (2) publications are kept. When publication n is written, publication n - 2 and its files are removed.

    4. How 3D takes them in¶

    1. Watching: p07 watches p06's completed folder and records each publication once.
    2. Only the newest: in a pass where no job runs, p07 takes the newest publication it has not taken. Older ones not taken are passed over, because the newest holds what they held.
    3. A publication p07 cannot use (no class averages, FRCs or cls2D, a count mismatch, a stack that changed size, image indices that disagree with the rows: publication_problem) is passed over with a warning before anything is merged, and listed in rejected_publications.txt in p07's folder, so a restart passes it over too. The stage waits for the next publication instead of stopping.
    4. The mask diameter is taken from every publication used (take_mskdiam): a change is logged and applies from the next job.
    5. The first publication p07 takes, into a stage without rows (in a fresh session, the first set, section 3, item 1; after a p07 restart, the newest), is taken as published, without the pool model (take_first_publication): its selected particles (the sieve's selection; the pool's updated and kept particles when p06 published its own) are merged as selected, all of them, and are the first set: their selection stays for the session, and later publications change only their 2D parameters (item 5). Without references the route to them is due on it (section 9); with references (a restart that found them) its assignment is (section 10). No publication is taken while a job runs, so the route sees the first set alone. Until 7 October 2026 p07 classified the first publication's particles again with a solve2D of its own; on 8 October 2026 the first publication stopped going through the pool model; from 12 to 13 October 2026 at most nptcls3D_max of it stayed selected.
    6. The pool model is logged, not applied, on later publications (since 13 October 2026). A publication's class averages are scored once with the pool quality model (score_cavgs_with_model, CAVG_QUALITY_MODEL_POOL_DEFAULT), with a mask diameter fitted to their box (fit_mskdiam), and the selected and rejected class averages written with JPEGs in quality_selection/<id>/. The verdict changes no row: the selected rows a rejected class holds are flagged (flag_model_rows) and counted beside the fit's rejection once the publication is assigned (section 10, item 5), so the two can be compared before the model is dropped. L_MODEL_LOGGED turns the scoring off. The rows' selection is the pool's, as published, less the fit's rejection.
    7. Merge into the stage's rows (merge_publication):
    8. A stack the stage holds is matched by its stack name, in whatever order the publication lists it, and each particle by its image index in the stack (indstk; the stage's row must record the same image, or the stage stops). Its particles take the publication's 2D parameters (transfer_2Dparams) and 2D state in place. The 3D state is an assignment's: a particle the publication deselects gets 0, a selected particle keeps its state, and a selected particle without one (state 0) gets 1 until it is assigned. They keep their 3D parameters, CTF and optics group. A first-set row takes the 2D parameters only: its selection and state stay, except that a row the latest assignment deselected (its sub-class rejected by fit, or without a class of the matching; fit_desel) is selected again, as state 1, and judged again by the next assignment.
    9. A new stack is appended with its micrograph and particles (2D and 3D), after the rows the stage holds.
    10. A stack the publication lacks keeps its rows, deselected (state 0 in both segments). This happens after a pool restart, until the restarted pool has classified it again. The first set's rows keep their selection.
    11. The stage's cls2D is the publication's, and so is its optics table when the publication carries one (the newest optics map's; group ids are kept across maps).
    12. The class averages and FRCs come with the classes (take_cavgs). The publication's are copied, with their halves, into quality_selection/<id>/ and replace the stage's cavg and frc2D entries in its out segment; the volumes and FSCs stay. The route's solve2D, the fallback's solve3D_cavgs and the expansion read them, and an assignment whose expansion fails matches them; p06 removes a publication two publications later (section 3, item 5).
    13. The jobs (next_job), each in the pass that makes it due, none while another runs:
    14. without references, once MIN_PTCLS_PER_STATE (5) particles per state (nstates) are selected: the route when it is due (the first set taken, or an attempt failed with attempts left), else the fallback when it is due (section 9);
    15. with references, a publication's assignment when one is due and 5 particles per reference are selected (section 10).

    Settings: the commander checks 2 <= nstates <= 20 (the GUI status holds MAX_STATES_SOLVE3D_MULTISTATE); nstates (default NSTATES3D, 3) is the reference volumes' states at most, and the states that hold class averages are kept. The jobs run on nparts3D parts (default 8; a job is distributed only with more than one) and nthr3D threads (default 8), with the pool's mask diameter; the matching runs in shared memory on nthr3D threads. Every other setting is a named constant of the stage. 7. Failures never stop the stage: a failed step of the route is skipped (section 9, item 11); a failed or unusable solve3D_cavgs is an attempt without volumes (section 9, item 10); a publication whose expansion fails has its pool class averages matched; a failed matching leaves the states as they were until the next publication; a failed local refinement leaves the assignment at the classes' poses, with the previous volumes. 8. After each local refinement the volumes and FSCs refine3D registered in its project, and the refined poses, become the stage's, in place of every earlier state's (take_refine_result); the stage's project is written into the refinement's folder as the snapshots' source (result_projfile) and as the stage's project (under a temporary name, renamed); the GUI gets each state's volume, population, resolution, FSC curve and orientation distribution (send_volumes). - The status reports the particles selected now (particles_imported, shown as "particles selected"), the particles the latest assignment gave a state (particles_at_last_refine), the assignments started (refine_iteration), and, once the references exist, each state's population and resolution as sent with its volume (none for the references). The rows' current labels are not used, since the particles merged since an assignment carry state 1 until the next. 9. What p07 keeps: the newest NQUALITY_KEPT (3) quality folders and those of the publications the route or the fallback started from (listed in quality_selection/runs.txt), with the class averages and FRCs copied into them; state_refs/ (each attempt's volumes in try_<n>/, the references and their record); the latest assignment's folder (assign/it_<n>/), its predecessors removed once it is refined; no folder set aside for a job left unfinished once an assignment is refined. A stop cancels the running job (doc/policies/stream/restart_policy.md). 10. The pool's final publication (pool_final=yes in its out segment) is assigned like any other, and nothing follows it. Taken before the references exist, it is assigned once they do (accept_refs); while the route's attempts fail it also ends the wait for a fallback publication (section 9, item 10). 11. Retired on 13 October 2026: solve3D on the first set (with the first set's cap and queue, cavg_ini_ext from the route's poses), the solve3D_addon runs on each growth (their cohort cap, verdict and rollback) and the final multistate refine3D. The policy as it was is in the git history of this file.

    5. Invariants a change must keep¶

    • No unclassified particle reaches 3D: a publication holds only stacks whose particles have been through an iteration, and publishes deselected any of their particles never updated.
    • A publication is one completed iteration, published before the next dispatch.
    • A publication is complete when its name appears (temporary name, rename), and its class averages and FRCs exist before it.
    • Publication ids only grow.
    • p07's rows only grow and are never renumbered: row i is the same particle image for the life of the stage. Every job's project is a copy of the rows, and the assignment maps its result back by row.
    • Particles are matched by stack name and image index, never by row, so a pool restart that reorders its stacks changes nothing in p07.
    • A row's 3D parameters, its state, CTF and optics group are changed only by an assignment, never by a publication, which only deselects or selects (a deselected row loses its state).
    • The references never change once accepted (plan, decision 5): every publication is matched against the same volumes, and the refinements from the particles never feed back into them.
    • One quality authority per row: a first-set row's selection is the first publication's, as the sieve made it (the pool, when p06 published its own), less the route's model selection, which deselects it for the session; every other row's is the newest publication's, as the pool made it. The fit's rejection is a per-publication judgement for every row: it deselects until the next publication, which brings the row back to be judged again (since 14 October 2026; a first-set row was deselected for the session before).

    6. Change rules¶

    • A change of what a publication holds, or when it is written, updates sections 2 and 3, and checks p07's merge against it.
    • A change of the merge keeps the row invariants of section 5.
    • A change of the check, the rejection or the ambiguity flag keeps its rule a pure function (refs_usable, reject_by_fit, flag_ambiguous in simple_state_assignment; best_attempt of the stage), tested on fixed numbers; it is shared with solve3D_pyramid.
    • Tests:
    • unit_stream "pool 2D": test_publication_holds_classified_stacks (classified stacks only, renumbering, image indices, the selected particles counted as published, nothing to publish before any iteration), test_export_numbering, test_pause_rules (exports_after: in a fresh pool only, iteration 10 or earlier once NPTCLS_FIRST3D are selected; every iteration from 25; first_set_due: NPTCLS_FIRST3D accepted particles handed off, 500 micrographs, or the final set) and test_sieve_final_set (the accepted particles, their micrographs and the final set noted as handed off; a first set that cannot be built fails once, publishes nothing and leaves it to the pool);
    • unit_stream "solve 3D": test_merge_publications (appending, matching in another order and by image index, selection and class in place with the 3D parameters and state kept, deselection of a missing stack, the classes), the watch order and mask diameter, test_take_cavgs, test_first_publication (taken whole as the pool selects it, the first set kept through later publications and a missing stack, the other rows following them), test_rules (the job rule, the best attempt, the final publication), test_reference_volumes, test_failed_attempts (section 9), test_assignment (section 10), the retention, the status, the volumes and the references sent;
    • unit_reconstruction "state assignment rules" (simple_state_assignment_tester): the check, the renumbering, the sub-class budget, the mask diameter, the fit rules (the outlier cut, the 30% cap, the worst first, no non-candidate rejected, none when all fit; the ambiguity flag), the first set's cut at whole stacks, the class averages by state and the jobs' command lines;
    • unit_pftc_align2D3D "cavgs state match": the matching (section 10, item 3);
    • lib_stream "sieve to 3D" and "movies to 3D" (simple_stream_chain_tester) run the jobs, until the first assignment is refined.
    • The scoring of a publication with the pool model has no unit test.

    7. Known gaps¶

    • Cost of a publication: each one writes the pool's whole classified set, about the size of the pool project, once per iteration from 25; the first set holds the sieve's sets instead.
    • Stack matching is a search from the last match; it is linear per stack only when the publication's order differs from the stage's (after a pool restart).
    • p07 never removes rows: a stack the pool drops for good stays deselected in p07's rows.
    • Retention of 2: a reader that lags two publications behind finds the files of the older one gone. p07 reads the newest in the pass it sees it.
    • The first set's selection is the least settled and stays: it is the sieve's selection, from its chunks' classification of the first sets handed off, with no pool model decision but the route's, and later publications do not change it.
    • The assignment is only as good as the references: the check refuses halves that disagree, nearly empty states and near-duplicate states (at 15 Å), but the references are never refreshed.
    • The rejection by fit may follow the sub-classes' sizes: smaller sub-class averages are noisier and correlate less at 15 Å whatever their fit. Each assignment logs the fit's median, deviation and threshold, each rejected class's correlation and population, the median population of the kept and rejected candidates, and the correlation of the best correlation with the log population over the candidates; a population-aware statistic waits for those numbers from a validation run.
    • The particles' poses start from the class averages, composed with each particle's 2D parameters, and are refined only locally (refine=cont: athres_cont, 10 degrees, and trs): a particle further off than that, or in the wrong state, stays wrong. Each assignment starts again from the class poses, so the refinement never accumulates over the session. It is one iteration (maxits=1) over every selected particle of every publication, still the most expensive step of an assignment.
    • The matching is at a fixed 15 Å (LP_ASSIGN): states that differ only at higher resolution are not told apart. Its shift limit is the program's default (10 pixels), not the pool's.
    • Every publication is expanded, which reads every particle of it, much like a pool iteration; when a publication's three jobs take longer than a pool iteration, p07 assigns only the newest waiting, so the assignment lags the pool rather than queueing behind it.
    • The rejection by fit discards what the references lack (a conformation or views the first set did not hold), and blurred junk may fit low-pass projections well enough to be kept; the pool model's verdict is logged beside it until the comparison settles which to keep.
    • A p07 restart takes the newest publication first, as the pool selects it, without the pool model; with references on disk it is assigned at once, otherwise the route runs on it.
    • The jobs are not tested end to end in the stage's unit tests: the command lines of cls_expansion, assign_cavgs_states and refine3D, and the reading of their results, are exercised only by the chained stream tests.

    8. 3D snapshots¶

    A 3D snapshot is a particle set the GUI asks for from multistate 3D's latest result: the particles of one or more selected states. The request (snapshot3D) and its report are in the IPC policy (section 6).

    In NICE the states come from the multistate page's state tiles (_cls3D_state_selector.html). A click on a tile only shows its state; the checkbox in its corner puts it in or out of the selection. Every state starts in. The states left out are kept in sessionStorage, because the zoom page reloads itself every 10 s without an interaction. The button names the states it sends and is disabled after one click.

    1. The source is the latest local refinement's rows (result_projfile, assign/it_<n>/assignment.simple), not the stage's current rows. Rows imported since that assignment have none yet. A failed refinement leaves the previous one as the source.
    2. What it holds:
    3. the particles of the selected states, merged into state 1 in ptcl3D and ptcl2D alike, with their 3D orientations;
    4. every other particle deselected;
    5. no state artifacts in out: no volumes and no FSCs (remove_state_artifacts_from_osout, as the batch selection oritype=ptcl3D states=... gives).
    6. Where: snapshots/<name>/<name>.simple in p07's folder, written with:
    7. <name>_micrographs.star and <name>_particles.star, with the optics table the result holds (the newest publication's) and p06's optics offset (OPTICS_ID_DELTA per GUI display);
    8. each selected state's volume, copied as vol_state<NN>.mrc with its original state number, which the project does not register.
    9. When: in the pass after the request arrives, between steps, so never while a job is started or collected. Each id is written once.
    10. Not written: a request before any result, or one whose states hold no particle, is answered with no particles and no file, as p06 answers a 2D snapshot of an iteration it no longer keeps.
    11. After the stream has finished, p07 no longer runs. NICE then starts the batch selection oritype=ptcl3D states=... on p07's final project (solve3D_multistate/solve3D_multistate.simple). Its result is a batch job, with no snapshot folder or STAR files.
    12. Tests: test_snapshot3D in p07's tester: before a result; from a fixture result with three states, two of them selected; the same request again.

    9. The first set from the sieve's 2D, and the reference volumes from class averages¶

    The first publication is the sieve's own 2D (items 1 and 2; since 12 October 2026). p07 then makes the states' reference volumes once, from class averages of its own (items 3 to 13). Decided 12 October 2026 as the initial volumes of the first solve3D (ini3D_cavgs), and since 13 October 2026 the references every publication is assigned to (section 10); it replaced the route of 8 October, on which p06 marked the first publication (sieve_ini3D=yes) and p07 ran solve3D_cavgs on the sieve's combined classes (doc/refactoring_notes/completed/stream_sieve_ini3D_plan_2026-10-08.md). The marker, the master's input and its forwarding are gone.

    1. p06 collects the sieve's 2D. While it has published nothing, each set the sieve hands off (as it is handed off, before the pool takes it) has its class averages (main, _even, _odd) and FRCs copied into sieve_cavgs/<set stem>/ (collect_sieve_set), with the first mask diameter and pixel size the sets carry; its accepted particles and micrographs are counted (nptcls_sieved, nmics_sieved) and the sieve's final set is noted (l_sieve_final_seen) for the trigger (section 3, item 1). A restarted pool with publications on disk collects nothing.
    2. The first publication is the sieve's 2D (publish_sieve_set), when the first set is due (section 3, item 1):
    3. every set the sieve has handed off, in the pool yet or not, re-read whole, combined (build_sieve_publication): the stacks renumbered with each particle's image index, the sieve's 2D parameters and selection, each set's class labels offset by the classes of the sets before it, the class tables concatenated with the sieve's states and the populations of the selected particles;
    4. the class averages, halves and FRCs concatenated in set order beside the publication under the usual names (combine_sieve_classes), refused when a file is missing, a stack does not hold its set's classes, or the boxes or FRC sizes differ;
    5. the newest optics map's groups and the sieve's mask diameter.

    It is tried once: when it cannot be built, the pool's publication goes instead, at the pool's trigger (section 3, item 1), with a warning. sieve_cavgs/ is removed after the attempt. Later publications are the pool's. 3. p07 takes it as any first publication (section 4, item 3), and the route below is due on it. Each step runs as a job on the 3D jobs' parts and threads, in the pass the previous one finished, and no publication is taken while one runs. 4. solve2D (start_solve2D_first, solve2D/) classifies every selected row into NCLS2D_FIRST (150) classes with the pool's mask diameter and cache=yes, and solve2D's defaults otherwise. Its classes, class averages, halves and FRCs and the rows' 2D parameters become the stage's (adopt_job_2D); a result without them, or with other rows, counts as failed (usable_job_2D). 5. The pool model's selection (select_first_set_classes): the classes are scored with the pool quality model (select_route_classes in simple_state_assignment, CAVG_QUALITY_MODEL_POOL_DEFAULT), and the selection is mapped to their particles; the selected and rejected stacks go to solve2D/quality_selection/. A rejected class's particles are deselected for the session: they stay first-set rows, whose selection later publications do not change. When the model cannot score the classes or keeps none, every populated class is kept, with a warning. 6. cls_expansion (start_clsexp, cls_expansion/) splits the classes of the selected particles into NCLS_EXPANDED (500) subclasses in all, and never fewer than the classes, which cls_expansion refuses (expansion_budget; ncls is cls_expansion's total since 14 October 2026). cls_expansion spreads the total over groups of similar class averages by population and keeps a class too small to split whole. Its sub-class averages are kernel pre-images of members (kernel-weighted, CTF-corrected means of the particles, the latent rank and bandwidth chosen by cross-validation; cls_expansion's only route since 14 October 2026), for the route and every publication alike; its settings are its defaults (minpop 25). The mask diameter is fitted to the particles' box. Its subclasses, their class averages, halves and FRCs, and the particles' subclass labels become the stage's (adopt_job_2D). 7. solve3D_cavgs (start_cavgs3D, solve3D_cavgs/) makes nstates volumes from those class averages (pgrp=c1, prune=no, the mask diameter; one run, no collapse restarts). Its low-pass limits are a fixed ramp from LPSTART_CAVGS3D (20 Å) to LPSTOP_CAVGS3D (12 Å) over its stages (lpstart, lpstop, force_lp_range=yes), not one derived from the class FRCs. Its angular sampling window is narrowed after the symmetry search (prob_athres_taper=yes: 90 degrees through stage 3, then halved each stage down to 10; solve3D_cavgs_policy.md, section 5.2), so the poses settle. Its result is not mapped onto the particles: they get their states from the assignments (section 10). 8. The check (finish_cavgs3D, register_attempt, refs_usable). solve3D_cavgs records the even/odd angular distance and state agreement of the class averages on its vol_cavg entries (solve3D_cavgs_policy.md, section 7). The angular distance checked is the median over the class averages whose halves are in one state (eo_angdist_median): the states are in frames of their own, so the mean over every class (eo_angdist, logged beside it, and checked only for a result without the median) mixes in poses compared across frames. The states that hold class averages are renumbered from 1 (compact_states), and their volumes, with the reprojection and orientation JPEGs beside them, copied into state_refs/try_<n>/ with a record (state_refs.txt: the count, box and pixel size, the even/odd values and the largest correlation between two docked states, the file names). solve3D_cavgs refines its states independently, so each volume is in a frame and hand of its own: every pair of states is compared after docking the second onto the first, as it is and mirrored, over rotations and shifts, from 100 Å to 15 Å, the matching's limit (docked_pair_cc, dock_vols); the better of the two hands counts, and a correlation that is not finite counts as identical. The volumes pass when the angular distance is at most EO_ANGDIST_MAX (50 degrees since 14 October 2026, 20 before; the first runs measured about 40 on volumes that looked usable), the agreement at least EO_OVERLAP_MIN (70%), no two docked states correlate above REFS_PAIR_CC_MAX (0.97: near-duplicate states would split the particles by noise), and at least NSTATES_MIN (2) states each hold STATE_FRAC_MIN (5%) of the class averages (provisional constants, until a validation run sets them). The run of 13 October 2026 that collapsed ended with the halves 90.6 degrees apart, as random orientations would be. The class averages each state was made from are written beside its volume, a stack and a tiled JPEG per state (cavgs_state<NN>, write_state_cavgs), when solve3D_cavgs' class averages are on disk. The GUI status is "checking the initial volumes". 9. Passed: the attempt's volumes, JPEGs (with the per-state class averages) and record are copied into state_refs/ and are the references for the session (accept_refs, restore_refs): the stage's states (params%nstates) are theirs, and the GUI gets them (send_refs) under their states, without FSCs, populations or orientations, since they come from class averages. The stage's project does not register them. 10. Failed (next_attempt): the route runs again from its solve2D (a new random start), at most ROUTE_TRIES (2) attempts in all; the first set's rows the earlier attempt deselected stay deselected. Then the fallback: once the next publication is merged, solve3D_cavgs runs once on its pool class averages (start_fallback), checked the same way. When the fallback fails too, or the pool has published its last, the attempt with the smallest even/odd angular distance (best_attempt; one not measured last) gives the references, with a warning, rather than run with no states. Without any volumes there are none, and nothing is assigned. 11. A failed step is skipped, with a warning: a failed or unusable solve2D runs solve3D_cavgs on the first set's own class averages; a failed or unusable cls_expansion on the classes the model kept; a failed solve3D_cavgs, or one that leaves no class with a state or no volume for a state that holds class averages, is an attempt without volumes. 12. The GUI shows "running solve2D on the first set", "running cls_expansion on the first set" and "running solve3D_cavgs", each with the 3D progress of 1; with the references the progress is 2. 13. Restart: a restarted p07 takes the references recorded in state_refs/ when every volume is there, and skips the route and the check (init_params); without them it runs the route on its first publication. A job folder a stopped step left is set aside (fresh_job_dir). 14. Tests: test_sieve_publication in p06's tester (the offsets, selection, class tables and populations; the combined files; a box mismatch and a missing half refused); in p07's, test_rules (the check, the best attempt, the renumbering, the sub-class budget), test_reference_volumes (an attempt that fails kept aside and the route run again, one that passes renumbered with its JPEGs and taken, a restart that takes it), test_failed_attempts (the fallback waiting for a publication, the best attempt after it or after the last publication, none without volumes), test_send_refs (the references sent under their states, without FSCs or populations, not registered) and test_take_cavgs; in the nightly lib_stream "solve 3D reference docking", test_reference_docking (two different structures distinct, a rotated and mirrored copy a near-duplicate). The fast tests compare no states (l_dock_refs off: the docking takes too long for them). The jobs and the pool model's selection of the first set run only in a stream.

    10. The assignment of a publication¶

    From the first publication after the first set (in p06's schedule, pool iteration 25), every publication merged once the references exist is assigned to them: its classes are split into sub-classes, whose averages are matched against the references, each particle takes the state and pose of its sub-class, and the poses are refined locally with the states fixed. Decided 13 October 2026 (doc/refactoring_notes/planned/stream_p07_state_assignment_plan_2026-10-13.md, decisions 1 to 12); the local refinement replaced the reconstruction from the class poses (reconstruct3D) on 14 October 2026.

    1. Due: each publication merged while the references exist (import_sets), the first set of a restart that found them, and the final publication when it came before them (accept_refs). Only the newest waiting is assigned (section 4, item 2). Each assignment works in assign/it_<n>/ (fresh_job_dir; n counts the session's assignments).
    2. The expansion (start_expand, assign/it_<n>/expand/): cls_expansion of the publication's selected classes into NCLS_EXPANDED (500) sub-classes in all, never fewer than the classes (expansion_budget), with kernel sub-class averages, both as for the route, with the mask diameter fitted to the particles' box, on the 3D jobs' resources. Pool classes group particles mainly by view, so particles of different states with similar projections often share a class; the expansion separates them, and the matching compares sub-class averages with references made from sub-class averages. A usable result (usable_job_2D) is matched; otherwise the pool's class averages are, with a warning. The expansion is not adopted: the rows keep the pool's 2D parameters, which the next merge refreshes.
    3. The matching (start_assign, assign_cavgs_states, a simple_private_exec program run in assign/it_<n>/ in shared memory on the 3D jobs' threads), on a copy of the expansion's (or the stage's) project, with the references as vol1..volN, lp=15 (LP_ASSIGN), nspace=1000 (NSPACE_ASSIGN) and the mask diameter fitted to the class averages' box (simple_cavgs_state_match):
    4. both sets are brought to one pixel size, the coarsest of lp/3 and the two sets' own (with boxes of 32 at least), then to one box by clipping, so references made from other class averages (the fallback's) match too;
    5. each reference is reprojected over an even grid of directions on the whole sphere (oris%spiral, reproject); a mirrored view is the view from the opposite side, so the grid holds every mirror and none is searched (match_imgs with l_nomirr);
    6. both sets are normalised within, and softly masked to, the particle, and every class average is aligned with every reprojection by a full in-plane search with shifts (match_imgs, the core of match_cavgs and cluster_cavgs);
    7. per class: its state, the one whose best reprojection correlates best; its correlation; its margin, that correlation less the best of any other state; its pose, that reprojection's direction with euler(3) = 360 − the in-plane rotation (refine3D's convention), and its shift rotated to the reference frame, in the class averages' pixels (reduce_state_matches, matched_pose).

    The project's cls3D is written in place: per class its state (0 for a class cls2D deselects), Euler angles, shift, corr, margin and class. 4. Rejection and flags (take_assignment): over the candidates, the classes with a state and particles, a class whose correlation falls below the candidates' median less FIT_NMAD (3) median absolute deviations is rejected (reject_by_fit: state 0), the worst first, at most FIT_REJECT_MAX (30%) of them; a kept class with a margin below MARGIN_AMBIGUOUS (0.02) is flagged (flag_ambiguous: cls2D key ambiguous 1 in the matched project) and assigned as any other. The matched project is written back with both, and the matched (sub-)class averages are grouped into the assignment's folder (write_assignment_cavgs): a stack and a tiled JPEG per state (cavgs_state<NN>, the ambiguous ones included), one of those the fit rejected (cavgs_rejected) and one of the ambiguous (cavgs_ambiguous). Every JPEG p07 writes is logged with its absolute path, then once more on a line of its own, >>> JPEG <path>, as the other stages log theirs. The log carries the fit's median, deviation, threshold and cap, each rejected class's correlation, population and state, the median population of the kept and rejected candidates, and the correlation of the best correlation with the log population over the candidates, so a validation run shows whether the rejection follows the classes' sizes (section 7). 5. Mapping: each kept class's pose and state are composed with its particles' 2D parameters (map2ptcls on the matched project), and each row selected before takes its particle's state, Euler angles, shift and correlation, in both particle segments: a particle follows its (sub-)class, so a later assignment that moves the class moves it. A row of a rejected class, or without a class of the matching, is deselected until the next publication, and recorded (fit_desel) so that a first-set row is selected again by that publication's merge (section 4, item 5) and judged again. The log counts each state's particles, the rejected and ambiguous classes, and the particles rejected by the fit and the pool model, by the model alone (not applied) and by the fit alone. 6. The local refinement (start_refine, assign/it_<n>/refine3D/): one iteration (maxits=1) of refine3D refine=cont over every selected row from its mapped pose (pgrp=c1, objfun=cc, the pool's mask diameter, the stage's nspace, the 3D jobs' resources, distributed even with one part), each pose refined continuously against its own state's reference within refine=cont's bounds (athres_cont, trs). refine3D reconstructs its starting volumes from the mapped poses (it clears the update counts at its first iteration, so every row with a state is taken), sets its low-pass limits from the FSC, and ends the iteration with the states reconstructed from the refined poses and postprocessed. The states never change: refine=cont searches none, and a multi-state start seeds no labels for it. One iteration bounds the job's cost per publication (refine=cont would otherwise run until its convergence rule, up to maxits=100). - The seeds (prepare_refine_seeds): the half-sets of the project's partition (eo_transaction: one half per micrograph, recorded on the stage's stack rows, so the same for every assignment; refine3D stops on halves that disagree with that record) and the projection direction closest to the mapped pose in the job's grid (set_projs), which refine=cont requires of a seed. A given nspace must be even (the reference spiral's); the commander refuses an odd one. - The states: the job's project holds the states the particles hold, renumbered from 1 (compact_states), since refine3D reconstructs no volume for an empty state; take_refine_result maps them back, so a reference whose sub-classes were all rejected has no volume until a later assignment gives it particles. - When done, its volumes, FSCs and poses become the stage's (section 4, item 8), the stage's rows are written as assign/it_<n>/assignment.simple, the snapshots' source, beside refine3D's own project, which is kept as the job left it, and older assignment folders go. 7. The GUI shows "expanding the classes", "assigning class averages" and "refining the poses locally" while the jobs run, and the refinement's states when done. 8. Tests: test_assignment (sub-classes of one pool class in two states, a badly fitting sub-class rejected and its first-set row selected again by the next publication and judged again, the margin flagged, a later matching moving particles, a matching of other rows refused), test_local_refinement (the assigned rows valid refine=cont seeds; a result taken under the stage's states with an empty state left out, with the refined poses; a result of other rows refused); unit_reconstruction "state assignment rules": test_state_cavgs (a stack and a JPEG per state, of the rejected and of the ambiguous; none for a state without class averages) and test_fit_rules; and unit_pftc_align2D3D "cavgs state match" (reduce_state_matches and matched_pose on fixed numbers; projections of two volumes of Gaussian blobs, each matched to its own with a positive margin at its own direction, in-plane angle and no shift, and a mirrored one at the opposite view).
