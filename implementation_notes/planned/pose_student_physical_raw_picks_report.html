    Report: the pose student with the physical teacher and alignment over the cone¶

    Run pose_raw, 2026-11-16 to 2026-11-18, on the Dell, unattended. Plan: pose_student_physical_raw_picks.md (beside this report; its Progress section holds the detail). Bases: launched on 1ea0f8340; moved to fd9af0add by ruling R4 (every measurement of Phases 0 to 4 was made there); moved to master's 68638be28 by ruling R6 (the final gates, identity and validations). Outcome: adopted under ruling R7's centring policy, uncommitted for review.

    The pose student is now trained on the physical posterior (no tempering), every row audited. Given the teacher's map, predict_poses predicts each particle at its stored shift, then aligns it against the map's reprojections of every direction of the predicted cone and its mirror, at every in-plane rotation over a 30 Å shift disk, and commits the best alignment. The policy, in Hans's words: "centering comes from the solve2D run prior to solve3D on both particles used for training and particles being predicted, shift refinement only enters after the prediction on the reference obtained from the training set, thus mirroring the class student." Against today's route (prediction and a 5 by 5 shift registration at the 2D shifts) the unseen particles' map is as good or finer on every set, and on streptavidin finer than the full search's own. The run's original idea, predicting on raw picks without 2D shifts, was measured and not adopted. validate_pose_student judges against the physical posterior's best pose and reports the unseen map.

    Terms. The pose student is a small network trained on a solve3D run (the teacher). For a new particle it gives a posterior over 1,000 projection directions folded onto mirror pairs: its mode and its cone (the directions holding 99% of the mass, at most 64). The reference is the full search: each unseen particle's best pose under the physical posterior over all directions, every rotation and the 30 Å disk, from the 2D shifts. The arms: C0, today's student and registration at the 2D shifts (the yardstick); R0, the same on raw picks; S1 and SK, the alignment over the mode's mirror pair or over the whole cone; A and P3, today's teacher (the search's tempering factor prob_beta times the particle's temperature T) and the physical one (T alone); P3SK, the physical teacher on raw picks with SK; C3SK, the same models at the 2D shifts with SK (the adopted route). The real sets are bgal, trpv1 and streptavidin, each with three frozen solve3D teachers and three training seeds (nine pairs); the truth sets T200, T1000, T5000 and T are simulated, with eight seeds. Comparisons are paired, with the paired standard error (SE) in brackets; "beyond 2 SE" is the plan's rule. From ruling R5 on, the unseen map's Fourier shell correlation (FSC) decides: against the teacher's map on the real sets, against the truth map on the truth sets.

    1. What was learned¶

    Predict at the 2D shifts, then align over the cone (Phase 4, ruling R7, fd9af0add). C0 → C3SK:

    C0 → C3SK bgal streptavidin trpv1
    unseen map, FSC 0.143 (Å) 5.12 → 4.32 (−0.80, 0.09) 7.02 → 4.90 (−2.12, 0.38) 7.74 → 7.77 (+0.02, 0.12)
    unseen map, FSC 0.5 (Å) 7.86 → 7.59 (−0.27, 0.07) 9.51 → 8.58 (−0.93, 0.11) 9.01 → 8.72 (−0.28, 0.09)
    the full search's own map, FSC 0.143 (Å) 4.31 5.06 7.78
    committed within 6° of the reference (%), diagnostic 68.1 → 90.8 13.5 → 62.1 35.2 → 86.6

    On the truth sets the map is finer at FSC 0.5 by 0.61 to 1.61 Å (T to T200), and the committed direction lies within 6° of the truth for 97 to 99% of the test half (C0: 74 to 93%). The committed shift equals the reference's at the median on bgal and trpv1: from the 2D shifts the alignment over the cone recovers the full search's answer.

    Raw picks lose what the 2D shifts give (Phases 0 and 4). Today's student on raw picks (R0) holds the reference in its cone 25 to 50 points less often than C0 on the real sets. With the physical teacher and SK on raw picks (P3SK) the map was finer than C0's on bgal (−0.49 Å), equal on trpv1, but coarser on streptavidin by 1.91 Å at FSC 0.143 (SE 0.49), so the raw-pick route was not adopted. The same models fed the 2D shifts (P3SK → C3SK) hold the reference in the cone 25 to 53 points more often, and streptavidin's map is 4.0 Å finer: the input shifts alone make the difference.

    Align over the cone (SK), not the mode's pair (S1) (Phase 2, raw picks, today's teacher): SK commits the reference's direction 6 to 50 points more often on every set and gives the finer map everywhere (0.14 to 2.79 Å at FSC 0.143).

    The physical teacher holds or improves the map while agreeing slightly less (Phase 3, raw picks, SK): streptavidin's map finer by 0.54 Å (SE 0.24), bgal and trpv1 within noise; the agreement reads dip by 0.5 to 1.6 points on four sets. This is the class student's pattern: a sharper teacher regularizes, and its maps do not suffer. The audit on real rows found no row off the physical temperature (the largest mass difference 2e-4 of the bound).

    Costs (reported, not gated; per particle, 24 threads, wall): the alignment over the cone 3.2 to 5.0 ms on the real sets from the 2D shifts (3.4 to 6.2 from raw picks), beside the full search's table fill of 2.3 to 4.0 ms and C0's 0.08 to 0.39 ms. Its cost is set by the cone: 44% of bgal's cones, 90% of trpv1's and all of streptavidin's reach the 64-direction cap.

    The truth sets' FSC reads saturate: at 3 Å per pixel FSC 0.143 crosses at Nyquist (6.00 Å) in every run of the aligned arms, and C3SK's 0.5 crossing sits at 6.86 Å in every run. A mean FSC over the shells finer than 20 Å, added as a diagnostic, still separates the arms.

    3. Decisions taken under delegation¶

    • How the reference is computed (decision 2): the dense rows at prob_beta 1 over the 30 Å disk from the 2D shift, because a raw-pick start put the mode within 6° of the truth for 72.5% instead of 99.1% (Phase 0).
    • The point groups and start groups read from the projects' own solve3D jobs (ruling R2; Phase 0).

    6. Left open for the maintainer¶

    • Reported, not gated: the alignment over the cone costs about as much as the full search's table fill (3.2 to 5.0 ms against 2.3 to 4.0 ms per particle), because 44 to 100% of the real-set cones reach the 64-direction cap; the student seeds few real-set particles (0 to 3%). A narrower cone (a smaller cap or a tighter mass) would cut the cost; untested.

    7. Commit message¶

    pose student: physical teacher, alignment over the cone after prediction

The pose student follows the class student's centring policy: training
and predicted particles are centred by the prior solve2D, and shift
refinement enters only after the prediction, over the cone, against the
training set's map.

learn_pose_student trains on the physical posterior (prob_beta 1) and
audits every row against exp(-T (L - Lmin)) within the rounding bound of
the single-precision losses. Given the teacher's map, predict_poses takes
each particle at its stored shift (center=no), predicts the cone and
aligns the particle against the reprojections of every cone direction and
its mirror, every in-plane rotation over a 30 A disk, through the existing
polar shift map (simple_pose_student_align, new, with its tester); the
best alignment is committed with the dynamic centring's state. The 5x5
shift registration is gone. validate_pose_student takes a frozen teacher
(dir_refine), predicts its unseen half at the 2D shifts, judges against
the physical posterior's best pose and reports the unseen maps and the
costs. The distributed master's sigma2 bootstrap now matches a
shared-memory run.

Measured (doc/implementation_notes/planned/pose_student_physical_raw_picks.md):
the unseen maps are as good or finer than today's route on every real and
simulated set (streptavidin 7.02 -> 4.90 A at FSC 0.143); the alignment
over the cone beats the mode's pair; the physical teacher's maps are as
good or finer; predicting on raw picks was measured and not adopted.
Off-state identity and the class student unchanged.
