    One Levenberg-Marquardt for the library: review report (Phases 0-4)¶

    Status, 2026-11-18: the run lm_optimizer (arun, FWM-S116118, unattended) carried out Phases 0-4 of lm_optimizer.md on base aae467bc7, under rulings R1-R10.

    • Phases 0-3 built the engine, the factory's 'lm' and the clients' curvatures.
    • Phase 4 did the following:
    • timed both solvers quietly;
    • cached the polar joint curvature (R7);
    • fixed the factory LM's re-evaluation after callbacks (R10);
    • judged each client by R6, on its comparison fixture and on the production-shaped fixtures the tests already have.
    • The switches. Two clients switch to 'lm': the polar joint (shift and in-plane) mode and quality_logistic. Three keep 'lbfgsb', because they are slower under 'lm' on noisy data: the polar shift mode, ftexp_shsrch and the continuous CTF fit.
    • Still open. The real-data reads are left to Cyril and Hans (R8, sections 7 and 8). The plan stays in planned/ until they are in (R5).

    Nothing is committed. The plan's Progress section holds the detail; evidence paths are under /Users/elmlundho/agent_work/arun/lm_optimizer.

    1. What was learned¶

    • One engine carries every LM client, the Cartesian route byte for byte (refine=cont, Release and Debug, rechecked after every phase, Phase 4 included).
    • The step control as extracted did not suit every client (the Phase 2 stop). The relative stops became options that take the factory's factr/pgtol (R3), and exact null coordinates take a zero step (R4).
    • Every client's Gauss-Newton curvature equals its Hessian at a zero residual (2e-12 to 9e-5 relative). This includes the polar joint mode after R7's cache.
    • Noise-free fixtures flatter Gauss-Newton.
    • On Phase 3's comparison fixtures (noise-free copies, zero residual), 'lm' is a Newton method. It wins every client: 0.29-0.63 of L-BFGS-B's median time per solve, quiet, far beyond the A-A spread.
    • On noisy data the Gauss-Newton matrix lacks the residual's second-order term, and LM converges linearly to the same factr stop. On the production-shaped fixtures the tests already have, three clients were slower:
      • the polar shift mode in the prob_neigh case of cont_refine3D_1jxy and the fills bench;
      • ftexp_shsrch and the CTF fit in simulated_workflow_6vxx's motion-correction and CTF steps.
    • The polar joint mode was no slower there. quality_logistic has the exact Hessian (Fisher plus ridge), so it is unaffected.
    • A defect in opt_lm, now fixed (R10). After every accepted step, opt_lm re-evaluated whenever a callback existed. In the polar alternating search the callback changes the objective only when the rotation changes.
    • With the fix, the fill searches' evaluations per search under 'lm' fall from 17.7 / 12.0 / 11.2 / 9.6 to 11.5 / 6.5 / 6.1 / 5.3 (iterations 1-4; L-BFGS-B 7.2 / 6.5 / 6.6 / 5.5).
    • The shift mode's thread-second excess falls from 36 / 29 / 26 / 22 % to 34 / 20 / 17 / 13 %.
    • What remains is more evaluations in iteration 1, which fits Gauss-Newton on large residuals. Later it is more time per evaluation at equal counts, where the likely cause is the all-rotation scoring the callback does at each accepted step (not measured separately).
    • The CTF cost is several |sin| periods per 0.1 µm wide at 5 Å. The continuous fit is a local refinement of the grid search.

    3. Decisions taken under delegation¶

    Each is recorded, with its reason, in the plan's Progress entry of its phase.

    • Phases 0-3:
    • the CTF baseline on mini_stream, the identity fixture, the exclusions and the probe method (Phase 0);
    • the negligible_step hook, the seed handling and N20's deletion, and export-built identity trees (Phase 1);
    • explicit factr/pgtol in L1-L6, and the L2 control at L2's tolerances (Phase 2);
    • each client's curvature, the polar log-form objective and the test seams (Phase 3).
    • Phase 4:
    • R6's "the client's tester fixtures" includes the production-shaped fixtures the tests already have.
      • The fixtures:
      • for the polar search, the prob_neigh case on cont_refine3D_1jxy's fixture and the polar_matching_bench fills bench;
      • for ftexp_shsrch and the CTF fit, simulated_workflow_6vxx's motion-correction and CTF steps;
      • for quality_logistic, its cavg quality nano suite.
      • The rule: a client switches when it is faster beyond the A-A spread on its comparison fixture and not slower beyond the spread on any of these.
      • The reason: the comparison fixtures are noise-free, and R6's intent is "good enough and fast" in use. Every negative is undone by one constant.
    • Each client has its own constant (decision 7). The polar joint mode is its own client (its own specify), so the polar constant is split: SHSRCH_SOLVER for the shift mode and SHSRCH_JOINT_SOLVER for the joint mode.
    • R7's form. Per solve, load_ref_joint_curv_8 computes |REF|², Im(conj(REF)·REF′) and |REF′|², with one FFT batch pair for REF′. Each evaluation sums them with the analytic shift-phase derivative and the CTF² at the rotated position, linearly interpolated.
      • It equals Phase 3's FFT form at whole rotation indices up to aliasing (4th digit), and is positive semi-definite in between.
      • The shift-mode curvature, constant over the shift, is formed once per rotation of a solve (decision 6).
    • Medians. The testers time repeated solves and report medians: pftc 20 × 3, CTF 5 × 8, ftexp 100, logistic 20.
      • "Faster than the A-A spread" means that the smaller of the two runs' gaps exceeds the larger of the two solvers' run-to-run differences.
      • On production-shaped fixtures the measure is thread-seconds of the affected stage, or wall time of a one-thread program run in a worker process.
    • R10's tester count. It uses private profile counters of pftc_shsrch_grad, read through the existing test-only seam submodule (shsrch_callback_profile).
    • The cc shift-prior stop. Under 'lm' the shift mode stops on a cc objective with a shift prior. That combination is unreachable in production, and the shift mode stays on 'lbfgsb'.

    4. Deviations from the plan¶

    • Phase 2 stopped once (R3, R4). Phase 1's rg exit holds as a word search, N20's stages i and j were deleted, and dose series manifest does not run the Guinier fit.
    • The R9 re-time of Phase 3's code printed means. The verdicts use the median re-time taken after the tester change and R7.
    • Decision 8's real-data A/B in this run was replaced by R5/R8.
    • R6 applied with production-shaped fixtures (decision 1 above). By the comparison fixtures alone all five clients would have switched. A first B build did switch them all; it was measured, and three of the switches were reverted.
    • Timing under other people's load. Some timings ran under someone else's load bursts (compiles, a load of 127 at 12:46). The decisive pairs were repeated or agree run to run, and the uptimes are recorded beside each.

    10. Commit message (for the maintainer, if the change is taken as one)¶

    lm_optimizer: one Levenberg-Marquardt engine for the library

simple_lm_solver (lm_problem, lm_solve) owns the LM step control once: the Cartesian pose solve
(refine_joint, refine_shift) runs on it byte-identically, the factory gains 'lm' (opt_lm,
fdfcurvfun_8, L-BFGS-B's factr/pgtol as its stops, a re-evaluation only when a callback changed the
objective), the five factory clients gain verified Gauss-Newton curvatures (the polar joint one
cached per reference), and the dose-series Guinier fit runs on it. cont_route/shift_then_joint are
retired. By ruling R6 the polar joint search and quality_logistic default to 'lm'; the polar shift
search, ftexp_shsrch and the continuous CTF fit, slower under 'lm' on noisy data, keep 'lbfgsb'.
One private constant per client switches it. Rulings R1-R10.
