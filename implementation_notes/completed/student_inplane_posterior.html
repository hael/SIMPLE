    The students learn the in-plane posterior¶

    Archived in completed/ on 2026-11-17 by maintainer decision. The status and open items below are as last written.

    Status, 2026-11-15: delivered: P adopted for the class student, 2026-11-15; pose student unchanged (ruling R7). P is the in-plane marginal at the physical temperature as the teacher (no prob_beta), with the log-sum-exp readout and the rotation loss, and validate_class_student judges against the physical posterior's best class. M and R were rejected: by decision 7 in Phase 3, and R again under ruling R4. The report is student_inplane_posterior_report.md beside this plan, and the plan stays in planned/ until the maintainer moves it. Bases: 86babf86b for Phases 0 to 3 and R4's first audit (ruling R1), 4282d58a3 for R4's readings (ruling R5), 603c44490 for R6's readings and the adoption (rulings R6 and R7). Supersedes: none. Depends on: the in-plane posterior weighting in 2D and 3D (landed; validated by Hans on solve2D and solve3D), the pose student (completed/pose_student_training_speed_report.md) and the class student (completed/class_student_2D.md), which share one core (simple_polar_student).

    1. Why¶

    Both students learn from a teacher row that gives each direction (3D) or class (2D) the loss at one in-plane rotation: score_ref returns one distance and one rotation per reference, and get_posterior_row turns those into the row's posterior. The students' readout matches that: for each direction or class it takes the maximum over the rotation grid, so the training signal flows through one rotation, and the rotation the students read out is never trained. The refinement no longer works that way: it inserts each particle under its in-plane posterior (inpl_quadrature, whose lnz is the log of the sum over rotations, and inpl_view_weights, the in-plane marginals). Hans (2026-11-15): the in-plane posterior should improve the network.

    Three expected gains, each read separately: - a cleaner teacher: the marginal over rotations is the posterior of a direction or class; the loss at one rotation out of a couple of hundred adds selection noise at low SNR. Better-calibrated targets should raise the share seeded at the calibrated target, the students' weakest read (pose student about 0.42 on bgal); - a matching readout: a log-sum-exp over the rotations at a learned temperature in place of the maximum, so the student computes the teacher's quantity and every rotation carries gradient; - rotation supervision: the teacher's in-plane posterior at the top directions or classes as a target, the first supervision of the rotation the students read out.

    2. Contract¶

    • Off state: refine2D and refine3D are byte-identical at one thread with a fixed seed. The teacher's marginal and its in-plane rows are computed only when a learn program keeps its rows (keep_batch_rows).
    • The experiment lives outside the tree until the verdict: every variant, the teacher's extension included, is a patch on a git archive export of the base. Only what is adopted enters the tree, in Phase 4.
    • The networks keep their symmetries: rotation equivariance on the rotation grid, mirror invariance (3D), the conjugation symmetry, bit-identical training at any thread count.
    • No new command-line key or parameter. If adopted, the row file and both model files raise their versions; other versions are refused, as now.
    • Neither student is closed by cost on small data sets (Hans, 2026-11-15): costs are reported, not judged, here.

    3. Decisions¶

    1. (2026-11-15, Hans) The in-plane posterior for both students, the 2D class student and the pose student, through their shared core.
    2. The teacher's marginal. For every reference of a kept row, score_ref (and the 2D table's scorer) also returns the log-sum over the rotation grid of exp(-T (d_r - d_min)) at the particle's temperature T, the quantity inpl_quadrature calls lnz, at the shift the fill scored at. The row's posterior then reads p(ref) ∝ exp(lnz(ref) - T d_min(ref)), the in-plane marginal, as inpl_view_weights forms it. The loss at the one rotation stays in the row for the base route. Made precise in Phase 1 (ruling R2, from the code): T is the temperature the table both draws its in-plane rotations at and reads its rows at, the tempering factor times the particle's own (prob_beta × T, the base targets' too). The fill already computes each entry's whole in-plane row to draw its rotation; the marginal's route computes that row once, draws the same rotation with the same keyed variate (the entry unchanged, bit for bit) and sums it, so the calculator is untouched (simple_eul_prob_marginal). An entry the fill refined in shift takes the row at its refined shift. The row's losses become d = d_min - lnz / T, whose tempered weight is the marginal, and the row record's mode rotation becomes the mode reference's in-plane mode (its most probable rotation, no longer one draw). The drawn losses stay in the table for its own assignment; the rows file carries the training losses with lnz and d_min beside them. Revised in Phase 4 (ruling R2, on the audit ruling R4 asked for): the teacher's in-plane weighting must be the refinement's, and it was not. The learn programs run at prob_beta = 0.3, so the marginal above was summed at 0.3 T, while the in-plane posterior insertion weighs every row at the particle's physical temperature T (3D inpl_pose_row, 2D insert_inplane) and, in 3D, adds the shift prior's term to each pose's view weight (inpl_prior_penalty); the sum was also taken in double where inpl_quadrature rounds each term to single precision first. Now each entry's row is drawn as before (the table's entries unchanged, bit for bit) and weighed by inpl_quadrature itself (an optional output post, the row's posterior, added; no existing result changes) at the insertion's temperature and at the shift the insertion would take the entry at (the refined entry's, else the seed as record_seed_shift records it); in 3D the entry's shift-prior term is inpl_prior_penalty's at that shift and centre, in 2D none (the 2D insertion has no view weights). A reference's training loss is d = d_min - (lnz - pen) / T, whose weight at T is the insertion's view weight exp(lnz - T d_min - pen); the row is read at prob_beta T as A's drawn losses are, so the class targets are the insertion's view weights tempered as the table tempers its rows. The rotation targets (decision 3) are the insertion's in-plane posteriors. The learn programs audit this on their first batch of real rows against the insertion's own route, and the targets' mapping on every row (Progress, Phase 4).
    3. The rotation targets. For the KMAX_INPL_ROW = 8 references of largest marginal mass, the in-plane posterior over the teacher's rotation grid, binned onto the student's rotation grid by angle. In 3D a mirror pair's targets follow the readout's rule (the unmirrored direction's rotation, or the reflected one mapped through the pair's frame angle); Phase 1's tester pins the mapping at 0, 75 and 180°, as the HHCNN tester pins the readout.
