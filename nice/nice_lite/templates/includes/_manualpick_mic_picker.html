{% comment %} _manualpick_mic_picker.html — full-size display of the micrograph currently selected in _manualpick_mic_selector.html, filling its container. Clicking the image (pickMicCoordinate) converts the click to mic-scale coordinates and adds it as a pick. Picks are kept in manualPickedBoxes, keyed by micrograph.i, so they survive switching to another mic and back. Box size/shape are controlled by _manualpick_pick_controls.html, included in the header below. {% endcomment %}
