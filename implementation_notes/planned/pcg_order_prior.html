    The PCG order prior: one solvent and detergent prior for soluble and membrane proteins¶

    Status¶

    In the loop, 2026-11-08, untested on real data. pcg_order=yes (with rec_backend=pcg) puts a ridge on what lacks NU evidence of order into the PCG re-solve of the base pair. Its ordered fraction and strength are chosen per state and iteration in closed form. solve3D starts PCG and the prior with the gold-standard phase (stage 6). Code: simple_pcg_order_prior (src/main/volume/), the PCG master (simple_rec3D_pcg_strategy), the operator (reconstructor_pcg). The offline program simple_exec prg=order_prior (simple_order_prior_offline) checks the closed-form choice against full solves of a Fourier surrogate of the operator. Unit sub-suites: unit_reconstruction suite=order_prior and suite=order_prior_offline.

    The order prior replaces the solvent prior pcg_solvent (decision log 2026-09-18 to 09-22) and no longer uses its density weight (section 3). Sections 9 to 11 record the offline rounds that shaped it. This note takes over the first step of the cross-validated NU-evidence threshold: the evidence mask is the prior's order weight.

    1. The problem¶

    solve3D with gold-standard matching from stage 6 gives usable maps of small membrane proteins. For the smallest ones, though, the map does not separate ordered protein from disordered detergent: the two are connected in the map and in every mask derived from it. The solvent prior flattened solvent with a ridge outside a density weight w, a logistic on |x| smoothed at max(8 A, 2 x FSC0.143) and Otsu-thresholded. At that scale a micelle is as dense as the protein it surrounds, so w is about 1 on both and the micelle stays. The goal is an alignment reference without the micelle. The final reconstruction never sees the prior.

    2. What separates protein from detergent: order, not density¶

    The NU evidence margin is about zero on a micelle and positive on ordered protein. The margin is the gain of the best finer candidate over the 20 A candidate in cross-half prediction, smoothed at amsklp (algorithm). A micelle is reproducible at 20 A and at nothing finer.

    The order weight w_ord is a logistic on the margin. Its threshold is the (1-f) quantile over the support, where f is the ordered fraction; its width is the spread of the margin below the threshold. There is no binary MRF and no morphology, because a prior weight needs no hard boundary. The weight is built from both halves, like the NU label field, so a pair it shaped never reaches an FSC.

    3. The model¶

    penalty = lambda h (1 - w_ord) x^2,     the same weight for both halves

    h is the operator's real-space diagonal, the data precision per voxel (the mean of D over all shells). In these units one lambda means the same thing for every molecule: a fully penalized voxel shrinks by 1/(1 + lambda). A strength relative to the low-band data scale, the first version, acted 0.46 to 11.8 times as strongly in h depending on the shape of D(k), so each molecule needed its own value (Hans, 2026-11-08: "we cannot design a system where we need to set a different regularization parameter for different kinds of molecules").

    The prior penalizes whatever lacks evidence of order: solvent and disordered density such as detergent. The density weight w of pcg_solvent was in the penalty (1 - w w_ord) until 2026-11-08. It leaked into the protein: 12-25 % of the core's voxels had 1 - w > 0.2 (surface, thin and low-density features near its Otsu threshold), which a strength that removes the micelle cuts to under half. The maps looked heavily low-pass filtered (Hans). The order weight alone puts that penalty above 0.2 on 0.7-7 % of the core and still penalizes the solvent (mean w_ord 0.04-0.19 there). The density weight now only defines the regions of section 4.

    4. The choice of f and lambda_rel¶

    Two regions are fixed from the prior-free pair before the scan:

    • Protein core: support voxels that are dense (mean of the two halves' density weights >= 1/2) and whose NU label is at or finer than 1.5 x FSC=0.143.
    • Peri: the dense voxels outside the core.

    The signal of a region is its cross-half sum of even x odd, in which the halves' independent noise cancels. For every f in ORDER_FRAC_GRID (1 % to 64 %) and lambda_rel in ORDER_LAMBDA_GRID (0.1 to 20), the selector predicts the signal each region keeps. Among the settings that keep at least 95 % of the core's signal (choose_order_setting, shared with the offline check):

    • with dense density around the core (a micelle, at least 1000 voxels), the most peri signal removed;
    • with none (a soluble protein), the strongest lambda_rel, at its smallest fraction: solvent flattening;
    • none qualifies, or the core is too small to check: no prior this iteration.

    Auto is the default with pcg_order=yes (Hans, 2026-11-08, on the auto maps of section 11: "absolutely fine"; to be revised on the loop tests). An explicit pcg_order_lambda, for testing, is applied as given every iteration, at the smallest fraction that keeps 95 % of the core (else the largest fraction).

    Whole-map cross-half agreement cannot choose the removal. It counts every reproducible voxel as signal, detergent included, so any removal costs it and it always prefers to keep the micelle (Hans, 2026-11-07). Two guards failed before the magnitude guard on the core signal, which removing noise does not lower and removing density does:

    • Core objective within one paired SE: it admitted a weak ridge on the core itself.
    • Removal within one SE: solver-level differences of a fraction of a percent are many SE.

    Neither score says anything about alignment. References were once multiplied by an envelope and lost pose discrimination on PfCRT (2026-09-02). The ridge attenuates rather than zeroes, and only the loop can test it.

    5. In the loop¶

    base solve (prior-free) -> FSC, w per half
  -> select_order_prior (closed form) on the previous iteration's
     <vol>_nu_evidence.mrc and <vol>_nu_locres.mrc
  -> ridge on both operators -> the same cold solve again (the order pair)
  -> NU on the base pair, its label field composed from the prior'd pair
    • Closed form, banded: each half is split into octave bands of Fourier shells ([0,1], [2,3], [4,7], ...). Per voxel, band b shrinks by D_b / (D_b + lambda h (1 - w_ord)), where D_b is the operator's diagonal over the band's shells (reconstructor_pcg%get_shell_diagonal). The predicted half is the sum of the shrunk bands. Only the dense voxels are scanned, and no solve is repeated. One scalar h for all shells, the first version, missed where the core's signal sits in D(k) (section 11).
    • Lag one: the NU evidence and labels the selector reads come from the previous iteration of the same state and box. The NU run of the current iteration needs the re-solved pair, and the re-solve needs the prior. The NU state filter writes the evidence map only under pcg_order=yes, so the first iteration with the key, and the first at a new box, run without the prior and say so in the log. Without NU filtering the prior never acts; a standalone reconstruct3D uses it only on evidence an earlier run left in its directory.
    • Gold standard and outputs: the prior-free pair keeps the FSC, the NU competition, the evidence and the _unfil files. The prior'd pair is written as <half>_order; its ML replay is the state map, and the NU references are composed from it. The weight is written as pcg_order_weight_stateNN.mrc, overwritten every iteration.
    • Final reconstruction: prior-free (simple_final_rec forwards no pcg_order).
    • Exclusions: atm_prior=yes and pcg_order=yes are exclusive. reconstruct3D_wave and refine3D_states_cavgs refuse the prior.
    • solve3D: gridding through stage 5, PCG and the prior from stage 6 (PCG_START_STAGE), independently of gold_std_stage. The PCG accumulation and solves are the cost, so starting them late is the saving.
    • Trailing chains: they are per backend. The stage-boundary reconstruction takes the next stage's backend and seeds the PCG chain at full mass. The prior acts after the blend, on the restored pair.

    6. The offline surrogate of the PCG re-solve¶

    Offline there are no accumulators, so the operator is modelled. The PCG base operator is close to Fourier-diagonal: CTF-squared, sigma2-whitened sampling density. For an unregularized reconstruction its diagonal is the inverse of the half-map noise power, which the pair measures:

    sigma_n^2(k) = N <|F(E - O)|^2>_shell / 2     (fft divides by N)
D(k)         = 1 / sigma_n^2(k)
(F^-1 D F + lambda h diag(1 - w_ord)) x = F^-1 D F x0     per half
h            = mean D over all shells

    The shared CG engine (pcg_solve) solves it with a two-region preconditioner, sqrt(v) D^-1 sqrt(v) + sqrt(1-v) (D + lambda h)^-1 sqrt(1-v) with v = w_ord, which is exact inside a region of one kind.

    With D constant, the operator reduces to the closed form (unit tests). Where D(k) falls with k, a ridge acts as a local low-pass rather than a mask. The re-solve then keeps the low-resolution density the data determine and suppresses the rest, which the closed form cannot see. Shells beyond the last one with data are held at zero; such shells occur in a pair Fourier-padded from a crop box.

    7. The program¶

    simple_exec prg=order_prior vol1=<odd>_unfil.mrc vol2=<even>_unfil.mrc smpd=<A> mskdiam=<A> \
    [outvol=<name>.mrc] [amsklp=8] [pcg_order_lambda=<x>] nthr=<n>

    Feed the unregularized pair: an ML-regularized pair flattens both the noise model and the evidence. mskdiam must leave solvent around the micelle, because the NU noise level and the evidence need it.

    The program runs in this order:

    1. The density weights (the regions).
    2. NU on the pair, capped at FSC/1.5 as in assembly, giving the evidence margin and the label field.
    3. The closed-form choice and its tables.
    4. The same grid solved through the surrogate (warm-started along lambda_rel), chosen by the same rule.
    5. Agreement or disagreement of the two choices.
    6. The surrogate pair at the closed-form choice, which is what the loop ships.

    Outputs, with <name> from outvol (default order_prior):

    file content
    <name>_base.mrc, _base_nu_filt.mrc the input pair average, and its NU filtering
    <name>_nu_evidence.mrc, _nu_locres.mrc the smoothed evidence margin; the label field's local resolution
    <name>_density_weight_even/odd.mrc per-half density weights (they define the regions)
    <name>_order_weight.mrc w_ord at the chosen fraction (1 minus it is what the ridge acts on)
    <name>_order_even/odd.mrc, _order.mrc the prior'd pair at the closed-form choice
    <name>_order_nu_filt.mrc the base pair's label field composed from the prior'd pair: the reference the matcher would see

    8. What to read¶

    • In the loop: one PCG ORDER PRIOR line per state and iteration, giving the ordered fraction, lambda_rel, the peri signal removed and the core kept, or prior off this iteration (no evidence yet, or no setting keeps the core). Read it next to the _order halves and the order weight. Diagnostics added 2026-11-13 to find why the NU references looked low-passed with automsk=no:
    • the closed-form tables;
    • the spread of 1 - w_ord over the core, the dense density around it and the rest of the domain;
    • the choice a guard on every octave band to FSC=0.143 would make (the loop still uses the total);
    • after the re-solve, a table per octave band: the band's share of the core signal, D_b/h, lambda_rel/(D_b/h) (a voxel with penalty p keeps 1/(1 + p x that) of the band), and the core kept and density around it removed, predicted and measured on <half>_order against the base pair.

    pcg_order_regions_stateNN.mrc maps the regions (2 core, 1 dense around it). - Offline: the two CLOSED FORM tables and the two operator tables, then the operator at the closed-form choice removes ... keeps ... and whether the two choices agree. The BAND GUARD choice and the per-band table (measured on the surrogate's pair) are printed as in the loop. A steep D(k) (streptavidin, embb, exp_gate) is where they can differ.

    Two failures:

    • The scalar h misses where the core's signal sits. The core's cross-half signal is at low and mid resolution. Where D is below h there (tat at 8-17 A, PorF below 40 A), the operator removes much more of the core than predicted. Where D falls steeply, it removes less.
    • The fallback broke the guard. The weakest strength was applied even when it failed the core guard: streptav, embb and exp_gate would have lost 20-35 % of the core. With steep D (low band 9-15 x the mean), lambda_rel 0.5 is already 5-7 x the real-space diagonal.

    12. With automsk=yes (2026-11-13)¶

    refine3D_local with pcg_order=yes automsk=yes cut PorF in half. What survived had the best resolution Hans had seen on it. The solve support was the density envelope of the published reference, which under ml_reg=yes is the ML replay of the prior'd pair. The ridge's removals fell below the envelope threshold and were solved as zero from then on. The support is now made from the previous iteration's prior-free pair, for every automsk run (reconstruct3D_pcg_policy.md, section 3). The division of labour:

    • the envelope, from prior-free data, owns the solvent as a hard support;
    • the order prior owns the disordered density inside it (the micelle) as a soft ridge, its fraction and evidence null taken inside the envelope;
    • the NU references are masked by the current prior-free pair's envelope.

    13. Next¶

    The question is whether the prior improves the alignment, not what it deletes. From gold_std_stage (default stage 6) the halves align independently, and the final map is prior-free, so the loop judges the prior. The order weight is shared by the halves, like the NU label field: the evidence of order is a cross-half quantity, and without the per-half density weight (section 3) nothing in the penalty is per half. It is smooth (8 A, logistic), a shared soft solvent mask rather than strict two-fold independence; accepted for the tests (Hans, 2026-11-08). An FSC gain only at low resolution would point to it. Strict independence would need quarter reconstructions, each half's weight from the evidence between its two quarters.

    Test on PfCRT, PorF and streptavidin in auto (pcg_order=yes alone), the setting whose offline maps Hans approved. Each dataset gets a pcg_order=no arm on the same build, since PCG now starts at stage 6. Compare the final prior-free maps and their FSC, the stage 6-8 references, the per-iteration PCG ORDER PRIOR lines, and the run time.
