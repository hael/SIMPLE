    Ghostbuster's operators in SIMPLE: report of the run¶

    Run "wave_operators", 2026-10-23 to 2026-10-24, unattended on the Dell (Intel Core Ultra 9 285, 24 cores, no GPU), against base 5111068bf. The plan is ghostbuster_operators.md beside this report; its section 8, "Progress", holds the detail and the evidence of every phase. Paths of the evidence are under the run directory /home/elmlundho/agent_runs/wave_operators; each phase's diff is review/phase_N.diff there. Nothing is committed: the change is the working tree against the base.

    What the run delivered¶

    SIMPLE can now predict a particle image from a 3D electrostatic potential with wave optics, under four models (projection, first Born and Rytov on the Ewald paraboloid, and multislice), and has the exact, hand-written derivative of that prediction. It is built on SIMPLE's own machinery: the Kaiser-Bessel (KB) gather and scatter of the PCG (preconditioned conjugate gradients) reconstruction's padded lattice, SIMPLE's CTF, its particle read path, its PCG start solve, density and maximum-likelihood (ML) prior. specter, the package that contains Ghostbuster, served only as the oracle that wrote reference files; nothing in the repository calls it.

    On top of the operators:

    • reconstruct3D_wave, a new shared-memory program, reconstructs half maps from a project with known poses and CTF under any of the four models, by nonlinear conjugate gradients from the flat PCG map.
    • simulate_scattering now draws its images with the same operators, with corrected sign conventions and the defocus at the specimen midplane (section "simulate_scattering" below).

    What it buys, on simulated data with a known answer:

    • On a 300 A ribosome at 100 kV, where the Ewald curvature limits a flat map beyond 4.7 A, Rytov with the data's curvature sign raises the agreement with the truth between 3 and 2.4 A (the Fourier shell correlation, FSC, of the map against the truth) from 0.66 to 0.81; the wrong sign drops it to 0.21. specter's converged reconstruction reaches 0.83.
    • On an ordinary protein at 300 kV all models agree, Rytov slightly ahead (0.762 against 0.733 between 3 and 2 A), level with specter.
    • On a 469-atom Pt nanoparticle every map finds all 469 atoms; the wave models place them more accurately (0.029 to 0.030 A against 0.035 A) and repair part of the band near the particle's own curvature limit (about 1 A). specter's own multislice matches SIMPLE's over the last band (0.801 both).

    Cost: a Rytov gradient pass costs 1.7 to 3 times a PCG accumulation; the ribosome's wave reconstruction took about 9 min against 1.5 min for PCG at 24 threads. Multislice costs about 3 s a particle on one core at box 160, which suits nanoparticle sets, not 100 000-particle cryo-EM sets.

Reference cases and three ground-truth datasets (protein, ribosome, Pt nanoparticle) simulated by specter's multislice with their truth. Both findings about simulate_scattering (inverted phase, exit-face defocus) confirmed with numbers.
    • Phase 1, foundations (review/phase_1.diff).     • Phase 2, the single-scatter operator (review/phase_2.diff). wave_imaging (the 2D chain of one particle) and wave_projector (the operator over a particle set) for projection, first Born and Rytov, forward, linearisation and adjoint, threaded and reproducible; the fold of the scatter accumulator moved into the lattice.     simulate_scattering: what changed, for its author¶

    Ruben wrote simulate_scattering on 2026-10-23 (commit f4cac4773); this section is for him. Ruben, your simulator was the starting point of the 2D wave engine; the run moved it onto the shared operator. Your note ghostbuster_scattering_simulator.md carries the full account in a dated section; in short:

    • The images were those of -V. The propagator exp(+i pi lambda d k^2) and the lens exp(+i chi) were both conjugated relative to Kirkland while the transmission was exp(+i sigma V), so a positive potential came out bright at underfocus and the multiple-scattering terms were those of the negated potential. Against specter on the same potential and pose (Phase 0), the simulator's image of V correlated with specter's image of -V at 0.98 to 1.000 and with that of V at -0.90 to -0.93. The absorption had the right sign. Kirkland's signs are now used throughout.
    • Defocus referred to the exit face, half a box below the midplane that SIMPLE's CTF fits and specter use (the best match was 48.6 to 51.4 A off for a 48 A half depth). It now refers to the midplane.
    • Keys. scattering_model became wave_model (with projection and rytov added), ewald selects the curvature sign or, for multislice, the beam direction, and absorption is retired for fraca: with the object potential (sqrt(1 - alpha^2) + i alpha) V the weak-phase limit of every model is SIMPLE's CTF with amplitude contrast alpha, tested to 1e-5, so they are one quantity. No aliases (release 4).
    • No real-space rotation. Every model samples the potential's transform on the PCG lattice; multislice gathers the rotated spectrum cube from it. The data are made by the exact operator the reconstruction inverts. rotvol had not corrected the KB window's roll-off (8 to 10% weaker off the centre).
    • Lens and geometry. The lens is SIMPLE's CTF phase, with a phase plate acting on the scattered waves only; shifts and phase shifts of the orientation table now apply.
    • Code and tests. simple_scattering_simulator.f90 and its tester are gone: the FFT buffers became wave_field, the lens and detector wave_imaging (detect_intensity holds your Poisson and Gaussian noise), multislice wave_multislice, the Poisson sampler poisson_rnd. Your multislice limits (zero, constant real and constant absorptive potentials) are kept; the first-Born constant-potential check gave way to the weak-phase identity and to first Born being Rytov's linearisation, and the vacuum is tested for every model. New tests can see the sign and the defocus reference (a weak sheet 16 A downstream images at df + 16 A, not df - 16 A). The FFTW plans now use SIMPLE's one planner lock (the unnamed critical section).

    The PDB route, the potential route, the dose and noise, the defaults and the outputs are as you made them. The program and its tests ran on Linux (Oracle Linux 8, gfortran 15) throughout this run.

    Decisions taken under delegation¶

    Each is recorded, with its reason, in the plan section named.

    • The FFTW planner lock is the unnamed critical section, not a named one (section 4.2, Phase 1).
    • The window helpers, plane_loc and rim_free are module procedures of pcg_lattice, not type-bound, to leave flex_pcg_t unchanged (Phase 1).
    • ewald_curv refuses a paraboloid more than 0.49 padded voxel past the Nyquist sphere (Phase 1).
    • Rytov exponentiates at the exit face, as specter and the approximation's definition do (section 3.2, Phase 2); the midplane differed by 2 to 17%.
    • A phase plate's shift leaves the unscattered beam alone, T(0) = 1 (section 3.2, Phase 2).
    • The wavelength stays SIMPLE's CTF value, shared by design (section 3.2, Phase 2).
    • Oversampling stays 2 with no key; the wave operator keeps the PCG lattice's three-tap window, and the existing key winsz serves a caller who wants seven taps (Phases 2 and 3).
    • specter's positive curvature sign is one voxel of defocus off the symmetric reference; SIMPLE keeps the symmetric one (section 3.1, Phase 2).
    • The program's design details (section 3.6, Phase 3): the prior in volts carries boxpd^3; the start is the base PCG solve shrunk by the ML prior in closed form, written beside the wave maps; the Wiener filter is undone per shell; the step subset is 5% of a half within 256 to 1000 particles; the repeated passes use the in-memory pass buffer; pcg_mskfile is the support key; halves from ensure_field_halves.
    • ewald defaults to no for projection and neg otherwise; projection with a curvature and multislice without one are refused (Phases 3 and 4).
    • wave_multislice is a per-thread type run by wave_projector as its fourth model, not a second extension of the lattice with its own batch frame (section 4.1, Phase 4); its constant is WAVE_MSLICE, since Fortran is case-insensitive and WAVE_MULTISLICE would name the type.
    • The ground-truth sets render each particle from its rotated atoms, are normalised by the dose so the gain is exact, and ship SIMPLE's orientation table beside their star files; the ribosome set propagates unpadded (Phase 0).
    • specter reconstructs Rytov with its surface sampling (its real-space rotation would have taken 8 to 33 h); for multislice its real-space rotation is the only mode (Phases 3 and 4).
    • The ML prior is left as the plan fixes it, from the flat start pair, so that Phase 3's results stand (Phase 4); see "Open".
    • This phase added doc/policies/3D/reconstruct3D_pcg_policy.md to the file table, since the routines it names moved in Phase 1 (the item Phase 1 left for Phase 5).

    Deviations from the plan¶

    • The lattice's accuracy is ten times the plan's estimate. Section 6 assumed about 1e-3; the three-tap window interpolates a section to 0.7 to 1% of the image contrast for content off the box centre (Phases 1 and 2). The parity tolerance was measured at zero curvature, where both packages are exact by another route (8.2e-3 of the image contrast, 1.3e-2 of a gradient), and the golden test's bound set at 1.25 times that; a seven-tap window brings the operator to 4e-5 to 9e-4. Far below any particle's noise.
    • specter at general poses under multislice. specter's multislice exists only with its real-space trilinear rotation, whose own error (12.6 to 19% against its surface sampling) bounds parity at general poses; parity was established at quarter turns (to 7e-5 and 2e-3) and the gradient's 30% difference traced to specter's real-space gradient (Phase 4).
    • The orientation test's criterion is a factor of four between the wrong and the right sign, not ten: the two signs differ by only 2% at box 24 (Phase 4, recorded before relied on).
    • The particle cache is not used (it is two-dimensional only); the pass buffer serves the repeated passes (section 4.3).
    • The nanoparticle set's expectation ("multislice recovers atoms the linear map misplaces or misses") did not arise: at this dose every map finds every atom; the difference is in position error and in the band near the curvature limit (Phase 4).
    • simulate_scattering kept rotvol for multislice between Phases 3 and 4, then lost it as planned.

    Evidence against the baseline¶

    Open for the maintainer¶

    • The prior near the Ewald limit. The ML prior comes from the flat start pair's FSC, which collapses exactly where the flat model fails, so it is strongest there. On the nanoparticle a ten times weaker prior (tau=10) recovers most of that band (truth FSC 0.47 to 0.50 at 1.12 A against 0.18 to 0.20); rebuilding the prior from the wave pair under the present prior would not, since that pair's FSC is held near zero there. A prior from a weakly regularised first wave solve is the candidate.
    • The ribosome's last band stays 0.02 below specter's (0.810 against 0.833) while the half maps agree: an error common to both halves, in the observation preparation shared with PCG (edge taper at large delocalisation, background-based noise normalisation), outside this run's files.
    • Noise normalisation and the scale link. The particles' background spread was 1.035 to 1.098 of the true noise on these sets, because the background carries delocalised signal; the nonlinear models' absolute scale depends on it. The fitted gain shows it.
    • SIMPLE's wavelength is 3.4e-4 short of the exact relativistic value: about 0.1 rad of lens phase at Nyquist for 2 um defocus at 300 kV. It concerns the CTF as a whole.
    • Star interchange (Phase 0): export_relion writes no poses or origins, and the star import and exports read RELION's origins in angstroms as pixels. Not touched by this run.
    • objfun defaults to euclid (SIMPLE's global default), so reconstruct3D_wave stops on a project that has no registered noise spectra unless objfun=cc is given; every run of this work passed cc. The user guide says so; whether the program should default to cc, as a reconstruction from imported poses usually needs, is the maintainer's choice (found in Phase 5, which changes no code).
    • The base's fast-gate failure in unit_pftc_align2D3D (code of base commit 0147279c4).
    • Integration (section 4.7 of the plan): rec_backend=wave in reconstruct3D and refine3D, distributed execution, Ewald correction in the PCG and gridding backends, CTF and pose refinement.
