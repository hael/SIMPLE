    Report: a nano context for model_cavgs_rejection¶

    Run of 2026-10-27/28 on the Mac, for the plan nano_cavgs_rejection.md. Phase 4 (the 3D criterion) was cancelled by the maintainer's ruling once the agreement criterion had decided.

    The answer¶

    • What was delivered is the nano context of model_cavgs_rejection: validity gates, a bank of six lattice features, and normalization against the project's floor of lattice-free classes.
    • No nano preset is promoted. The learned model ranks the classes exactly as one feature, lattice_peak, does. Its learned threshold costs specificity, so on every held-out dataset it falls below the best single-feature baseline.
    • The decision to use by hand is a threshold on lattice_peak of about 2 floor standard deviations above the floor. Pooled over the three labeled datasets, 2.09 floor sd reproduces the maintainer's selection with a balanced accuracy of 0.941. That number is in sample.
    • Added after the run (2026-10-28): the built-in preset nano (quality_model=nano) is a threshold on the raw lattice_peak, not on the floor-normalized one. The run never tried a fixed threshold as a baseline; on the three labeled tables it is as good or better: lattice_peak >= 0.93 gives balanced accuracy 0.984, 0.936 and 0.939 (plateau 0.90 to 0.95; best per project 0.91, 0.94, 0.845), against 0.967, 0.935 and 0.942 for the 2.09-floor-sd gate. The ratio is normalized by its ring mean, so the threshold carries across datasets, and it needs no floor: on 71-17/traj01 after refine2D_nano (chunk classes) the mixture split a continuum (separation 1.2 floor sd), the guard failed, and a floor-relative preset kept every class; the raw threshold rejects 71 of 605, those without a lattice by eye. The maintainer then retired the floor altogether (ruling of 2026-10-28): the nano bank is lattice_peak raw plus median/MAD for the other five, and thres= moves the preset's threshold for a run. The threshold itself was then set by the maintainer at LAT_PEAK_MIN = 0.65, for recall: at 0.93 and at 0.90 the rejected pile of 71-17/traj01 still held classes with faint fringes that he keeps, and for these time series losing useful frames costs more than carrying some junk into 3D. On the labeled sets 0.65 keeps 98 to 99% of the selected classes (TPR 0.984, 0.988, 0.981) and lets 27 to 56% of the rejected ones through (TNR 0.733, 0.444, 0.670); on traj01 it rejects 7 of 605 valid classes beside the 44 empty ones. The learned model is still not promoted.
    • The stored class correlation, the old by-hand gate, is clearly worse: balanced accuracy 0.756 to 0.890 with a threshold learned on the other datasets, 0.860 to 0.937 with the best threshold on each project itself.

    Terms. A class average is the aligned mean of a class's frames. A label is the maintainer's keep/reject state of a class. The floor is the lower component of a two-component Gaussian mixture fitted to the classes' lattice_peak: the classes without a visible lattice. One "floor sd" is that component's standard deviation. AUC is the chance that a randomly chosen kept class scores above a randomly chosen rejected one. Balanced accuracy (BA) is the mean of the fraction of kept classes accepted (TPR) and the fraction of rejected classes rejected (TNR). Classes are counted once each; the two empty classes of each 71-6 set are left out.

    What was learned¶

    1. The cryo-EM features do not transfer as they are. At 0.358 A per pixel in a 43 to 57 A box, every nanoparticle class is hard rejected in the chunk, pool and sieve contexts. The 100-40 A band is empty, and the foreground geometry, the band-pass feature and the relational feature are constant.
    2. The lattice is the evidence. The per-class AUC of the raw features against the labels:
    feature 71-17/traj04 71-6/traj01 71-6/traj04
    lattice_peak 0.990 0.975 0.983
    spot_snr 0.987 0.972 0.974
    spot_count 0.985 0.640 0.873
    band_presence 0.995 0.585 0.841
    spot_arc (lower is better) 0.322 0.255 0.244
    spot_diam 0.214 0.372 0.302
    stored class correlation 0.965 0.907 0.908
    population 0.227 0.437 0.322
    relational feature (nano band) 0.559 0.404 0.522
    1. With the cryo-EM resolution constants divided by 8 or 16, the local-variance features become strong (AUC 0.95 to 0.97). They still add nothing once the lattice family is in: the held-out balanced accuracy changes by less than 0.01 without them, against 0.08 to 0.11 without the lattice family. By the plan's rule, only the lattice family entered the nano bank.
    2. The floor must be guarded, and the guard must fit class averages. On 71-6/traj01 the rejected classes form two groups, so the floor is wide and lies only 3.7 floor sd below the lattice classes. The plan's guard of 4 sd failed there. The fallback normalization put that dataset on another scale, and every lattice model failed on it (balanced accuracy 0.50 at AUC 0.975). The guard is now: the floor owns at least 5% of the classes, lies at least 3 floor sd below the upper component, and that gap is at least 0.2 in log10 ratio. The last condition catches a narrow split of a single population, seen on a synthetic project where every class carries a lattice.
    3. The learned model is lattice_peak with extra steps. Leave one dataset out, the real learner chose probability thresholds of 0.10 to 0.20. Its objective keeps nearly every selected class (recall 0.95 to 0.99) and pays in specificity (0.71 to 0.85). At its best threshold on each project, the model reaches what lattice_peak alone reaches.
    4. The maintainer's selection sits at about 2 floor sd, not at the 4 sd of the per-frame floor rule. The 4-sd rule keeps only 28% to 75% of the selected classes on the 71-6 sets.

    What was built¶

    • quality_context=nano in model_cavgs_rejection (UI choice, per-mode check, commander):
    • Gates: empty class, invalid pixels, both local variances at EPS, no measurable spectrum.
    • Bank: lattice_peak, spot_count, spot_arc, spot_diam, spot_snr, band_presence.
    • The floor and the guard above; the policy lattice.
    • The relational term on a nano band (15 A to 1.5 A, 3 px), because the model schema requires it.
    • analyze without a nano model is the hard-gate-only decision. Its table header records the floor and the median particle diameter.
    • apply with a nano model writes diam on each scored class and its median on the class-average entry of os_out.

    Defects¶

    Fixed in the class-average program:

    1. Project-backed evaluate failed for every relational model. It passed no relational feature to the scorer.
    2. A model file that names a preset without restating its interaction terms was refused, contrary to the policy note.
    3. evaluate accepted training files and a model of different banks. It now refuses them.

    Also fixed, a mistake of this run caught by the new tester before any use: a half-plane mask in the ported lattice code had lost its lower array bound.

    Found outside the file table, not fixed:

    1. simple_strategy2D_srch.f90 (prep4srch, around lines 154-185). On a fresh 2D start the search sets each frame's previous class to a random populated class. The time-chunk assignment written by init_tseries_refs is therefore never read, and strategy2D_tseries moves each frame only among the neighbours of a random class. The classes of analysis2D_nano are not time chunks: 0.3 to 0.5% of consecutive frames share a class and a class spans 0.85 to 0.9 of the trajectory. That holds on the delivered runs, on the previous run's refine2D_nano, and on this build's analysis2D_nano of a hold-out.
    2. simple_nano_density.f90, line 373 (profile), an array bound mismatch (prof 448 against 1001) in test_transfer_kernels. It is the one failure of the Debug fast gate (unit_single). The Release fast gate passes.
    3. The comment on quality_context in simple_parameters.f90 still lists three contexts.

    What was built and run, with results¶

    • Builds:
    • Release, out of tree: gfortran 16, OpenMP, ARCHOPT, OpenBLAS 0.3.27. Its fast gate passes 15 of 15.
    • Debug, bounds-checked: its fast gate passes 14 of 15, the one failure above, before and after the change.
    • unit_numerics passes in both builds.
    • The new sub-suite passes, and passes three times at 11 threads, bit-identical to one thread.
    • check_test_registry.py passes.
    • The cryo-EM byte gate. learn, promote and evaluate run on five synthetic training tables (the repository has no class-average fixtures). analyze runs in the chunk, pool and sieve contexts on a cryo-EM project with a manual selection and on a nano project. All 49 output files are identical before and after the change. The newly working project-backed evaluate has a baseline of its own.
    • The three labeled datasets were rebuilt into projects from the delivered fields (import, make_cavgs, class states). Populations, states, alignments, indstk and eo equal the delivered ones class by class and frame by frame.
    • analysis2D_nano ran on all seven hold-outs. analyze quality_context=nano ran on the three labeled projects. Leave-one-out learn and project-backed evaluate ran on each labeled project. apply ran on copies of the hold-outs, and on a copy of a labeled project with prune=yes. The prune kept the selected frames of the accepted classes, with indstk and order intact.
    • The by-hand gate was run end to end on a copy of a labeled project, as the policy note writes it: analyze, the table filter, two selection calls. It kept 7,244 frames, exactly the frames of the 378 classes at or above 2.09 floor sd.
    • Phase 4 (3D): cancelled by ruling. Two of its five refinements had started and were stopped; none finished. No 3D table exists.

    What is left¶

    • A balanced-accuracy objective for the nano learner, or simply the floor threshold as the product's decision. The learner's false-negative-tolerant objective was built for cryo-EM chunks. On nano it picks thresholds too low to beat the floor.
    • The floor guard on wide floors: two hold-outs with a clear lattice population fail it at 2.6 and 2.9 sd. A floor from a three-component fit, or a guard on the gap alone, would deserve a test on more labeled data.
    • More labels. Three labeled trajectories are the minimum for leave-one-out; the hold-out tables are ready for review.
    • The tseries fresh-start defect in simple_strategy2D_srch.f90, and the bounds error in simple_nano_density.f90.
    • If the class diameter is wanted downstream, autorefine3D_nano can read diam from os_out in place of estimate_diam. That is a change in the nano3D commander, which was outside this run.
