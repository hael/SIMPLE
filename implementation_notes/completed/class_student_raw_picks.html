    The class student on raw picks: no centring, alignment after prediction¶

    Archived in completed/ on 2026-11-17 by maintainer decision. The status and open items below are as last written.

    Status, 2026-11-16: adopted (ruling R4), uncommitted for review. The class student predicts on raw picks and aligns each unseen particle over the shift disk against every class of its candidate list; center_ptcls left the route and the validation; the amplitude channel was rejected (decision 7 (b)). The report is class_student_raw_picks_report.md. Base: 1637eeb1c (the class student learns the physical posterior, P of student_inplane_posterior.md). Supersedes: decision 9 of completed/class_student_2D.md as revised on 2026-11-13 (unseen particles centred by center_ptcls before the student labels them). Depends on: the class student (doc/algorithms/class_student.md), center_ptcls (kept as a program, dropped from the route).

    1. Why¶

    The class student exists to extend a 2D solution to new particles without a 2D analysis of them. Since 2026-11-13 its route first centres every unseen particle with center_ptcls, a 2D analysis of its own: on the maintainer's runs of 2026-11-16 the centring took 6.9 of 7.1 ms per particle on streptavidin, 1.6 of 2.1 on bgal, 1.2 of 1.6 on trpv1, more than the full search it was meant to replace. The premise recorded then ("mirror the pose student, which always worked on centred particles") misdescribed the pose student: it predicts first and registers the shift afterwards against the reprojection at its predicted pose (its validation inputs carried a 2D solution's shifts; its pipeline has no centring step). The maintainer's feedback on all three sets: the student's class averages are "in desperate need of shift refinement"; today's registration is a local gradient refinement of the shift at the predicted class and angle (predict_classes_exec, predict_one), not a search over the picking error.

    The maintainer's design (2026-11-16): no centring; the student predicts on the raw picks; then a single-reference alignment per particle against the training chunk's class average of its predicted class, every in-plane rotation and shifts over the disk. That turns the shift problem from a solve2D run into one small alignment per particle. Searching each class of the student's list instead of the predicted class alone uses the network as a neighbourhood structure (Hans, 2026-11-16: keep this in mind for the pose student's post-prediction refinement, planned/polar_student_future_directions.md). Separately, the amplitude channel, shift-invariant by construction, may let the network rank classes on uncentred particles at all (Phase 3: it did not, decision 7 (b)).

    What was measured on 2026-11-13 (completed/class_student_2D.md, the shift probes) and frames the risk: picking errors of 8 to 10 Å median (16 to 22 Å at the 90th percentile); the network's mode degrades at 5 to 10 px off-centre; wider shift augmentation costs accuracy; power spectra alone learn nothing. Whether the student's candidate list still holds the right class on raw picks, so that a wide alignment over the list recovers it, was never measured: that is Phase 0's first question.

    2. Contract¶

    • Off state: refine2D and refine3D byte-identical at one thread with a fixed seed (simulated_workflow, both suites, test=identity); the pose student byte-identical to the base (model header and weights).
    • center_ptcls stays as a program, unchanged; only the class student's route and its validation stop calling it.
    • The training set stays the teacher's (solve2D's) particles at the teacher's shifts; only unseen particles change.
    • No new command-line key or parameter; no second code path kept for a rejected variant. The amplitude variants live as patches on an export of the base until their verdict.
    • The networks keep their symmetries (rotation equivariance on the grid, the conjugation symmetry; the amplitude path cannot tell r from r + 180°, the complex path does) and training stays bit-identical at a fixed thread count.
    • Costs are reported, measured, and enter verdict 7 (c) only as stated there; small data sets close nothing (Hans, 2026-11-15).

    3. Decisions¶

    1. (2026-11-16, Hans) No pre-centring of unseen particles in the class student's route. They enter at their picked positions: their shifts set to zero, as imported.
    2. (2026-11-16, Hans) Alignment after prediction, against the teacher's class averages (the training chunk's), every in-plane rotation of the polar grid and shifts over the disk of the validation's reference pass (CLASS_DENSE_SHIFT_SIGMA, three sigma), at the student's band. Two variants, both measured (Hans: "measure both"):
    3. S1: the predicted class (the mode) alone;
    4. SK: every class of the student's candidate list (its ncand classes, at most KMAX_CLASS), the best alignment kept, its class committed. Every unseen particle is aligned (shortlisted or not), so the reads cover both routes; the route field still says whether the particle was shortlisted. The alignment reuses the search code the 2D table's fill uses for the disk (the shift map and the gradient shift search of src/main/pftc), unchanged; a new routine there is a stop (section 7).
    5. The amplitude channel for the class student: the design then planned for the pose student (the modulus of the complex input after the CTF multiplication and the shell scaling, its own three blocks of the same shape with a ReLU, its own readout of even angular orders against a per-class amplitude template, scores added before the maximum, or before the log-sum-exp of P's readout). Variants: B (P plus the amplitude path), B' (B with each particle's amplitude channel from another particle, a fixed permutation: the capacity control), C (the amplitude path alone: a wiring check that must reproduce the old negative, held-out classes recovered at most 0.10 on bgal; guessed). (Revised in Phase 3 under ruling R2, a deviation for the maintainer to confirm: C recovered 0.484 of the held-out classes on bgal at seed 1, against the guessed 0.10. The old negative was the pose student's within-6° read, which needs the in-plane angle that amplitudes cannot give; a 2D class is recognisable from its amplitude spectrum. The patch shows exactly the path's contract instead, so the wiring check became that contract, its thresholds set after C's first run: C's predictions are shift-invariant (its reads at the picked positions and at the reference's shifts within one point of each other; measured 0.2, 0.1 and 0.0) and its read-out angle cannot resolve the half turn (of the held-out rows of agreeing class, at least four in five within 10° of the teacher's angle or of its opposite, and at least one in four at the opposite; measured 0.896 and 0.563, B 0.025 at the opposite).) Confirmed by Hans (ruling R4, 2026-11-16): amplitudes alone recognise 2D classes; the check is the design's properties, the shift-invariant reads and the half-turn ambiguity.
