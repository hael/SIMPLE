{% load static timestamps %} {% comment %} _mic_selection_element.html — one micrograph tile. Expects `micrograph` ({dfx, dfy, ctfres, path}), `jobstats` (for xdim_mic/ydim_mic dimensions), and optional `selectable` (bool). When selectable, starts selected; click toggles selection (see toggleMic in _mic_viewer.html) and a checkmark/highlight border is shown. Right-click on the micrograph half or the power-spectrum half opens that half's own contrast/brightness menu; sort/select/scale controls are shared and appear in both menus. When `micrograph.boxes` is present (picking panels), a canvas overlay is drawn on the micrograph half from micrograph.xdim/ydim; see drawMicBoxOverlays in _mic_viewer.html. Optional `hide_pspec` (bool): omits the power-spectrum half entirely and widens the micrograph half to fill the tile. {% endcomment %}
