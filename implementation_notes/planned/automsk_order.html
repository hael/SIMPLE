    automsk=order: the order weight as a reference mask on both backends¶

    Status, 2026-11-21: implemented locally; real-data validation outstanding. Rulings: section 3. Base: f9fd7ca21 (tree clean). Supersedes: the PCG order prior, moved to rejected/ during Phase 4. Depends on: the NU evidence margin and label field (validated: yes, in production since 7e81790da); the density envelope (validated: yes, production); the order weight and core guard (validated: offline only, six pairs, 2026-11-07/08, under the order prior; maps approved by Hans); a benefit of attenuation to alignment (validated: no; Phase 7 is that test).

    Terms¶

    • Half maps, base pair. The even and odd reconstructions from the two halves of the particle set; the base pair is the pair no prior has shaped. It gives the FSC (Fourier shell correlation).
    • Gridding, PCG. The two reconstruction backends. PCG (preconditioned conjugate gradients) solves the reconstruction as a linear system and can solve on a support, a real-space region outside which the solution is held at zero.
    • NU filtering. Nonuniform filtering: a bank of low-pass candidates (20 Å down to 4 Å, capped at FSC=0.143/1.5) competes voxel by voxel on cross-half prediction. The winning label field gives the _nu_filt references (each half filtered label by label) and _nu_locres (the label as a resolution in Å). It runs per state in nonuniform_filter_state (simple_nu_state_filter), which gridding volassemble and the PCG master both call.
    • Evidence margin. Per voxel, the gain of the best finer candidate over the 20 Å candidate in cross-half prediction, smoothed at amsklp (8 Å). About zero on solvent and on a detergent micelle, positive on ordered protein.
    • Density envelope E. automask3D of the pair average: low-pass at envmsklp (20 Å), Otsu, largest component, dilation of at least 7.5 Å, cosine skirt. It keeps every density present at 20 Å, the micelle included. File automask3D_stateNN.mrc. Its core is the binary before dilation.
    • Order weight w_f. A logistic of the evidence margin whose threshold is the (1-f) quantile of the margin over the dense set and whose width is the spread of the margin below the threshold: 1 on ordered density, 0 on solvent and disordered density. f is the ordered fraction of the dense set.
    • Core, density around the core. The core is the dense voxels (inside the envelope's core) whose NU label is at or finer than 1.5 × the pair's FSC=0.5 resolution; the density around the core is the dense voxels outside it, on a membrane protein mostly the micelle.
    • Kept signal K. A region's cross-half signal sum y_e y_o, in which the halves' independent noise cancels, after the mask over before it.
    • Lag one. Made in iteration N-1, used in iteration N.

    1. Why¶

    Hans retired the PCG order prior (pcg_order=yes) on 2026-11-21: in the loop it did not do its job. Its record (pcg_order_prior.md, sections 11-13) says where it failed: the ridge in the PCG operator is nonlocal (the operator removed 2 to 4.5 times the core signal the closed form predicted on the membrane pairs), it acts as a local low-pass where the data precision falls with resolution, it lags one iteration (off at the first iteration and at every new box), and it costs a second cold PCG solve per half, state and iteration. Those are exactly the parts that tied it to PCG. Its order weight and its core guard need no backend, and automsk=nu already has the plumbing a backend-free mode needs: it works on the live competition inside the one state filter both backends call, and applies its mask to the current references. automsk=order keeps that plumbing and changes only the mask. The comparison of the two workflows at the base is in the appendix.

    2. Contract¶

    • The mask. A(r) = E(r) · M(r), M(r) = 1 / (1 + λ (1 - w_f(r))) with λ = 1: a voxel without evidence of order keeps half its amplitude, an ordered voxel all of it. A multiplies the composed _nu_filt pair in nonuniform_filter_state, after the skirt median of E has been removed. Nothing else in the loop sees M.
    • Order is density plus one multiplication. Under automsk=order the NU competition, its noise level, the label field, the handoff, _nu_locres, the background clamp (density), the PCG solve support (density envelope of the lag-one prior-free pair), the FSC (gridding: density, phase-randomized; PCG: on its support) and every published map other than the _nu_filt products are those of automsk=density on the same build.
    • Off-state invariant. With automsk=no|density|nu no line of the order-mask producer runs, and every output is byte-identical to the base at one thread (with yes spelled density).
    • Backends and modes. Gridding and PCG, shared-memory and distributed, run the same code (the state filter) and must behave the same. Both backends define the solvent region the same way (decision 14).
    • No default changes. automsk=order is opt-in everywhere. The only default value that changes is the spelling yes → density where yes is the default today (exec_refine3D_workflow, which serves refine3D_auto and refine3D_local, and estimate_diam).

    3. Decisions and rulings¶

    1. (2026-11-21, Hans) The PCG order prior is retired: the keys pcg_order and pcg_order_lambda, the program order_prior and the code behind them are deleted, with no alias.
    2. (2026-11-21, Hans) automsk=no|density|nu|order. yes is renamed density everywhere, the standalone mask programs included (no|density|tight there); yes is rejected, with no alias. The automsk field (character(len=5) at the base) is widened, since density would be cut to densi.
    3. (2026-11-21, Hans) Depth λ = 1: M runs from 1/2 to 1. Under the Euclidean objective a micelle kept at 1/2 leaves 25 % of the image/reference mismatch that full removal causes.
    4. (2026-11-21, Hans; amended the same day by decision 14) The quantile domain is the dense set: the density envelope's core, restricted to the NU domain, on both backends. The first reading of the ruling was the mskdiam sphere ("as it is done now"). On PCG the sphere outside the solve support holds exact zero/zero voxels, which the NU code treats as boundary conditions and keeps out of every statistic (simple_nu_filter_evidence: "a degenerate cluster at gap ~0 that would otherwise pin the lower quartile"); on gridding the same voxels hold solvent noise. A quantile and a width taken over the sphere would therefore draw the line between ordered and disordered density in different places on the two backends. The dense set lies at least the dilation width (7.5 Å or more) inside the PCG support, so both backends reconstruct every voxel of it.
    5. (2026-11-21, Hans: "a much safer core") The core's resolution limit is 1.5 × the pair's FSC=0.5 resolution (the order prior used FSC=0.143). nonuniform_filter_state therefore takes the pair's FSC=0.5 resolution as a new argument; both callers hold it (res05s(state)) before they call it.

    9. The order prior's guard (0.95, ruled 2026-11-08) is kept; the strength is not scanned (decision 3 fixes it). The scan reads the core voxels once per f and uses no transform.

    14. (2026-11-21, Hans: "We should be consistent in how we define the solvent region between PCG and gridding. That's my only constraint.") Under automsk=order the solvent region is the density envelope (automask3D at envmsklp, binwidth, edge) in every consumer on both backends, and the order mask reads nothing that one backend defines differently from the other:

      • E, the dense set and the core come from the current base pair in nonuniform_filter_state, the same code on both backends;
      • the quantile, the width, K_core and the share removed are taken over the dense set only (decision 4), so PCG's zero/zero voxels never enter them;
      • the evidence margin enters only through quantile ranks and the spread below the threshold, so the global noise level by which each backend normalises it (nu_objective_noise_scale, over observed voxels) cancels;
      • the NU-evidence envelope, whose null differs by backend (the dilation ring on a constrained PCG pair, the solvent majority of the sphere on gridding), is written as a diagnostic and used by nothing under order.

      One difference stays, inherited from the standing envfsc contract and not changed here: PCG must hold its support before it solves, so its FSC (and the FSC=0.5 resolution of decision 5) is taken on the lag-one density envelope inside the estimator, while gridding applies the current density envelope post hoc with the phase-randomized correction. Both use the same envelope construction. Phase 0 measures how far apart the two backends' envelopes and FSC=0.5 resolutions are on the same particles and orientations, and Phase 2 reports the same for the order masks (section 6). 15.     7. Common rules and stop conditions¶

    • No new key beyond those ruled (automsk=order, the density spelling, automsk on nu_filt3D and on solve3D's UI). No floor, tolerance or assertion relaxed unless this plan says so. Fix as you go, with a file-table row added before the edit. New OpenMP code is tested at 11 to 24 threads, repeated. The THROW_HARD/THROW_WARN arguments are never //&-continued (an errmsg variable instead). No short-circuit on allocatable arrays in one if. No goto.
    • Policies change in the phase that changes the behaviour they describe: automasking_policy.md (the living description of the mode) in Phases 1 and 2, nonuniform_filtering_policy.md in Phases 2 and 4, reconstruct3D_pcg_policy.md in Phase 4, solve3D_policy.md in Phases 4 and 5.

    Stop when:

    • an off-state identity case differs and the difference is not a defect of the base path (a base defect is fixed in the phase and recorded);
    • the order-against-density comparison differs in an undeclared file;
    • a floor of section 6 would have to move;
    • a file outside the table needs an edit that changes behaviour;
    • the code contradicts a fixed decision (for example, a caller of nonuniform_filter_state that has no FSC=0.5 resolution, or a path on which the envelope's core is not available);
    • an available validation exposes a regression against a non-data gate. Missing real-data inputs are recorded as outstanding and do not block implementation (user ruling, 2026-11-21).

    Gates no ruling waives: the off-state identity cases at one thread; the sub-suites order mask and nonuniform filtering; scripts/run_fast_gate.sh; check_test_registry.py; check_cli_chain.py.

    Appendix: the two workflows at the base¶

    automsk=nu pcg_order=yes
    backends gridding and PCG PCG only
    statistic evidence margin at amsklp the same margin, plus the label field
    threshold null median + nu_msk_sig (3) MADs, over the solvent majority of the sphere or the density envelope's dilation ring (1-f) quantile, f chosen by the core guard
    shape binary: MRF, components, 1 Å growth, 6 Å edge soft logistic, no morphology
    acts on the _nu_filt references (multiply) and the background clamp the operator (a ridge λ h (1-w) x²), then a second cold solve; the references through the ML replay of that pair
    micelle zeroed attenuated by up to 1/(1+λ) where the operator is flat, less where it is not
    timing current evidence for the current references; lag one only for the gridding FSC mask and the matcher fallback lag one throughout; off at the first iteration and at a new box
    validity null size, solvent majority core size, core signal, a qualifying setting
    prediction none: the mask is what is applied the operator lost 2-4.5 × the predicted core signal on membrane pairs
    cost one evidence pass and morphology a second cold solve per half, band transforms
    final map NU artifact as mask, NU filt_mode prior-free

    Both start from the same statistic. A fixed MAD multiple does not transfer between noise levels (in the numpy check of the cross-validated threshold note the right multiple ran from about 7 to about 30); the core guard adapts because the core is defined from the pair's own FSC. The order prior's strength cannot be chosen from the half maps either: cross-half prediction prefers no removal at every noise level (same note, section 4), which is why λ is ruled rather than scanned.
