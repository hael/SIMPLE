    In-plane quadrature insertion in 3D: report¶

    Written 2026-11-14 by the autonomous run "inpl3D" of the plan on the dell, base 85213734e, which differs from the plan's base 06d3a4de9 in the plan file alone. Nothing is committed: the change is the working tree against that base. Each phase's diff is in /home/elmlundho/agent_runs/inpl3D/review/phase_N.diff (N = 0 to 4). The plan's Progress section holds the detail of every phase. Run artefacts are under /data2/agent_runs/inpl3D/scratch/keep/ (keep/ below).

    What it delivers: a temporary key, inpl_posterior=yes|no (default no), on refine3D and solve3D. Under the key, every pose a particle is inserted at in a probabilistic refine3D pass (the committed pose and the weighted insertion's extra poses) enters the reconstruction at a 64-point quadrature of its in-plane posterior. That posterior is the distribution of the particle's in-plane angle given the pose's direction, taken from the losses at every grid rotation at the pose's solved shift. The poses are reweighted by their in-plane marginals, keeping the shift prior. It works with gridding and with PCG (the preconditioned conjugate-gradient reconstruction), in shared memory and over parts. With the key off the run is the base's byte for byte. Whether the maps improve is the maintainer's test (section 6).

    1. What was learned¶

    1. The off state holds. After every phase that touched code, the simulated workflows matched the base: 6vxx 186 files the same and 1jxy 223, none differing, by test=identity at one thread with SIMPLE_SEED=7. The key-off PCG solve3D smoke matched too: 158 files the same, none differing. Two base runs needed no exclusions.
    2. A partial reconstruction differs between 1 and 16 threads even at the base, and the cause is upstream of the change. In the base probe (one refine3D refine=prob iteration on the fixture):
    3. repeats at each thread count are byte-identical, and the committed poses are identical across thread counts;
    4. between 1 and 16 threads, 14 to 42 of millions of voxels differ, by at most 2.5e-8 of the volume's largest value;
    5. the noise spectra (sigma2) that each run bootstraps from the particle images differ in 89 bytes between thread counts, while the gridding insertion adds into each lattice cell in a fixed order whatever the team;
    6. started from an iteration whose committed sigma2 the next one reuses, the partial reconstructions are byte-identical across thread counts, at the base and with the key on.
    7. On the 87-particle 6vxx fixture the key spreads the early stages and collapses the late ones. In stage 3's first iteration there are 1.74 rows and 6.68 entries per particle, 1.4 % of rows collapsed to one angle, mean n_eff 2.9 (n_eff is exp of the entropy: the effective number of angles a row spreads over). In the last stage 92 % of rows collapse, at 1.08 entries per particle. Over a whole solve3D, poses inserted grew 1.6-fold, and gridding accumulation time grew from 0.97 to 1.34 s. Peak memory was unchanged within 50 MB: the reservation is 792 bytes per particle at topk=3. The fixture is too small to say anything about map quality.
    8. The marginal reweighting barely moved the committed direction's share on the fixture. The mean committed view weight (0.72 in stage 3) equals the table's committed weight to two decimals. The shift between direction and extras that the decision anticipates, toward poses whose angle is uncertain, did not show here.
    9. The login environment points SIMPLE_PATH at the maintainer's build. The local queue's job scripts start $SIMPLE_PATH/bin/simple_private_exec, so any run that does not reset the variable runs another build's workers. The run caught this on its first workflow launch and runs every job through keep/env.sh <tree>.
    10. solve3D's and refine3D's children copy the parent's command line and switch it to objfun=cc: start-volume reconstructions, solve3D's reconstructions, and the external-reference pose initialisation. A key refused under every non-Euclidean objective would stop every key-on run at such a child, so the refusal applies only where the key acts.
    11. The command-line chain check reads a yes/no key's choices from the first parenthesised a|b group of its declaration comment. A comment naming state|geom before (yes|no) fails the fast gate.
    12. Readout sums kept per thread depend on the dynamic schedule (which particles each thread gets), so a part's double-precision sums could differ between repeats. Per-particle slots summed in particle order fix that.

    3. Decisions taken under delegation¶

    Each is recorded in the plan: in the decision's text where one was revised, and in the phase's Progress entry.

    • The objective refusal narrowed (the objective decision, revised in Phase 1). inpl_posterior=yes is refused under objfun other than euclid only on a refine3D pass in one of the key's modes: refine=prob, or prob_neigh with prob_neigh_mode=state|geom. Elsewhere the key is ignored. Reason: finding 6. solve3D sets objfun=euclid itself.
    • The readout counted per particle (the readout decision, revised in Phase 2). Each particle's search fills its own slot, and the matcher sums the slots in particle order. Reason: finding 8, from the Phase 2 review.
    • The prior centre computed by the tables. The shift search keeps its prior centre private, and src/main/pftc/ was out of scope, so the tables apply the search's double-precision rule themselves, at the refinement's start. In Phase 3 this became one routine, seeded_window_refine, shared by both tables.
    • Per-pose work arrays in the search's per-thread state (s3D). The strategy object is created per particle, so holding them there would allocate per particle. This added Phase 2 to simple_strategy3D_alloc.f90's file-table row, under the maintainer's launch ruling on file-table rows.
    • The PCG bench line in Phase 1 (the PCG strategy's row extended to Phase 1 under the same ruling).
    • The multi-state final assignment sets inpl_posterior=no only when the key was given, so a key-off command line stays the base's.
    • The key in solve3D's own descriptor, not in the inputs solve3D shares with solve3D_chunks, which does not forward it.
    • The thread gate judged on a probe with identical input spectra (see deviations).
    • The test placements and the Phase 3 refactors (see deviations).

    4. Deviations from the plan¶

    • The thread gate. The probe frozen in Phase 0 (a fresh fixture, sigma2 bootstrapped per run) gave, at key on, a spread one ulp-level step above the base's in two of four partial reconstructions:
    • the even sums: 2.77e-8 against 2.47e-8 of the largest value;
    • the even densities: 2.40e-10 against 2.13e-10.

    With identical input spectra (finding 2), the base's partial reconstructions are byte-identical across thread counts, and the key-on ones are too. The plan says that when the base is byte-identical the key-on partials must be, so the gate was judged on that stricter probe. This is a revision of a criterion the run set itself after seeing a result. If the maintainer keeps the Phase 0 criterion, Phase 2 should count as stopped on it. Evidence: keep/probe/thread_gate_p2on.txt, keep/probe/thread_gate_fixed_sigma.txt. - Phase 3 is not tests-only. Four refactors let the checks run the production code: - the tables' seeded window refinement as one routine; - the particle counts as a helper; - insert_inpl_entries made public, on the point group; - the PCG chunk loop as a public routine with the chunk capacity as an argument.

    The key-off identity was re-run after them: 186, 223 and 158 files the same, none differing. The key-on fixed-input probe's partials are unchanged byte for byte. insert_inpl_entries and accumulate_pcg_inpl_batch are public for their tests, as pcg_extra_entries and accumulate_pcg_extras already were. - Test placement. - The key-on gridding check is in simple_rec3D_strategy_tester.f90 (the same unit_reconstruction entry), not in simple_reconstructor_tester.f90, which stays unchanged: a volume submodule importing the matcher's module would invert the layering. - The prior-centre check is in the in-plane tester, which has the shift-search fixture it needs. - File-table rows extended (no file outside the table changed): - the PCG strategy to Phase 1; - simple_strategy3D_alloc.f90 to Phase 2; - the table utilities, the two tables, simple_strategy3D_prob.f90, simple_matcher_3Drec.f90, the PCG strategy, simple_strategy3D_srch.f90 and simple_convergence.f90 to Phase 3. The last two carried reverted plants only and end unchanged from the end of Phase 2 (simple_strategy3D_srch.f90 is unchanged from the base). - Rows with nothing to do: simple_refine3D_strategy.f90 (the merge is generic) and simple_strategy3D_srch.f90 (no access needed) are unchanged. - Expected effects that did not occur on the fixture. - The reweighting did not move the committed view weight (finding 4). - The converged-map probe inserts almost no extras, because the cone at 7.6 A is tight. - Neither says anything about real sets. - A false start in Phase 3, recorded. The first batch of plants ran a stale binary, because the driver did not install. It reported nothing caught; it was rerun, and none of its results is used.

    6. Left open for the maintainer¶

    • Decide: whether the thread gate may be judged on the probe with identical input spectra (section 4).
    • Reported, not gated: the smoke numbers above and the PROB_TAB_INPL lines (keep/p2_readout/).
    • Data-dependent:
    • whether the maps improve;
    • how stage 3 behaves on real sets (the history of the weighted insertion says stage 3 is the risk);
    • how many entries a symmetric set's on-axis views keep (streptavidin, D2);
    • whether m_max reaches 64 at the late stages of a real set (the readout prints it beside the budget).
    • Follow-up runs, the plan's protocol, paired by seed, ten restarts per arm:
    • one start per dataset: simple_exec prg=solve3D_cavgs projfile=<set>.simple mskdiam=<d> pgrp=<pg> nparts=4 nthr=6
    • the control arm, seeds 1 to 10: SIMPLE_SEED=<s> simple_exec prg=solve3D projfile=<start>/1_solve3D_cavgs/<set>.simple mskdiam=<d> pgrp=<pg> nsample=2500 cavg_ini_ext=yes nparts=4 nthr=6
    • the key arm: the same command with inpl_posterior=yes;
    • a PCG pair of either arm adds rec_backend=pcg, which acts from stage 6;
    • the sets: PorF (~/Processing/PorF), PfCRT, streptavidin (D2: read the REC3D bench's poses inserted), and one small C1 membrane protein (SLC or Tat), where the top views' in-plane angle is the question.
    • The readout, in this order:
    • the stage-3 maps and the iteration stage 3 exits at;
    • the final FSC=0.143;
    • PROB_TAB_INPL per iteration: rows and entries per particle, the share of rows collapsed, mean n_eff, and the committed view weight beside PROB_TAB_INSERT's table weight;
    • BENCH/REC3D_BENCH_PART*.txt: accumulation time and poses inserted;
    • peak memory beside the reservation.

    The plan suggests a reading rule to adopt or replace: the key arm is not worse if it matches the control's count of successful restarts at the control's final resolution range, and its stage-3 exits are no later in more than two of ten paired seeds. - After that read: the key becomes a fixed part of the route, or is removed (as topk_inpl went in 2D). - Separately: multi-state is covered by the unit assertions and the off-state identity only; its smoke and scale test is a separate step.

    7. Proposed skill lines (to apply only with approval)¶

    • simple-refine3d, under the weighted insertion: "inpl_posterior=yes (temporary) inserts every pose a particle enters the reconstruction at (committed and extras) at a 64-point quadrature of its in-plane row at the solved shift, the poses weighed by their in-plane marginals with the shift prior; key-off paths are the base's code byte for byte; readout PROB_TAB_INPL, REC3D bench 'poses inserted'."
    • simple-main-strategies-probabilistic, under Rules: "Under inpl_posterior=yes, prob_insert_poses holds a worst-case reservation (reserve_inpl: max(1,topk) blocks of 64 packed entries per particle, the memory estimate's term). The strategy fills a particle's blocks, or empties all of them so the particle falls back to the insertion without the key. Gridding (insert_inpl_entries) and PCG (accumulate_pcg_inpl_batch, chunks of MAXIMGBATCHSZ) read them in the same entry order. Readout counts are kept per particle and summed in particle order."
    • simple-modern-fortran or the test policy, a general lesson: "A plant or test driver that rebuilds must also install: simple_test_exec runs from the install tree's bin/."

refine3D pass, the committed pose and the weighted insertion's extras, enters at a 64-point quadrature of its in-plane

PCG; worst-case reservation in the memory estimate; readout PROB_TAB_INPL; the weighted insertion's margin on the
exits. Key off is the previous run byte for byte (simulated workflows and the PCG smoke). Assertions with planted
